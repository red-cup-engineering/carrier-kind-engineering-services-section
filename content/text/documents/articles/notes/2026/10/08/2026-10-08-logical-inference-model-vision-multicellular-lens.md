# Blastema: a tile-assembling embryo of proof (self-assembling predication model)

*A model that does not run a forward pass but GROWS: a zygote context carrying one genome (the Duquenne–Guigues basis compiled to SignChain rules) attaches typed cells at its growth front wherever glue strength (independent witnesses in the cell's past light cone) clears a temperature threshold (Winfree aTAM), differentiates each cell into a formal concept (its settled row), lays positional information by sheaf heat flow from pinned organizer cells, kills cells whose contested seams exceed a refusal number (T2/apoptosis), and regenerates any amputated body from a content-addressed checkpoint because the settled body is a least fixed point; the thing it grows is a proof term, and a sentence is one bead row of that body.*

This is an unjudged round-one vision. It is kept as source material, not as an adopted design.

## Image

THE MEDIUM. A two-dimensional (later n-dimensional) field of discrete sites, each site a SignCell triple address (subject, predicate, object) from signcell/src/cell.mjs. A site is empty (knowledge-bottom, the void), or occupied by a cell told one way (bulk phase t or f), or contested (both sides told, FOUR top). That is the foam reading from the house 2026-07-26 note (void / two bulks / interface), and it is literally what signcraft/src/settle.mjs partition() and cones() compute today. Nothing in the medium is a real number except the morphogen field (below); everything else is exact: canonical CBOR bytes, ni: hashes, BigInt rationals.

WHAT LIVES IN IT. A biological "cell" is one object row g: its signed attribute profile over the alphabet M ⊔ ¬M, exactly the Map "g m" -> FOUR reading that signcraft/src/inference/nuclei.mjs settle() operates on. Every cell carries the same genome: lawfulBasis(ctx) from metrologic/src/closure/formal-context.mjs (the Duquenne–Guigues basis, filtered to realized premises so a contradiction cannot explode), compiled one-for-one into SignChain rule() objects (signchain/src/rules.mjs) whose antecedent is "these attribute cells read at least potential" and whose consequent is the conclusion attribute. Holofoam already does this compilation at compileModel() (apps/holofoam/src/simulation.mjs line 33-40: genome -> rule() list -> settleCells), but for a 4-bit genome and a 16-row table; here the genome is the mined basis and the table is unbounded.

HOW IT MOVES. Four kinds of event, each an admitted SignCraft history observation (signcraft/src/history.mjs observeHistory), so the whole organism is a fold over its log and can be replayed, checkpointed and merged:
1. ATTACH (growth). The growth front is the set of enabled moves of every frontier row: generate.mjs enabledMoves() already returns, per candidate attribute, its prerequisites (the implication premise) and its witness count (bearers in extent). Read the premise as the tile's glue sides and the witness count plus premise knowledge heights as glue strength. A candidate attaches iff strength >= tau. tau = 1 is non-cooperative: any single witness suffices, growth is crystalline and (by substitute_preserves_path / refutePeriod) periodic. tau = 2 is cooperative: two independent sides must both be present, which is Winfree's aTAM temperature-2 regime, the one that is Turing-universal. The Gibbs temperature already in generate() (exp(-dF/T)) is the kinetic TAM relaxation of that hard threshold; the two are kept distinct.
2. DIVIDE (cleavage). A round of division is a block substitution: metrologic/src/models/dynamics/substitution/tiling.mjs inflateGrid() replaces every cell by its block of daughters; tilingPatch(sub, seed, level) is the embryo after level cleavages. The daughters inherit the genome unchanged and inherit the parent's row as their seed valuation (knowledge is joined, never retracted), then differ only by which attachments the local field permits. Growth rate is the Perron root of abelianize(rule) (substitution/index.mjs), so a Fibonacci genome grows at phi and a hat-metatile genome at phi^4 (HatReflectionFrequency.lean).
3. DIFFERENTIATE. A row's fate is its settled intent: delta() transfers along realized profiles (Galois double prime), sigma() closes under the basis. The fixed point of sigma∘delta is a closed intent, i.e. a formal concept. A tissue is a concept sublattice (the principal filter of an intent); germ layers are the three or four coarsest concepts under the zygote's intent. Differentiation is driven only by a cell's past light cone: delta() refuses to transfer when extent(held) is empty, so a cell can only become what some witnessed neighbour has already been. Positional information is laid by signgraph/src/cellular-sheaf.mjs diffuse(): rows are vertices, shared attributes or specialization moves (signgraph/src/site.mjs generators) are edges with restriction maps, and organizer rows are PINNED (the `fixed` option, documented in the file as "a pinned vertex value is admitted evidence (a boundary condition)"). The harmonic section is the morphogen gradient; the `crossings` list (edges whose residual crosses tolerance) is the French-flag threshold set.
4. APOPTOSE (T2). A row whose contested cells give a refusal number sqrt(E/lambda_1) (inference/complex.mjs via nuclei.obstructionsOf) above a cutoff is removed and its sites return to void. This is pymetrologic foam.py try_t2_removal and the game-foam note's "T2 = daimon", and it is the only non-monotone move; it is admitted as a history event, never as a retraction of evidence.

PATTERN MEMORY (Levin). The target morphology is NOT in the genome. It is in (a) the pinned organizer values and (b) the content-addressed checkpoint (history.mjs checkpointHistory: "cut at the causal frontier"). Same genome, different pins -> different body, because settle computes the least fixed point above the seed, and the seed is the pinned prepattern. Regeneration after amputation is replay from the checkpoint plus re-settlement; lfp uniqueness is the regeneration theorem. Rewriting the body without touching the genome = changing a functional-predicate observation on an organizer cell (history.mjs sectionOf: later causal observation supersedes).

FOAM PHYSICS, honestly. What is mechanism: the four-phase field, interface = contested cell, T2 removal, the cost functional continuationEnergy dF = alpha*opened - beta*closed + T*dE where dE is a graph-Laplacian disagreement term (generate.mjs lines 133-152), the H-theorem ledger (nuclei.settle throws on a firing with zero flux), and the seam check (cycle rank |E|-|V|+|C|, CoordinationCohomology.lean h1_vanishes_iff_forest). What is metaphor: Plateau angles, surface tension as a physical constant, and Turing's diffusive instability. sigma and delta are idempotent nuclei; they have no instability. Spots and stripes here come from the lattice of concepts (which intents are closed), not from two species diffusing at different rates. I keep the word "morphogen" for diffuse() because it is a real diffusion with a real boundary condition; I do not claim a Turing pattern.

## Cycle

PERCEIVE. Utterances and observations arrive as PAM messages (signgraph/src/pam.mjs reifyMessage {sender, claims with polarity}) or RelConLang lines (signcraft/src/inference/relconlang.mjs parseLine -> cellsFromRows). Each becomes a SignCell cell via membrane.mjs cellOfUtterance / tellingOf (negation parity decides for/against). Stance particles produce stance REPORTS, not witnesses (the report-do-not-inject law); only an evidential policy promotes a report to witnessFor/witnessAgainst. The new cells are observed into the organism's history (history.mjs observeHistory, parents = current heads) and joined into the context: makeContext over trace.mjs contextFromTrace. Each arrival is a point on the future tip of the organism's cone; its time coordinate is for+against (LightCone.lean time).

INFER (differentiate). For every row touched by the arrival, run delta then sigma (nuclei.mjs settle) until valuationEq. Each firing emits a witness {cell, by, law, lane}; the ledger flux must equal the knowledge-height deficit (H-theorem check throws otherwise). The row's fixed point is its concept (differentiation state). Rows whose settled profile is contested are interfaces; obstructionsOf computes refusal numbers over the conductance complex. Nothing outside the row's past cone was consulted: delta transfers only along realized extents, sigma only along basis implications whose premises the row holds. The organism's current body = the settled valuation; settlementAdmits (settle.mjs) is its proof-checker by replay.

GENERATE (grow). Lay the morphogen field: build cellularSheaf with vertices = rows, edges = shared attributes with restriction maps = projection onto the shared sub-alphabet, pin organizer rows (`fixed`), run diffuse; crossings mark threshold edges. For each frontier row compute enabledMoves; score each by glue strength str(g,m); attach every move with str >= tau (aTAM), or sample among those with Gibbs weights exp(-dF/T) (kTAM) where dF = continuationEnergy, logging excludedMass as the wit record. Cleave when a row's intent is a substitution symbol: inflateGrid the row into its block of daughters, each seeded with the parent's valuation. Emit the visible phenotype as forgetDecoration of the (attribute, stage) pair; the hidden stage decoration is what keeps the body from completing periodically (fib_deep_sofic pattern). Every attachment is a history observation. Before closing any loop of rows into a cycle (a bracelet, an argument returning to its premise) run the seam check: cycle rank |E|-|V|+|C| over the cover nerve must be zero or the closure is refused with the responsible cycle.

LEARN (two exact mechanisms, no gradients). (a) Re-induce the genome: dgBasis/lawfulBasis over the enlarged context (formal-context.mjs); a new pseudo-intent is a surprise, an implication that dies is a counterexample. (b) Fit the glue: for relation-shaped data, residualLeft/residualRight (inference/algebra.mjs) give the greatest W with A ⊗ W <= B per evidence lane; trainTwoLayer alternates the adjoints to a fixed point. (c) Adapt conductances D by flux (adapt.mjs) so dE in the energy reflects which attributes co-fire. Learning is monotone and witnessed: the basis is content-addressed (semanticId) and every change is a history revision.

REPRODUCE. Three kinds. (1) Cleavage (above): daughters share genome and seed. (2) Checkpoint/regeneration: checkpointHistory cuts the body at its causal frontier; any node restores it (restoreHistory verifies every address) and re-settles to the same lfp. (3) Horizontal transfer: two organisms merge by mergeHistories (order-independent) and glueSignGraphs (signcell/src/semantic-graph.mjs, reports conflicting-relation obstructions); contested cells after merge are the lenticle census; H1 of the merged cover is the number of independent unresolved disputes. Apoptosis closes the cycle: rows whose refusal number exceeds the cutoff are removed as T2 events, returning sites to void, which re-opens frontier for attachment. The organism's "wits" (excluded mass + ledger flux + lenticle census per round) are the residue that, per RegimeCycle residue_dichotomy, guarantees the record never repeats while staying comparable to its seed: alive, not crystal.

## Language surface

TOKENS ARE BEADS; A SENTENCE IS A ROW OF TILES. A word is a tile whose W glue is what must precede it and whose E glue is what it offers to what follows. In Lambek terms (LambekCalculus.lean) the W side is the right residual c/b and the E side is the left residual a\c; a word attaches to its neighbour exactly when the glue matches, and matching IS modus ponens (modus_ponens_left: a·(a\c) <= c). That is why a sentence is a proof: a complete row with all interior glues matched and a single S on the outer edges is a Lambek derivation, and the derivation's witness trace is the sentence's proof term. It is why a sentence is a tiling row: the words are Wang tiles with coloured E/W edges, grammaticality is edge-matching, and the undecidability/aperiodicity results of the domino problem are the formal reason natural language cannot be finite-state. It is why a sentence is a weave: the left hand reads each attachment for lawfulness (sofic.mjs path on the grammar graph; SFT admissibility, truth order) and the right hand reads it against the accumulated discourse record (history prefix order, knowledge order); where the two disagree the bead is contested and held, not rejected (lenticle census). Dialogue particles are polarity: ku/du (question, request) are negative moves that receptivity forces to be answered, se/ha/nes/nul are positive closures with at most one per history, so a dialogue is an innocent strategy and Theorem 6.8 of the game-foam note (receptive closure R(sigma) exists and is minimal) says every well-formed conversation can be completed to one that answers every open relevance.

READING. relconlang.mjs parseLine gives EAVT rows and (s,P,o, reading, aspect) cells; membrane.mjs turns each into a told cell. Stance (ta/na/sha/mu) is a Chu row (chu-matrix-semantics-audit-v0.1.json) and is emitted as a stance report; reading a sentence never fabricates witnesses. Comprehension = settling the organism with the new cells: the sentence's meaning is the set of rows whose intent changed, and its unsaturated holes (speak.mjs comprehend) are typed questions = enabled moves with unmet-conclusion prerequisites.

WRITING. The growth front of the organism is the set of enabled moves; speaking is attaching word-tiles along a row until the outer glue is S. generate() draws each attachment with priced Gibbs sampling and logs what it did not say (excludedMass); the hidden decoration state (clause role / stitch state: straight, turn, increase, join) travels with each tile and is forgotten on emission (fib_deep_sofic), so the row stays locally lawful while the hidden hierarchy forbids periodic completion. Each produced sentence is appended to history as a revision whose parents are the discourse heads, so re-reading one's own sentence is replay, and two speakers' rows merge order-independently. What the surface honestly is today: a 42-line conlang corpus, a hand-written particle parser, and an SVO slot-filler (speak.mjs); the residuated-tile parser and the decorated-stage emitter are the two modules to build (see minimalKernel code/weave.mjs and sys/speak.mjs).

## Why a new class

WHAT IT CAN DO THAT A TRANSFORMER STRUCTURALLY CANNOT.
1. Every output is a proof term with a content address. The witness DAG ({rule, from} in rules.mjs; {cell, by, law, lane} in nuclei.mjs) IS the derivation; settlementAdmits replays and checks it; semanticId addresses it. A transformer's output has no proof object, only a probability.
2. Knowledge is monotone and merge is a CRDT join, proved (crdt_merge_order_independent, calm_monotone_coordination_free). Many organisms grow in parallel and glue with no coordinator; disagreement appears as contested cells and nonzero H1, quantified by refusalNumber, never averaged away. Transformers average; they cannot represent "both told" as a stable first-class state.
3. Locality is enforced by the past cone, so a cell's fate is a function of what reached it; horizons are computable (HoleRegion). Attention has no causal order and no horizon.
4. Learning is exact: residuation gives the greatest consistent weight in one pass; the DG basis is the minimum implication cover; both are witnessed and content-addressed. There is no stochastic descent, no epoch, no unexplained weight.
5. Generation is constraint-respecting by construction (aTAM attachment, sofic admissibility, seam check) and priced (excluded mass logged per draw), and the hidden-decoration mechanism gives long-range non-periodic structure with zero entropy rate, which an n-gram cannot and a transformer only approximates.
6. Pattern memory is separable from genome: same rules, different pinned prepattern, provably different body; regeneration is replay to the same lfp. No neural model has this decomposition.
7. Deterministic, replayable, exact arithmetic (BigInt rationals, canonical CBOR): bit-identical across machines, unlike float attention.

HONEST LIMITS (what it cannot do and transformers can).
1. No distributed representation, no similarity metric, no embeddings; unseen attributes read bottom. Generalisation is only through the concept lattice and the realized-continuation cone, which cannot leave the hull of witnessed extents.
2. dgBasis is exponential in |M| (guard 2^min(|A|,20)); settle is O(steps * |G|^2 * |M|); diffuse rebuilds L per call with O(V) lookups; relations are string-keyed Sets. Nothing here has run above ~20 attributes or ~500 vertices.
3. tau = 2 attachment is Turing-universal, so the regime of a cooperative genome is undecidable in general: the organism can fail to settle, and the closure-regime ladder (strict / scott / transfinite / aperiodic) is then a certificate requirement, not a guarantee.
4. No perception, no fluency, no world knowledge; the language surface is a controlled conlang. Nothing has beaten a frequency baseline (signcraft evaluate fixture ties it 5/5 seeds, with a metric that short-circuits).
5. The morphogen field is numeric R^d, the logic is FOUR; the two are joined only by thresholds (crossings), not by one algebra.
6. "Foam" is a reading of a four-valued field plus an energy; no Plateau law, no surface tension constant, no Turing instability has been derived or measured.

## Minimal kernel

A JS package `blastema` depending only on SignStuff + metrologic + computer-data-engineering four.mjs. Twelve modules, four layers, dependency order data -> code -> networking -> systems. Every function pure; every state change is a history observation.

DATA (signcell + metrologic carriers; no I/O)
- data/genome.mjs: genomeOf(ctx) = lawfulBasis(ctx) compiled to rules.mjs rule() list; genomeId = semanticId(sorted implications). Also implicationOfRule / ruleOfImplication (bijection).
- data/tile.mjs: tile(row g, intent, glue {N,E,S,W}: signed attribute subsets, decoration stage) as a canonicalCarrier over cell.mjs cells; matches(leftTile, rightTile) = definite agreement on shared attributes (bottom-or-consistent), returns FOUR; wordTile(word, lambekType) with W = right residual, E = left residual.
- data/cone.mjs: witnessOrder(witnesses) builds the derivation DAG from {rule, from} / {cell, by, law}; pastCone(address), futureCone(address), spacelike(a,b); time(cell) = for+against, space = for-against (LightCone.lean coordinates on record()).

CODE (SignChain layer: composition of typed claims)
- code/attach.mjs: glueStrength(ctx, v, move) = Sum kHeight over move.question.prerequisites + move.question.witnesses; attachTau(ctx, basis, v, frontier, tau) = knowledge-join of all moves with strength >= tau (aTAM step, inflationary); attachKinetic(..., T, rng) wraps generate.mjs generate() (kTAM); both return {v, witnesses, wit}.
- code/divide.mjs: cleave(organism, substitution) = tiling.mjs inflateGrid over the row grid, daughters seeded with parent row; growthRate = perronConvergent(abelianize(substitution)).
- code/apoptose.mjs: t2(ctx, v, cx, cutoff) removes rows whose refusalNumber (complex.mjs via nuclei.obstructionsOf) > cutoff; returns removed rows and the T2 event list.

## Risks

- Scale. dgBasis is exponential in the attribute alphabet (2^min(|A|,20) guard, doubled by the denial lane); nuclei.settle is O(steps * |G|^2 * |M|) with Map copies per tell; cellular-sheaf rebuilds L per call with O(V) findIndex lookups and dense BigInt rank. The embryo will not grow past a few hundred rows without interning, bitsets, CSR Laplacians and incremental (AddIntent-style) basis maintenance.
- Undecidability. tau=2 attachment is Turing-universal, so a cooperative genome can fail to settle; the closure-regime ladder then demands certificates the system cannot produce by iteration. Budget every round (settle maxSteps, closureOrbit's one-stage-per-rule bound) and treat non-settlement as a first-class outcome.
- Mechanism/metaphor drift. Turing instability, Plateau angles and surface tension are NOT mechanisms here; morphogen = linear sheaf diffusion with pins, pattern = concept lattice structure. The maps show the house repeatedly promoting names over computation (brim constant top, literal-true receipts, 'potentialAperiodicArticulationCell'); Blastema must ship each term with the function that computes it or not use it.
- Numeric/logical seam. diffuse() is R^d; the logic is FOUR. Thresholding crossings into intents is a design choice, not a theorem; E5 may fail and the morphogen layer would then be decorative.
- Thin evidence. The only existing language result ties a frequency baseline with a biased metric on a 42-line corpus; E7 may be a null result. The inference class has never beaten a baseline anywhere in the repo.
- Three code islands. Foam physics is Python (pymetrologic.foam), settlement is JS (SignStuff), proofs are Lean; the aTAM, Wang and horizon pieces exist in none of them. The kernel is honest new code, not a wiring job, and the SignCell layer it builds on has three incompatible 'cell' notions, two cell-set implementations and uncommitted modifications in cell.mjs/predication.mjs/utterance.mjs.
- closureOrbit fires each rule at most once (settle.mjs `fired` set), so if a premise gains evidence later the rule never re-fires; the lfp claim rests on 'one derivation per ground rule'. Blastema's attach must use nuclei.settle semantics (re-run to valuationEq) or fix closureOrbit before relying on it.
- Heuristic causal order. settle.mjs causalOrder uses stage + shared term, over-approximating the true derivation order; horizon and cone experiments are meaningless until the witness DAG replaces it (E6 first).
- Misattributed theory. The two-hands note cites Autopoiesis.history_separation_aperiodicity, which does not exist (the statement is open); 'mutuallyLocallyDerivable' is matrix-invariant equality, not MLD; 'aperiodic' regime is three Boolean flags. Build on fib_deep_sofic, residue_dichotomy, h1_vanishes_iff_forest and crdt_merge_order_independent, which are proved, not on the labels.
- Apoptosis breaks monotonicity. T2 removal is the one non-monotone move; if it is implemented as evidence retraction rather than as a history event over a fresh site, the CRDT and lfp theorems stop applying and E2/E4 fail.
- Grandiosity feedback. A model that narrates itself in embryology and foam physics will be tempting to over-describe; every receipt must be a computed number (ledger, census, H1, refutePeriod, ordering fraction) and never a literal true.
