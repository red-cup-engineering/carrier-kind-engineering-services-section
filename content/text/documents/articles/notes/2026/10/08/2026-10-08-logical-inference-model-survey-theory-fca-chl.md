# Logical inference model survey: theory-fca-chl

## Summary

The house "FCA–Lambek circle" is a four-edge loop stated in Obsmetrologia/Calculus/Logic/FCALambekCircle.lean: (1) the FCA derivation closure closeA = primeB∘primeA on a formal context (G,M,I) is an extensive/monotone/idempotent closure operator, i.e. literally the same `Order.Closure` shape that `Settling.Reading.Closure` iterates, so "computing concepts is running settlement"; (2) the concepts form a complete lattice (proved, Boolean case only); (3) every integral commutative residuated lattice is a commutative Lambek algebra with lres = rres = imp (proved, `lambekOfResiduated`); (4) the closing edge, Wurm 2012 (syntactic concept lattices are complete semantics for the Lambek calculus) and Pentus 1993 (Lambek grammars = CFLs), is prose only, recorded in code as `modelTheoreticClosingEdge: "documented-boundary"`. The ONE picture the corpus intends: a Fregean predication cell (s,P,o) is a Chu point evaluated in FOUR = Bool×Bool (FregeanFourChu.lean); the attribute rows of a context are a residuated/Lambek algebra; a concept (Galois-closed row) is a type; Lambek residuals a\c, c/b are the two directions of "what must combine with this to land in that"; and a sentence is a proof because combination-by-residuation is proof-by-modus-ponens (`modus_ponens_left`: a·(a\c) ≤ c). Curry–Howard–Lambek is claimed as the "categorical-logic shadow of the predication hyperdoctrine" (∃_f ⊣ f* ⊣ ∀_f are the Σ/Π type formers), and the CHL quartet (type theory / logic / category theory / domain theory) is conjectured to be the nuclear quartet (H_t, Fix σ_t, Fix Δ_t, H_t*) of two commuting nuclei on a Heyting fiber.

What is genuinely ours, as opposed to Ganter–Wille/Belohlavek/Lambek/GJKO textbook: (a) the five-restriction thesis that FCA is a degenerate predicated knowledge system (value domain 2⊗2→2; predicate category→{has-attribute}; closure rules→Galois pair only; scopes→one; witnesses→none), in the 2026-05-02 note; (b) the executable two-nucleus kernel in signcraft/src/inference/nuclei.mjs, which is FCA run over a sign-dichotomized alphabet M ⊔ ¬M with FOUR-valued rows: σ = Duquenne–Guigues implication closure of each object's signed row (the DG basis treated as the machine's compiled knowledge), Δ = Galois double-prime transfer along realized profiles, both knowledge-order, with ex-falso guarded by `lawfulBasis` / `extent(...)>0` so contradictions stay local (paraconsistent FCA), every firing reified as a witness, and a free-energy ledger (deficit = Σ knowledge-height gained, flux = commits + mints + 2·jumps) checked each step; (c) the residuated relational network in signcraft/src/inference/algebra.mjs + network.mjs: layers Y = X⊗W over (FOUR, joinK, meetK) per evidence lane, exact backward pass by left/right residuation (greatest W with A⊗W ≤ B), training = meet of residuals / alternating residuation to a fixed point, no gradients; (d) the Lambek-residual-defect-is-curvature theorem `residuals_coincide_iff_commutative` plus discrete Bianchi identity in ObsmetrologiaPhysicalScience/CausalSets/Curvature/Lambek.lean, which is the only place Lambek bi-residuation is tied to causal (light-cone) order.

What is claimed but not computed: there is no Lambek grammar anywhere (no categorial types on words, no sequent/proof-net search); the "semiotic language model" (speak.mjs) is Gibbs sampling over per-predicate FOUR fibers with magic weights, fed by a hand-written particle parser (relconlang.mjs); the Lean `curry_howard` inhabits every type once one base fact holds (so the propositional reading has no refutation), the CCC laws are meta-level `rfl`, and `chl_quartet_is_nuclear` is a bijection between two 4-element enums; σΔ = Δσ for the real kernel is only measured on 60 random contexts, not proved; many-valued (Belohlavek) FCA is prose only; the quartet conjecture's three requirements are explicitly open and `FiberNuclearLanguage` exists in no code.

- **Computes:** relconlang.mjs: tokenize; parseLine with fixed particle tables (reading particles ta/na/sha/mu → FOUR points, force ku/du/se/ha, role/info markers ga/ma/pa/fi/ko/ri, obliques lo/ke, 12 verb roots, 4 aspect suffixes, 14 noun roots, pronouns, ccomp via ve/do ve) → EAVT rows → cellsFromRows → (s,P,o,reading,aspect,event). speak.mjs: witnessCells builds per-predicate subjFiber/objFiber (Map arg → FOUR, always told-for); holeConcepts builds an FCA context (objects = arguments, attributes = 'V subj'/'V obj'/'feature:tag') and concept-mates via intent/extent; speak: Gibbs over predicates weighted log(1+count), then over fiber entries weighted ±2 by stance (backoff mates at 0.5 when <3 saturators), emits '<s> ga <P> do <o>.' with a receipt; comprehend: parse or return typed 'unsaturated' questions; evaluateSaturation(AcrossSeeds): held-out object prediction on 5-candidate pools vs frequency baseline.

The FCA use is real but shallow (concept-mates = objects sharing any attribute via single-attribute extents, i.e. a 1-step neighborhood, not concept closure). Weights 2/−2/0.5 and T=0.7 are unmotivated.

## Gaps and slop

- No Lambek grammar exists anywhere: no categorial type assignment to words, no sequent calculus or proof-net search, no derivation 'sentence : S'. relconlang.mjs is a hand-written finite-state recognizer over fixed particle tables. The 'sentence is a proof' half of the picture is unrealized; Wurm 2012 and Pentus 1993 are cited in prose in FCALambekCircle.lean, LambekCalculus.lean, fca-lambek-circle.md and lambek-calculus.md and nowhere computed.
- Lean Curry–Howard is degenerate: `curry_howard` inhabits EVERY type once `Reading.pos (v φ) = true`, so the propositional reading cannot refute anything (no ⊥, no negation, no uninhabited type); HasTy is never linked to propOfTy by soundness; ccc_* laws are `rfl` on Lean's Prop functions, not on the term model; Lambek's STLC≅CCC theorem is absent. `chl_quartet_is_nuclear` is a bijection between two 4-element enums with an arbitrary corner assignment, named as if it were a theorem.
- σΔ = Δσ (the 'commutative nuclear square') is not proved for the real kernel; signcraft/test/inference/commutation.test.mjs measures it on 60 random contexts and asserts only (σΔ)² = σΔ. nuclei.mjs's σ/Δ are knowledge-order closures and are explicitly NOT the truth-order σ/Δ of FiberAlgebra.lean, and Δ (per-row Galois closure) is not the hyperverse Δ_t (intersection of one-step-extension images); the names are overloaded three ways.
- Many-valued (Belohlavek) FCA is prose only (FormalConceptAnalysis.lean header, belohlavek-bridge.md). No residuated derivation operator ⋀_g (A g → I g m) exists in Lean or JS even though four.mjs has residuumReading; the only non-Boolean move is the M ⊔ ¬M dichotomization.
- Four separate FCA implementations: metrologic/src/closure/formal-context.mjs (pairs + denials), metrologic/src/closure/formal-concepts.mjs ({id, attrs}, own intersection closure, prunes singletons/top/bottom while claiming 'every closed concept', greedy fallback that is not FCA), agent-organization-engine evaluating/connecting/formal-concept.mjs (SEN terms, O(n²) folds), metrologic analysis/fca-lambek-circle.mjs (now delegates after a 2026-09-11 note admitting it 'used to hand-roll them again'); plus vendored copies under sites/crucible.play.561.group/dist/vendor/.
- Receipt tautology: residuated-lattice.mjs, lambek-calculus.mjs, fca-lambek-circle.mjs return objects whose every boolean is `true` by construction (otherwise they throw or return an obstruction), e.g. `fcaClosureIsSettlingClosure: true`; the receipts carry no information beyond their `kind`.
- Stale and self-contradictory FOUR docs: relating/docs/four-charge-algebra.md lists `active` twice ('negative evidence only' and 'both'); relating/docs/four-corner-cell.md's table says Lean (false,false) = potential / kBot and (true,true) = absurd / kTop and lists `potential` twice in the JS column, but Obsmetrologia/Articulation/Articulating/Cell.lean ofStatus has absurd=(f,f), potential=(t,f), active=(f,t), stable=(t,t), matching four.mjs. signgraph/src/chu-term.mjs comment writes potential as ⟨0,1⟩ while four.mjs indexes it as (for=1, against=0).
- Relating docs cite theorem names that do not exist in the Lean: RelMath.FCA.galois_connection, RelMath.FCA.closeA_closure, RelMath.FCA.primeA_primeB_primeA (formal-concept.mjs header); RelMath.Order.FCALambekCircle etc. The namespace is Obsmetrologia and the real names are prime_adjunction, derivation_galois_connection, closeA_idem, primeA_closeA; the file lives under Calculus/Logic not Order.
- Hyperdoctrine.lean proves ∃_f ⊣ f* ⊣ ∀_f only for bijective f (converse), where the three coincide; the CHL header's 'hyperdoctrine IS CHL at dependent-type level' rests on that partial object. Projection quantifiers exist only in finite JS (signgraph fiber.mjs, predicating-quantifying).
- The CHL quartet conjecture is duplicated verbatim in three files (research-curry-howard-lambek-quartet.md, relational-hyperverse-repository/math/curry-howard-lambek-quartet.md, -tetrad.md) with all three requirements marked open; `FiberNuclearLanguage` is not defined in any code; nuclear-quartet.md is a bare 'renamed' stub.

## Attachment points

- Make 'a concept is a type' literal in the kernel: define Ty := closed signed attribute sets (Fix Δ, i.e. concept intents of ctx with lanes M ⊔ ¬M), propositions := basis-closed rows (Fix σ), terms/proofs := the witness traces settle() already emits (each witness {cell, by, law, lane} is a proof step), and typing judgment Γ ⊢ row : intent := impClosure/closeAttrs reaches the intent. This realizes the CHL-quartet conjecture's first three columns on the finite kernel (all rows, Fix σ, Fix Δ, settled rows = Fix σ ∩ Fix Δ) and gives a concrete object to test σΔ = Δσ against instead of random sampling.
- Build the missing Lambek grammar on top of LambekCalculus.lean / lambek-calculus.mjs: assign residuated types to RelConLang particles (e.g. ga : NP\(S/NP)-style, stance particles as modal prefixes on S), replace relconlang.mjs's hand parser with sequent proof search in a finite Lambek algebra whose carrier is the concept lattice of the corpus context (Wurm's syntactic concept lattice), so that parsing a sentence is finding a proof and the proof is the (s,P,o) Chu point. Pentus gives the CFL ceiling; Wurm gives completeness of the concept-lattice model.
- Implement Belohlavek residuated FCA over FOUR using four.mjs residuumReading/fusionReading: primeA A m := meetK over g of (A g → I g m) and dually, replacing the M ⊔ ¬M dichotomization with genuinely FOUR-valued incidence; then the concept lattice carries the twist-product residuated structure and edge (4) of the circle becomes computable rather than documented.
- Light cones as residuals: in the kernel's closure order, read lres a c (what must follow a to reach c) as the future cone and rres c b as the past cone of a settlement step; use CausalSets/Curvature/Lambek.lean's curvatureDefect to measure non-commutativity of the σ/Δ transport per object (where σΔ ≠ Δσ in commutation.test.mjs is exactly a nonzero defect); the enabled cone of generate.mjs is then the future light cone of the object's row.
- Foam reading of nuclei.mjs: each object row is a cell, σ is the intra-cell membrane (basis closure), Δ is the inter-cell membrane (Galois transfer across objects sharing a profile), contested cells (both lanes told) are foam interfaces, the refusal number √(E/λ₁) is surface tension, and the free-energy ledger is the Plateau/Lyapunov functional; adapt.mjs's conductance update is the Physarum-style wall thinning. Make the kernel multicellular by letting Δ transfer between contexts (federation = the missing scope relaxation).
- Tiles and beads: Settlement.lean names Tiles/Beads/Hole/Cech1 as instances of the same Settlement structure settle() iterates; treat basis-closed rows as allowed local patches and the concept lattice as the sofic shift of admissible rows, so a settled valuation is a tiling and the DG basis is its local rule set; the weaving-beads 'two hands' are σ (past-facing, what the row already implies) and Δ (future-facing, what every realized continuation shares).
- Unify the four FCA implementations into one metrologic closure/formal-context carrier (pairs + denials) with the SEN-term version regenerated from it, and have the typed-theory.mjs generators (converse, projections, specialization) act as reindexing on the kernel's context so predicate structure (restriction 2) finally enters the closure; then signgraph fiber.mjs's ∃/∀ become the kernel's cross-predicate transfer.
- Replace receipt tautologies with executable conformance against Lean-emitted vectors: for LambekAlgebra, ResiduatedLattice and FCA closure, emit finite models from Lean (Two, TwoLambek, small contexts) and have the JS receipts check equality against emitted tables, as the FOUR/CHL/measurement fixtures reportedly already do.
