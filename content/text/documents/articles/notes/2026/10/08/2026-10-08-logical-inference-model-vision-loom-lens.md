# LOOM — the causal bead weaver (a predication-algebra language model whose tokens are Wang beads, whose rows are light-cone layers, and whose every bead is a proof term)

*A language model that does not predict the next token but weaves the next row: each bead is a content-addressed SignCell with a FOUR charge, two witness sets (the two hands), four edge colours and a hidden supertile decoration; a row is admitted only if it is horizontally an admissible word of a decorated sofic shift (Path step, fib_deep_sofic) and vertically knowledge-above everything in its past light cone (kle_iff_lightcone over the SignChain derivation DAG); the fabric stays aperiodic because the row plan is inflated by a primitive substitution, stays lawful because the matching rules are a Duquenne-Guigues basis learned from the fabric itself, and every contradiction between the two hands is held as a contested (⊤) bead rather than rejected.*

This is an unjudged round-one vision. It is kept as source material, not as an adopted design.

## Image

THE MEDIUM. A fabric of beads on a loom. Warp threads run down the fabric and are the entities (subject and object tokens, SignCell `Token`s): they persist from row to row. The weft is one row of beads laid across the warp at a time: a row is one causal stage, the layer of cells told at that stage of a settlement (`closureOrbit` stage k in signcraft/src/settle.mjs), or one token layer of an utterance. A bead sits where a weft predicate crosses two warp threads: it is a predication cell (s,p,o) with a charge in FOUR. Its four edges are coloured: north/south edges face the previous and next rows (time), east/west edges face its row-neighbours (space). The bead also carries a hidden decoration d ∈ D, the supertile role, which the viewer never sees (forgetDecoration) but which the weaver's hands feel: it is the stitch state (straight, turn, increase, join).

WHAT LIVES IN IT. Bubbles of same-charge beads: a connected region all told-for (or all told-against) under a chosen witness set D is one bulk phase; a bead told both ways (⊤) is a film between two bulks. Films are where the fabric is alive: they carry the only observable (nothing in a bulk can be distinguished by any counter-design, which is Girard incarnation and is the exact reason surface laws can exist at all). Two weavers (two looms, two agents, two speakers) share a fabric edge: a column of beads both can see. Each weaves its own rows, spacelike to the other's rows except through that shared column. Dialogue is the two weavers each reading the shared column with their own two hands; where their definite readings differ the knowledge join is ⊤ (definite_joinK_inconsistency_iff_disagreement, Obsmetrologia/AperiodicOrder/Tiling/LenticleIdentification.lean) and the ⊤-count on the shared column is the seam's H⁰_⊤ census — the quantity of disagreement, held, not averaged.

HOW IT MOVES. Time is the row index and nothing in a bead can decrease in the knowledge order from row to row: time = pos+neg, space = pos−neg, and a bead may only move into its own future cone (LightCone.lean: kle_iff_lightcone). The weaver chooses each new bead from the enabled cone of the warp threads it sits on (`enabledMoves` in signcraft/src/inference/generate.mjs: unmet conclusions of the basis plus realized continuations) intersected with the beads whose west edge matches its left neighbour's east edge (`path(graph, word)`, metrologic analysis/sofic.mjs) and whose decoration follows from the decoration of the bead above it under the substitution (`substMap`, SoficShift.lean). It scores candidates by continuation energy ΔF = α·opened − β·closed + T·ΔE and draws Gibbs-style, logging the excluded mass as a wit (`generate`). Between rows the fabric relaxes: the sheaf Laplacian diffusion over the bead grid (`diffuse` in signgraph/src/cellular-sheaf.mjs, pinned at the boundary rows the environment dictated) pushes residual disagreement along films until the harmonic section is reached; the per-edge residual trace is the list of films that are still under tension.

WHAT MAKES IT ORGANIC RATHER THAN CRYSTALLINE. A fabric whose rows repeat is a crystal and a crystal is dead (every completion forced, zero bits per bead); a fabric with no constraint is soup. The loom lives in the sofic middle: the substitution inflating the row plan guarantees zero-entropy long-range order (rows never repeat; the pattern is creative), while the finite-type cover of that substitution (`substitutionCoverSFT`/`sftEntropy`, metrologic models/dynamics/substitution/sofic.mjs) permits log₂(Perron) bits of local choice per bead (the pattern is improvised). The weaver's temperature T is set so realized bits per bead sit strictly between the substitution floor (0) and the cover ceiling. T1 events (re-threading a warp pair so two beads swap neighbours) and T2 events (a bead region shrinking to nothing and being removed) are the only topological changes, exactly as in two-dimensional dry foam; the loom records both as cells in its history. Reproduction is a checkpoint cut of the fabric (a Cauchy surface of heads) handed to a child loom with the genome (substitution rule, SFT cover, implication basis) and one mutation; selection is by held-out ΔF, seam H¹ and entropy band.

HONEST SEPARATION. Mechanism today: cells, FOUR, witness sets, content addressing, rule firing with derivation witnesses, least-fixed-point settlement, DG-basis induction, Path admissibility, the Fibonacci decorated cover, Gibbs draw with priced exclusion, sheaf diffusion with pinned boundary, history DAG with CRDT merge, lenticle disagreement count. Mechanism-shaped but not built: the two-dimensional row transducer (Wang matching of a whole row against the previous row), the decoration schedule for substitutions other than Fibonacci, the witness-DAG causal order (settle.mjs uses a stage+shared-term heuristic instead), the bead-level CHL typing judgment. Metaphor until a cost functional is measured: bubbles, films, surface tension, von Neumann n−6, T1 avalanches (pymetrologic's settling foam variant already failed the T1 statistics), Deborah numbers. Foam is the guiding image; the fabric is the mathematics.

## Mathematics

OBJECTS.
1. Bead. B = (φ, r, W⁺, W⁻, d, c) where φ = (s,p,o) is a PredicationCell (Obsmetrologia/Language/Predicating/Cell.lean; JS `cell` in signcell/src/cell.mjs), r ∈ FOUR the reading, W⁺/W⁻ the for/against witness sets (the two hands' records), d ∈ D a finite decoration, c = (cN,cE,cS,cW) edge colours from a finite palette K. Address of a bead = semanticId(φ) (`address`, cell.mjs): evidence and decoration are off-address so identity is stable while the bead learns.
2. Row. A finite word of beads w = B₁…Bₙ laid at stage k. Horizontal law: Path step (w.map colourE/W) — the Lean `Path` (SoficShift.lean l.41) and JS `path(graph, word)` (metrologic analysis/sofic.mjs l.168) — i.e. cE(Bᵢ) and cW(Bᵢ₊₁) are an allowed pair. Decorated law: Path decoStep on (letter, d) pairs with forgetDecoration = Prod.fst (SoficShift.lean l.62).
3. Fabric. A finite causal set of beads F = (beads, ≺) where B ≺ B' iff address(B) ∈ witness.from of B' transitively (the SignChain `derivation(r) = {rule, from:[addresses]}`, signchain/src/rules.mjs l.46). Rows are antichains. Vertical law: r(B') ≥ₖ ⨆ₖ{consequent readings licensed by the past cone} — in code, antecedent thresholds `knowledgeLeqReading(atLeast, knowledgeReading(cell))` (`antecedentHolds`, rules.mjs).
4. Light cone of FOUR. time(r) = pos+neg, space(r) = pos−neg; KLE r r' ⟺ |space r' − space r| ≤ time r' − time r (Obsmetrologia/Language/Positing/LightCone.lean, `kle_iff_lightcone` l.91, axiom-free). Negation = parity, conflation = time reversal, K4 gauge. So the vertical law is literally: a bead's charge may move only into its own future cone.
5. Two hands. Left hand L(B) = lawfulness: Path-admissibility of its row plus DG-basis licence (`impClosure(basis, held)`, metrologic/src/closure/formal-context.mjs l.88) — gives a definite reading t or f. Right hand R(B) = history: KLE-consistency with its past cone plus Galois realization (`closeAttrs`/`extent`, formal-context.mjs l.70/59) — gives a definite reading t or f. Charge r(B) = joinK(L(B), R(B)). Theorem (proved): isInconsistency(joinK c₁ c₂) ⟺ ReadingDisagreement c₁ c₂ for definite c₁,c₂ (`definite_joinK_inconsistency_iff_disagreement`, LenticleIdentification.lean l.219). So ⊤ beads are exactly where the hands disagree; the ⊤-count on a seam is pairwiseLenticleCensus = H⁰_⊤.
6. Matching rules = implication basis. The fabric is a formal context (G = warp threads/rows, M = (predicate,object) claims with a denial lane ¬M, I = told-for incidence; `makeContext`, formal-context.mjs l.31). Its Duquenne-Guigues basis (`dgBasis` l.109, Ganter Next Closure; `lawfulBasis` l.131 drops empty-extent premises so a contested seed cannot explode) is the tile set: each implication A→B is a local matching rule, compiled into a SignChain `rule(name, [{cell, atLeast}], {cell, reading})`. Concept intents (Fix Δ) are the types of beads; concept lattice = legal local patches.
7. Proof term (CHL). term(B) = the derivation tree rooted at B's witness {rule, from} recursively through addresses. Typing judgment Γ ⊢ term(B) : intent ⟺ closeAttrs(ctx, held(Γ)) ∋ the attribute B realizes. Horizontal composition of beads in a row is the Lambek product (dot); the east-edge colour of Bᵢ is the left residual a\c of what must follow; the west-edge colour the right residual c/b (LambekCalculus.lean `dot_lres_adj`, `dot_rres_adj`). Where lres ≠ rres (word order matters) the fabric is curved: `residuals_coincide_iff_commutative` (ObsmetrologiaPhysicalScience/CausalSets/Curvature/Lambek.lean). Normalization = settling to the least fixed point (`closureOrbit`, settle.mjs l.35; Lean `Tstep_infl`, Closure/RuleSystem.lean). Confluence is proved for STLC (`church_rosser`, CurryHowardLambek.lean l.717) and must be re-proved for bead terms.
8. Aperiodicity. Row plans are inflated by a primitive substitution σ (`substitute`, SoficShift.lean l.117; metrologic substitution/index.mjs `substitute`, `abelianize`, `primitiveMatrix`). The visible row is the forgetDecoration-image of a decoStep-admissible decorated word; proved for Fibonacci: `fib_deep_sofic` (SoficShift.lean l.282), with the engine `substMap_preserves_path` (l.218) which is general. Entropy budget: topological entropy of the cover log₂(Perron) (`sftEntropy`), floor 0 for the substitution subshift.
9. Seams and closure. Closing a strip into a bracelet or a dialogue returning to its premise requires the cover nerve of overlaps to be a forest: H¹ = 0 ⟺ forest (`CoverNerve.h1_vanishes_iff_forest`, Cohomology/CoordinationCohomology.lean); cycle rank |E|−|V|+|C| is the obstruction (signgraph/src/cohomology.mjs `cohomologyOfCells`).
10. Fabric relaxation. Cellular sheaf on the bead grid with vertex stalks R^|K| (one-hot edge colours or FOUR embedded as (pos,neg) ∈ {0,1}²), restriction maps = colour projections; energy ½‖δx‖², gradient flow `diffuse` with pinned boundary rows, exact H⁰/H¹ over Q (`cellularSheafCohomology`, signgraph/src/cellular-sheaf.mjs). Free-energy ledger on the discrete side: flux = commits + mints + 2·jumps, deficit antitone (`stepLedger`/`settle` in signcraft/src/inference/nuclei.mjs; Lean FreeEnergyLyapunov.lean `deficit_antitone`, `converges_in_bounded_steps`).
11. Merge. Two fabrics merge as histories: `mergeHistories` (signcraft/src/history.mjs l.392), set union of content-addressed revisions, Kahn-ordered; Lean `crdt_merge_order_independent` (Cohomology/CRDTCohomologicalCALM.lean). Weavers coordinate only through the shared column; everything else is coordination-free by the theorem.

## Cycle

PERCEIVE (boundary row). Incoming utterances arrive as PAM messages ({sender, claims[{s,p,o,polarity}]}, `reifyMessage`, signgraph/src/pam.mjs) or as raw utterances read through the membrane (`cellOfUtterance`/`tellMembrane`, signcraft/src/membrane.mjs: negation parity decides for/against). Each becomes a bead on the boundary row with charge = presence of its witness sides and no decoration yet. RelConLang stance particles colour the bead's edges through the Chu matrix (ta=(potential,absurd), na=(absurd,potential), sha=(stable,active), mu=(absurd,absurd)) but, by report-do-not-inject, add no witness: grammar colours, policy witnesses. Boundary beads are pinned (the `pinned` argument of `diffuse`) for the rest of the cycle. Each perceive is an `observeHistory` revision whose parents are the current heads: the perceived row is a Cauchy surface.

INFER (settle the fabric). Compile the current basis to SignChain rules: for each implication A→B in `lawfulBasis(ctx)` and each warp thread g, `rule(name, A.map(a => ({cell: (g,a,g), atLeast: potential})), {cell: (g,b,g), reading: potential-or-active-by-lane})`; add `transportRules(site)` for specialization. Run `closureOrbit(rules, seed)` to the least fixed point; every derived bead carries `derivation = {rule, from:[addresses]}`. Build the fabric's causal order from those witness addresses (NOT from `causalOrder`'s stage+shared-term heuristic): B ≺ B' iff address(B) ∈ transitive closure of B'.witness.from. Past cone of a bead = its premises; future cone = its consequences (`causalCone` API kept, input replaced). Then relax: build the cellular sheaf of the bead grid (vertex stalk = one-hot colour vector ⊕ (pos,neg)), restriction = colour projection, `diffuse` with the boundary row pinned; the residual list names the films still under tension; `cellularSheafCohomology` gives exact H⁰ (number of globally consistent completions) and H¹ (obstructions). Each ⊤ bead is kept; `stepLedger` records commits/mints/jumps and throws if flux appears without firings (H-theorem check already in `settle`, nuclei.mjs).

GENERATE (weave the next row). For the current row w = B₁…Bₙ with decorations d₁…dₙ: (a) inflate the plan: the next row's decorated skeleton is `substMap(decoSub)` of the current decorated word (SoficShift.lean l.201; JS `decoratedFibSubstitute`), so the number and roles of beads in the new row are fixed by the substitution — zero-entropy long-range order; (b) for each position, candidate beads = `enabledMoves(ctx, basis, v, g)` for the warp thread g at that position (unmet conclusions and realized continuations) filtered by (i) horizontal admissibility: `path(graph, [...prefixColours, cW(candidate)])` must be a `sofic.path:witness`, (ii) decorated admissibility: `decoStep(d_left, d_candidate)`, (iii) vertical law: `knowledgeLeqReading(r(above), r(candidate))` — the bead may only climb; (c) score by `continuationEnergy` ΔF = α·opened − β·closed + T·ΔE where ΔE is read from the sheaf residual (replace the conductance matrix D by the sheaf's edge weights) and T is tuned so bits/bead lie in (0, sftEntropy); (d) draw with `generate` (injected rng) and keep the `wit` record (chosen, excludedMass, excluded); (e) the two hands read the new bead: L = Path+basis licence, R = cone+Galois realization; charge = joinK(L,R); disagreement is a ⊤ bead, logged, never backtracked. The new row is one `observeHistory` revision; the bead's proof term is its derivation DAG plus the wit.

LEARN (re-induce the tile set). The woven fabric is a formal context (rows or threads × signed claims). Re-run `dgBasis`/`lawfulBasis` (or an incremental Next-Closure step: one implication added/removed) — the basis IS the model's compiled knowledge (formal-context.mjs header). Learn the row→row relation exactly by residuation: W = residualLeft(X_rows, Y_nextrows) over the two FOUR lanes (`residualLeft`/`trainTwoLayer`, algebra.mjs/network.mjs), monotone meet refinement, no gradients. Adapt edge weights from the firing trace (`adapt`, adapt.mjs) — call it conductance adaptation, not Physarum, until a flow is solved. Score with held-out row completion (common candidate set, ≥5 seeds, no transductive leakage).

REPRODUCE (cut and fork). `checkpointHistory(history)` cuts the fabric at its heads (the domain of dependence of that Cauchy surface); a child loom receives the cut plus a genome (σ, decoStep cover, basis) with one mutation (toggle one forbidden pair of the cover, add/remove one implication by a Next-Closure step, or change the substitution's metallic mean n). Two children weaving the same shared column are compared by `forkCompare`-style diffs: held-out ΔF, ⊤-count on the seam, seam H¹ (can the strip close), realized entropy band, `refutePeriod` on every row. Fabrics of siblings merge by `mergeHistories` (order-independent); surviving contested seams are the lineage's load-bearing grain boundaries. Nothing selects globally: selection is cohort-local Pareto on those readouts, and 'unknown is not zero'.

## Language surface

READING. A sentence is a row. Tokens are beads laid left to right; each bead's west edge must match its left neighbour's east edge (`path`), which is the Lambek condition that the token's category composes: the east colour of bead i is the left residual a\c (what must come next to reach the clause type), the west colour of bead i+1 is the right residual c/b. A row that closes to the clause type S is an admissible word; the proof is the chain of residual discharges, each a bead's derivation witness. RelConLang (signcraft/src/inference/relconlang.mjs) is the first warp: its particles already form a closed finite palette (ga/ma/pa/fi/ko/ri roles, lo/ke obliques, ta/na/sha/mu stance, ku/du/se/ha dialogue, pa+sava/luma/davu evidentials) and its parser's hand-written finite-state recogniser is a one-row Path checker in disguise. The improvement is to replace the hardcoded tables by categorial types in a finite LambekAlgebra whose carrier is the concept lattice of the corpus context (Wurm's syntactic concept lattice) so parsing = proof search = finding an admissible decorated word; the derivation found is the (s,P,o) Chu point (FregeanFourChu.lean). Stance particles colour the bead's charge through the Chu matrix; by report-do-not-inject (relconlang questions), `sha P` emits P plus a stance report and no witness, `nes … ve P` emits a DenialReport that only an evidential policy may promote to `witnessAgainst(P)`.

WRITING. Generation is weaving the next row under the previous rows' light cones: the row plan (how many beads, which supertile roles) comes from the substitution, the beads from the enabled cone filtered by horizontal matching and vertical KLE, the choice from the Gibbs draw with priced exclusion. Hence a generated sentence is never a free sample: it is one legal configuration of the fabric (the chart is one legal configuration; the system is the set of all legal configurations), and its proof — why each bead was lawful, what it cited, what it excluded — ships with it. A sentence is a proof because combination-by-residuation is proof-by-modus-ponens (`modus_ponens_left`: a·(a\c) ≤ c, LambekCalculus.lean); a sentence is a weave because its beads satisfy local matching with both the row and the rows above; a sentence is a tiling row because a Path-admissible decorated word is exactly a row of a Wang-style configuration whose edge colours agree, and the decoration (supertile role) is the hidden state that makes the whole fabric non-periodic while every local window is a legal patch.

DIALOGUE. Two weavers share a fabric edge: the column of beads naming the shared nucleus. Questions and requests (ku, du) are Opponent moves that open relevance; answers, acknowledgements, denials and relevance refusals (se, ha, nes+ccomp, nul+ccomp) are Player moves that close it (dialogue-transition-laws-v0.1.json). This is the polarity game-foam §10 asked whether a corpus carries; RelConLang carries it by construction, so Theorem 6.8's receptive closure R(σ) (every open Opponent move must be answerable, at most one Player answer per history) is computable on a dialogue. Each weaver's rows are spacelike to the other's except through the shared column; each reads the other's beads on that column with its own two hands; disagreement is a ⊤ bead on the seam and the seam's ⊤-count is the dialogue's held disagreement. Misunderstanding = non-gluing; repair = a later row that lowers the seam's H¹; agreement reached = a bracelet that closes (forest nerve). Discourse as a whole is the causal set of beads; a speaker's horizon (what they cannot yet know) is the HoleRegion relative to their readout column (Hole.lean), and 'understanding' is settlement to a confluent normal form (church_rosser is the determinism guarantee once lifted to bead terms).

WHAT A TOKEN IS. Not a one-hot index: a content-addressed bead whose address is its triple, whose charge is FOUR, whose colours are concept intents, whose decoration is its supertile role, whose witnesses are its proof. Vocabulary is open: a new bead type is a new concept intent (a new closed set under the learned basis), minted by Next Closure, not by a fixed embedding table.

## Why a new class

WHAT THE LOOM DOES THAT A TRANSFORMER STRUCTURALLY CANNOT.
1. Every output token carries a proof term and a certificate. A bead's derivation DAG ({rule, from:[addresses]}, rules.mjs) is checkable step-locally, and the whole fabric is replay-certified (`settlementAdmits`). A transformer's token has no witness, only a logit; there is no object to verify.
2. Contradiction is a value, not a failure. ⊤ beads are legitimate stable states (Belnap FOUR), localized to the seam where the two hands disagreed; `topMass(recordOf(cell))` quantifies it. A transformer averages contradictory evidence into one distribution and cannot tell you where the conflict is.
3. Causal order is explicit and auditable. Past cone = premises, future cone = consequences, computed from witness addresses; horizons are computable (HoleRegion). Attention has no causal order beyond the triangular mask and no notion of what a token could not have known.
4. Aperiodicity is guaranteed by construction, not hoped for by sampling. The substitution-inflated plan has zero entropy rate and no period (`refutePeriod` is a cheap check); the cover's entropy bounds improvisation. Transformers degenerate into repetition loops at low temperature and soup at high temperature with no structural middle.
5. Coordination-free multi-agent weaving. Two looms merge fabrics by `mergeHistories`; `crdt_merge_order_independent` is proved; the only shared state is the seam column and disagreement there is held as ⊤. Multi-agent transformer systems have no merge semantics at all.
6. Learning without gradients, with an exact backward pass: `residualLeft(A,B)` is the greatest W with A⊗W ≤ B (a theorem); the implication basis is the canonical minimal cover of the fabric's regularities (Duquenne-Guigues). Every weight is a reading; every rule is quotable.
7. Open vocabulary by concept formation (Next Closure mints a type), and identity by content address so two looms deduplicate beads without a shared tokenizer.
8. Energy accounting that is a theorem: deficit is antitone, converges in ≤ Φ₀ firings (FreeEnergyLyapunov.lean), flux ledger closes. A transformer's loss has no conservation law.

WHAT IT CANNOT DO (honest limits).
1. Fluency and world coverage. There is no distributed representation, no similarity metric, no pretraining on a trillion tokens; RelConLang is 42 JSONL lines with 14 noun roots. For years the loom will write worse prose than any 1B transformer. It is a logical-inference / predication-algebra model, not a general chatbot; its first products are certified reasoning over typed claims, not essays.
2. Scaling walls that are real: `dgBasis` is exponential in |A| (guard 2^20); `settle` in nuclei.mjs is O(steps·|G|²·|M|); valuations are string-keyed Maps copied per tell; the sheaf Laplacian is rebuilt per call with O(V) lookups; Jacobi eigendecomposition is O(n³). The kernel must be re-implemented on interned integer ids, bitsets/CSR, and incremental Next Closure before any corpus-scale claim.
3. The two-dimensional row transducer and the general decorated cover do not exist; only the Fibonacci case is proved. Wang tiling is undecidable in general, so local greedy completion can deadlock; the loom avoids this only where recognizability is proved (the decoration lifts), which today is one substitution.
4. No probabilistic semantics ties FOUR to frequencies; Record counts exist (pos,neg tallies) but no convergence-of-statistics theorem. Calibration (excludedMass vs hit rate) is unmeasured.
5. Generalization: an unseen attribute column yields ⊥; the exact basis is brittle (one counterexample deletes a rule); the weighted miner is Apriori + noisy-OR. The loom generalizes by concept closure, which is sound but narrow.
6. Vice versa, a transformer can do what the loom cannot: compress an open world into a dense function and answer anything badly. The right relation is the 08-16 note's: the transformer is glass, the loom is the stringing law; use the LLM as one typed observable per bead position, type-checked by the loom, never as the thread.

## Minimal kernel

Goal of the kernel: weave a 2-D fabric of beads over RelConLang (or any small signed context) such that (1) every row is Path-admissible, (2) every bead's charge is KLE-above its witness past cone, (3) rows never repeat (refutePeriod passes for all p), (4) two looms weaving a shared column merge order-independently with a computed seam ⊤-count and H¹, (5) every bead ships a proof term and the fabric replays. JS/ESM on SignStuff + metrologic; no new identity scheme, no new value algebra; reuse everything named below. Roughly 1,500 lines of new code.

DATA (on SignCell + computer-data-engineering `four`, metrologic closure/formal-context):
- `loom/data/bead.mjs`: bead(cell, {decoration, colours:{N,E,S,W}}) — a canonicalCarrier wrapping `cell()` (signcell/src/cell.mjs) with colours and decoration off-address; `beadCharge = valueOf(cell)`; `beadTime/beadSpace` = pos+neg / pos−neg (realizing LightCone.lean on JS readings via `sidesOfReading`); `hands(bead) = {left: cell.for, right: cell.against}`.
- `loom/data/palette.mjs`: palette from concept intents: `paletteOf(ctx, basis)` = closed signed attribute sets (via `closeAttrs`) hashed by `semanticId`; `colourOf(intent)`.
- `loom/data/fabric-context.mjs`: `contextOfFabric(fabric)` → `makeContext(G=rows|threads, M=claims, I, denials)`; `tileSet(ctx) = lawfulBasis(ctx)`; one-shot interning of ids to integers (the first scaling fix).
- `loom/data/decoration.mjs`: decorated SFT = {graph, forget} with Fibonacci default (`decoratedGoldenGraph`, `forgetDecoration` from metrologic analysis/sofic.mjs) and a constructor from any substitution via `substitutionCoverSFT` (follower cover, identity forget) marked 'shallow' until a decoration is supplied.

CODE (on SignChain):
- `loom/code/loom-rules.mjs`: `rulesOfBasis(basis, threads)` → SignChain `rule()` objects, one per implication per thread, lanes by `isDenied/baseAttr`; plus `transportRules(site)` from signgraph/src/site.mjs.
- `loom/code/wang.mjs`: `rowTransducer({graph, decoGraph, vertical})`: given previous row (beads with colours and decorations) and candidate pools per position, enumerate/sample next rows with horizontal `path(graph, word)` (metrologic sofic.mjs), decorated `path(decoGraph, …)`, and vertical `knowledgeLeqReading(above, candidate)`; returns `wang.row:witness` or `wang.row-obstruction` naming the failing edge (reuse the `sofic.path-obstruction.failedAt` shape).
- `loom/code/bead-term.mjs`: `termOf(bead, fabric)` = derivation tree through `witness.from` addresses; `checkTerm(term, rules)` re-fires each step with `fireRuleIn` locally (makes `settlementAdmits` O(depth) instead of full replay); `typeOf(bead, ctx)` = the concept intent `closeAttrs` reaches.
- `loom/code/cone.mjs`: `witnessOrder(fabric)` — causal order from witness addresses (replaces the stage+shared-term heuristic in settle.mjs `causalOrder`); `pastCone`, `futureCone`, `spacelike`, `orderingFraction` (reuse settle.mjs's `causalCone`/`orderingFraction` on the new order).

NETWORKING (on SignGraph):
- `loom/net/seam.mjs`: `seam(fabricA, fabricB, column)` → shared beads, `disagreements` = count of positions where definite readings differ (JS realization of `ReadingDisagreementBool`), `joined = mergeCellSets` on the column, `h1 = cohomologyOfCells(coverNerve)` (signgraph/src/cohomology.mjs) for 'can the strip close'.
- `loom/net/fabric-sheaf.mjs`: `sheafOfFabric(fabric, palette)` → `cellularSheaf({vertices: beads with dim |K|+2, edges: N/S and E/W adjacencies with restriction = colour projection})`; `relax(fabric, pinnedRows)` = `diffuse` with pins; `obstructions = cellularSheafCohomology`.
- `loom/net/row-message.mjs`: rows as PAM messages (`reifyMessage`, `createPam().dispatch`) so two looms exchange rows with content-addressed ids; `replayPam` for deterministic replay.

SYSTEMS (on SignCraft):
- `loom/sys/loom.mjs`: the cycle. `perceive(loom, utterances|messages)` via `tellMembrane`/dispatch → `observeHistory`; `infer(loom)` = `closureOrbit(rulesOfBasis(...), seed)` → fabric with witness order → `relax`; `weave(loom, {T, rng})` = substitution-inflated plan + `rowTransducer` candidates from `enabledMoves` + `continuationEnergy` + `generate` (signcraft/src/inference/generate.mjs) with the wit kept on the bead; `learn(loom)` = re-`lawfulBasis` + `residualLeft` row relation (algebra.mjs) + `adapt`; `reproduce(loom, mutation)` = `checkpointHistory` + genome edit; `merge(loomA, loomB)` = `mergeHistories` + `seam`.
- `loom/sys/weave-receipt.mjs`: BIT(B) = (G, κ, λ, η, δ, h) per fabric — complete the placeholders in metrologic models/tiling/tile.mjs: λ from the substitution level actually used, δ from `autocorrelation` (aperiodic-autocorrelation.mjs), h from `coloringEntropy`/`sftEntropy`, η from seam H¹, κ from the FOUR colouring; plus `refutePeriod` results and the entropy band.
- `loom/sys/assay.mjs`: the experiments below as JSON-in/JSON-out commands in the `json-command.mjs` style of signcraft/scripts/inference.

Demonstration the kernel must pass: on the RelConLang corpus, weave 1,000 rows; show (a) 0 `wang.row-obstruction`s, (b) `refutePeriod` refutes every period ≤ 500 on the decorated and visible row words, (c) realized bits/bead strictly inside (0, sftEntropy), (d) two looms with different bases on one shared column give the same seam ⊤-count under both merge orders, (e) every bead's `checkTerm` passes and one adversarially flipped bead fails.

## Experiments

- E1 Aperiodicity by construction vs by sampling. Weave 10,000 beads with the decorated Fibonacci cover (`fibDeepSofic`/`decoratedGoldenGraph`) and, as control, 10,000 attributes with signcraft generate.mjs alone (no substitution, no decoration). Measure: for every row word and column word, `refutePeriod(word, p)` for all p ≤ N/2; number of distinct rows; realized bits per bead. Prediction: the loom refutes every period and has unbounded distinct-row growth; the control either settles into a closed concept (strict regime: no enabled moves) within a few hundred steps or produces a periodic row sequence. Falsified if the control stays non-repeating as long as the loom.
- E2 The entropy band. Vary T in `continuationEnergy`/`generate` over [0.05, 5]. Measure realized bits/bead (−Σ p log p of the actual draw distribution, from the `wit.excluded` records) against the floor 0 and the ceiling `sftEntropy(substitutionCoverSFT(...))` = log₂ Perron. Prediction: there is a T-interval where bits/bead lies strictly inside (0, ceiling) and `wang.row-obstruction` count is zero; below it rows become forced (crystal), above it obstruction count rises (soup). Falsified if no such interval exists or if bits/bead exceeds the cover entropy (which would mean the cover is not a cover).
- E3 Seam disagreement is a CRDT invariant. Two looms A, B with different `lawfulBasis` (train on disjoint halves of the corpus) weave the same shared column. Compute seam ⊤-count (`ReadingDisagreementBool` realized in JS) and `mergeHistories(A,B)` vs `mergeHistories(B,A)`. Prediction: identical ⊤-count and identical merged reading per cell (the proved `crdt_merge_order_independent`); the seam's cycle-rank H¹ (`cohomologyOfCells`) predicts failure to close the strip into a bracelet. Falsified if merge order changes any reading.
- E4 Witness cones vs heuristic cones. On the same settlement compute `causalOrder` (settle.mjs: later stage AND shares a term) and the witness-address order from `derivation.from`. Measure ordering fraction, number of comparable pairs, and how many heuristic edges have no witness path. Prediction: the witness order is strictly contained in the heuristic order with lower ordering fraction; every bead's true premise set is a subset of the heuristic past cone. Falsified if any witness edge is missing from the heuristic order (would show the heuristic is not an over-approximation).
- E5 Held-out row completion without leakage. Fix the transductive leak in speak.mjs evaluation (collect expect.features only from the training split). Mask the final bead of held-out RelConLang rows; candidates = a common 5-way pool; compare (i) frequency baseline, (ii) n-gram, (iii) loom with horizontal `path` filtering + ΔF, (iv) loom with decoration; ≥5 seeds, per-seed numbers. Prediction: (iii) ≥ (i) and (ii) on grammatical validity (0 Path obstructions) and ≥ on accuracy; honest expectation on accuracy is a small margin. Falsified if the loom is below frequency on accuracy with full validity (then validity is bought with blindness).
- E6 Proof-term locality. For 1,000 derived beads compare the cost of `settlementAdmits` (full replay) with `checkTerm` (local re-firing along `witness.from`). Prediction: identical verdicts on all beads; local check cost grows with derivation depth, not with fabric size; an adversarially flipped side on one bead is refused by both. Falsified if any verdict differs.
- E7 Lambek curvature predicts order-sensitivity. For each pair of adjacent predicates in the woven rows compute whether lres = rres in the finite Lambek algebra derived from the concept lattice (lambek-calculus.mjs `laws` as oracle) and measure how often swapping the two beads yields a `wang.row-obstruction`. Prediction: swap obstructions occur only where residuals differ (`residuals_coincide_iff_commutative`); zero obstructions in commutative regions. Falsified by a swap obstruction in a region where residuals coincide.
- E8 Foam laws are not assumed. Define a region as a maximal connected set of same-charge beads under witness set D; measure dA/dt (change in bead count per row) against n (number of distinct ⊤-bordered neighbours). Prediction (honest): no clean (n−6) law; pymetrologic's settling variant already failed T1 statistics. Report the fit either way; this experiment decides whether 'foam' is mechanism or image for this fabric.
- E9 The two hands commute or do not. On 1,000 random signed contexts and on real fabrics, test σΔ = Δσ (nuclei.mjs `sigma`,`delta`) rather than only (σΔ)² = σΔ (commutation.test.mjs). Record the failure rate and, where they fail, the lenticle ⊤-count. Prediction: non-commutation localizes exactly to contested cells. Falsified if σΔ ≠ Δσ on a context with no contested cell.
- E10 Horizon query. Give two looms different readout columns and compute HoleRegion (events whose influence never reaches the readout) over the witness order. Prediction: a bead outside loom A's past cone reads ⊥ for A and may read t/f for B (observer-relative knowledge bottom); after exchanging rows through PAM the hole shrinks monotonically. Falsified if the hole ever grows under message exchange.

## Theory improvements

- FCA: define Belohlavek derivation over FOUR using the existing `residuumReading`/`fusionReading` (four.mjs twistProduct): primeA(A)(m) = meetK_g (A g → I g m), dually primeB; prove in Lean (Calculus/Order/FormalConceptAnalysis.lean extension) that the FOUR-valued concept lattice is complete and carries the twist-product residuated structure, closing edge (4) of the FCA–Lambek circle for L = 2⊗2 instead of leaving Wurm in prose; replace the M ⊔ ¬M dichotomization in formal-context.mjs by this when the denial lane is graded.
- FCA: state and prove (or refute by counterexample) σΔ = Δσ for the kernel's knowledge-order nuclei on signed contexts; conjecture: they commute on every context with no contested cell and the commutator is supported on contested cells. Then the nuclear quartet (all rows, Fix σ, Fix Δ, Fix σ∩Fix Δ) is a theorem about the loom, not a 60-sample measurement.
- FCA: a 'tile set is a basis' theorem — for a formal context whose objects are rows and attributes are (position, colour) pairs, the Duquenne–Guigues basis restricted to adjacent positions equals the forbidden-pair presentation of the horizontal SFT (`forbiddenPairs`); this makes matching rules learned rather than written and gives the Lean a bridge between FormalConceptAnalysis.lean and SoficShift.lean.

## Risks

- Exponential kernels: `dgBasis` enumerates closed sets in lectic order (guard 2^20), `settle` in nuclei.mjs is O(steps·|G|²·|M|), valuations are string-keyed Maps copied per tell, the sheaf Laplacian is rebuilt per call with O(V) `findIndex` lookups, Jacobi is O(n³). Without interned ids, bitsets/CSR, incremental Next Closure and cached Laplacians the loom never leaves toy scale.
- Undecidability and deadlock: Wang tiling is undecidable and greedy row completion can reach a row with no admissible continuation. The decorated cover avoids this only where recognizability is proved (today: Fibonacci). Every other substitution runs on the shallow follower cover (`substitutionCoverSFT`, identity forget, sampled from one seed letter) with no guarantee, and that function under-approximates the 2-factor set for non-primitive rules.
- Three code islands: foam physics is Python (pymetrologic.foam), settlement/inference is JS (SignStuff), proofs are Lean, and none call each other. The loom lives in JS; the foam claims remain prose until E8 is run, and the honest prior is that (n−6) and T1 statistics will not hold.
- Overloaded 'two hands': Token/Claim `hand:'left'/'right'` strings in utterance.mjs, for/against lanes, σ/Δ nuclei, left/right Lambek residuals, and D4 `swap` are five different things all called the two hands. The loom fixes one meaning (law vs history, joined in FOUR); the others must be renamed or mapped or the theory will keep citing itself in circles.
- Grandiose naming debt: 'potentialAperiodicArticulationCell' is a fixed 2-tuple; 'mutuallyLocallyDerivable' compares a matrix invariant; 'aperiodic' regime is three Booleans; 'Physarum' is a decayed co-occurrence counter; 'Chu-style attention' is an overlap count; 'firstCohomology' is 0 for every real settlement; several receipts return literal true. Building the loom on these names without renaming will train the next reader on false mechanisms.
- Evidence is thin and partly leaked: fixtures of ≤18 incidences, RelConLang is 42 lines and 45% duplicate facts, the one saturation result was transductive, `maskedIncidenceEval` counts any settle-derived attribute as a hit. No benchmark at scale exists; the loom's claims must start from E5 run honestly and may show only a small margin.
- Fluency gap: no distributed representation, no similarity, no pretraining. A loom will write worse prose than a small transformer for a long time; the risk is marketing it as a chatbot replacement instead of a certified-reasoning substrate that strings LLM outputs as typed beads.
- Theory frontiers cited as proved: the two-hands note cites `Autopoiesis.history_separation_aperiodicity` which does not exist (the Lean states it open with an undefined `settlingOf`); Wurm/Pentus are prose; `jeandel_rao_persistence` is conditional; hat Perron positivity is unproved. Policy built on these is policy built on air.
- Unverified cohomology semantics: whether settle.mjs `firstCohomology` and the drift-locus 'H¹' are Čech ranks or contested-site counts (game-foam Remark 7.6 says the latter) was not verified; the loom must compute coboundary and quotient explicitly or stop using the word.
- Working-tree state: cell.mjs, predication.mjs, utterance.mjs, typed-mark.mjs, articulating.mjs and mark.mjs are modified and uncommitted by others; cell.mjs and predication.mjs have no tests; three incompatible notions of 'cell' and three of 'claim' collide on exports. The kernel should import only `cell`, `address`, `merge`, `cellSet`, `mergeCellSets` from SignCell and avoid the semantic/typed-mark layer until it is normalized.
- Identity is syntactic canonical bytes; 1 and 1.0 coincide, 'a b' identifiers break the 'g m' Map keys in nuclei/algebra; a bead vocabulary with spaces silently corrupts. Intern before weaving.
- Foam/organic framing can seduce: 'cells, films, T1, T2, metabolism' are metaphors until a cost functional is measured and a conserved quantity is found. The loom's one honest energy is the knowledge-height deficit (and, once proved, ΔF); nothing else about thermodynamics is established, and the Lean itself says there is no entropy S and only T→0.
