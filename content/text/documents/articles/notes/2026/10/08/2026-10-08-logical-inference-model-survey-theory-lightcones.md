# Logical inference model survey: theory-lightcones

## Summary

The house light-cone theory has three strata. (1) PROSE, predicated cosmology (cosmology-services-section notes + glossary): a "light-cone predicate category" C_cone adds one morphism class to the predicate category, the light-cone restriction rho_{e->e'} for e <= e' in the causal order of an event set E_cos; rho is the identity on cells (s,p,o) with {s,o} in J^-(e) ∩ J^-(e') and undefined elsewhere, so the thin sub-category of restrictions IS the causal poset and closure rules are declared "spacetime-local" (fire at e, consume only J^-(e), produce at e or J^+(e)). From that, three claims: the horizon theorem (an observer at e_O values every cell with a coordinate outside J^-(e_O) at knowledge-bottom ⊥, observer-relatively), the arrow of time (= orientation of <=_k, since settlings are <=_k-monotone by definition), and "inference sub-settlings" (an observer's pipeline is a closure fixed point E_O = Close(E_O) on a sub-category C_O; two observers' values on a shared predicate that are not <=_k-joinable are a residuation failure = Hubble tension). Nothing in this stratum is formalized or executed; the object it all depends on (cosmological-settling) sits in an _archive directory and explicitly says its closure-rule system "has not been written down". (2) LEAN, two genuinely-ours finite theorems plus ~25 modules porting a ChatGPT memo. Genuinely ours: Obsmetrologia/Language/Positing/LightCone.lean proves axiom-free that FOUR's two evidence bits (pos,neg) are null coordinates (u,v) of a 1+1 Minkowski diamond: time = u+v in {0,1,2}, space = u−v, KLE a b iff |space b − space a| <= time b − time a, ⊥ past tip, ⊤ future tip, t and f spacelike-separated, negation = parity P, conflation = time-reversal T, K4 = {1,P,T,PT}, Chu duality = transpose∘parity. And Language/Settling/Cones.lean: two pointwise cones settle (close the measured value) <=_k brim (presume every free side then close), Void = cells where they differ ("void is space, apex/settling chain is time"). The Oriented.lean + 2026-09-21 note then say the cells articulating generates (⟨u.token, u.claim, a.head.token⟩) have "the shape a causal set has", with an open "void has dimension 3" frontier. The physical-science Lean (ObsmetrologiaPhysicalScience/CausalSets) realizes causal sets as Boolean transitive-closure settlings (Order.lean: CausalSet = finite DAG, causalStep T(v)(a,c) = v(a,c) ∨ ∃b v(a,b)∧v(b,c), reaches = settled value, finite => strict regime, H¹=0), and then the nine "red nodes" of the 2026-06-26 recommendations memo exactly as sketched: Cone (Future/Past/CausalLe/Spacelike/Diamond as thin predicates over reaches), Alexandrov (future cones are opens; specialization = CausalLe), Laplacian (causalClosure = future closure; harmonic iff future-closed; "gap one" = idempotence), LightObservation (past/future signatures; allObservers separate — trivially, every x is in its own reflexive past), ParaconsistentCone (ConeReport E→E→Reading, merge = joinK, Boolean embeds consistently), Net (regions, isotony, spacelike ⇒ independent as an abstract relation), Support/Microlocal (NSC firing = singularity; explicitly "a bridge, not an identity"), Diamond portrait, plus Curvature/Lambek.lean (residuals coincide iff product commutes; curvatureDefect = retarded − advanced Möbius sums, total zero, diamond (1,0,0,−1)), Dimension/OrderingFraction.lean (r = 2|Apex|/N(N−1), diamond 5/6, geometry ⊥ topology; the d_MM inverse is NOT computed), RegimeSection/PortGrowthTime (t(x)=|past(x)| strictly monotone; "time is the Bratteli level", the beads/weaving tie), and BlackHoles/Hole.lean (HoleRegion = events from which no listed readout event is reachable; future-closed; Shroud = its boundary adjacent to the readout exterior; EventHorizon carrier) — this last is the only computable realization of "horizon = what an observer cannot know yet", and Horizon.lean admits its physics names are "external-physics search aids". (3) JS: metrologic analysis/causal.mjs executes settleCausalSet (graph reachability closure, closed-timelike-curve obstruction witness, apexCount, orderingFraction, interval/pastLayer, Benincasa–Dowker d'Alembertian, longestChain/properTime, Möbius curvature) and then a tail of "receipts" that mostly return literal true; signcraft settle.mjs builds a causal order over a settling's own firing events (edge = earlier stage AND shares a subject/object term; transitive closure), with causalSeparation (timelike/spacelike), causalCone (past/future), orderingFraction, and cones()/vanish() (settle/brim/adjudicate/presume per cell; brim is the constant ⊤ so vanish = every not-fully-told cell); signcraft history.mjs is a content-addressed revision DAG (parents = heads; topological sort rejects cycles; checkpoint = "cut at the causal frontier"). The language stratum (A6 "language light cone", the reverse-lightcone emergence function 𝓡, curvature typology) is prose only; 𝓡's definition uses undefined symbols Λ, T, P, V and is explicitly flagged as empirical hypothesis; the "Language Cone" of A8/CHL is a categorical limit cone, a different object, and its commutation theorem (PPA-FR-040) is an open obligation.

How cones organize inference and language, as the corpus actually states it: a cell's past cone = the evidence that can reach it (premises); the future cone = what it can affect (consequences); the causal order of firings is the derivation order; horizon = observer-relative ⊥ (HoleRegion relative to a readout); causal diamond = a run/turn (run-causal-set.mjs: a session turn is a diamond from admission to settled exit, deshrouded while live); spacelike = independent, mergeable by knowledge join; causal set = oriented predication cells generated by articulating; and the knowledge order of FOUR is itself the causal order of a 1+1 diamond, so <=_k-monotone settling IS cone propagation, time = knowledge content, space = net evidence sign, and the void between settle and brim is "space". The relation to FOUR is therefore exact and proved at the one-cell level (LightCone.lean) and asserted but unproved at the valuation/settling level (horizon theorem, cosmological settling, void dimension).

### Light-cone predicate category C_cone and light-cone restriction rho_{e->e'}

- **File:** `services/science-services-section/services/natural-science-services-section/services/physical-science-services-section/services/astronomy-services-section/services/cosmology-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/light-cone-predicate-category.md`
- **Computes:** Nothing executable. Prose definition: objects = physical observable predicates; morphisms = the four generator classes (specialization, converse, two projections) plus a fifth, rho_{e->e'} : p -> p for e <= e' in E_cos, acting as identity on cells (s,p,o) with {s,o} ⊆ J^-(e) ∩ J^-(e'), undefined elsewhere (glossary/terms/light-cone-restriction.md). rho_{e->e}=id, rho composes along the causal order, rho is non-invertible (strictly more cells in the later cone). Admissibility of any morphism: reindexing (s,p2,o)->(s',p1,o') allowed only if a causal chain runs {s',o'} -> {s,o}. Closure rules firing at e consume only J^-(e) and produce at e or J^+(e).
- **Theory:** A presheaf of valuations on the causal poset (E_cos, <=), i.e. the thin category of restrictions is the causal partial order; 'relativistic locality' as a morphism class rather than a postulate. Claims Beck–Chevalley squares commute iff the spacetime is globally hyperbolic (flat Minkowski yes; curved/non-GH only conditionally). None of this is formalized; the object it depends on (cosmological-settling.md) lives under an _archive path and says its closure-rule system is unwritten. The structure is sound as a sketch: it is exactly 'sheaf on the Alexandrov site of a causal set', which the Lean Alexandrov.lean module does build at the finite Boolean level.

### Horizon bounds the knowledge order (theorem sketch)

### SignCraft history as a causal DAG with checkpoint = causal-frontier cut

- **File:** `services/engineering-services-section/services/computer-engineering-services-section/lib/signstuff/signcraft/src/history.mjs`
