# Logical inference model survey: theory-foam-life-language

## Summary

The house "foam" theory is one algebraic move repeated at many scales: take Belnap FOUR as a twist-product bilattice BL(pro,con) over a lattice, define a locus's phase as the knowledge join of what a chosen set of actors D have committed there (Φ_D(ξ)=⋁_k{a(ξ)}), and read the four values as void (⊥), two bulk phases (t,f) and interface (⊤ = contested). Everything else is ported foam physics stated about that field: Girard incarnation as the exact dry limit (two designs with equal incarnation are indistinguishable to the orthogonal, so no observable lives in the bulk and surface laws can exist), von Neumann dA/dt=k(n−6) ported to "a belief-community's fate depends only on its count of contested borders, not its size or evidential quality", T1 neighbour-swap = Rancière dissensus (a newcomer that changes a biorthogonal closure), T2 cell-death = daimon, a Deborah number De=τ_relax/τ_obs separating record from workspace, a thioether/disulfide/Pickering wall trichotomy for append-only vs witnessed-rewrite vs attested commitments, and a Maxwell–Calladine constraint count on strategies whose one genuine theorem (receptive closure R(σ) of an innocent play-set is well-formed and minimal) has no material analogue. "What the foam knows" is therefore exactly surface data: which loci are ⊤, how many distinct contested neighbours each bulk region has, and the clock on which walls move. It knows nothing about interiors by construction, and the note of that name is six falsifiable ports with named referees, not a result.

What was actually computed is much smaller than what is written. pymetrologic.foam (Python) implements a triangulated torus cell-adjacency graph with T1 diagonal flips and T2 removal of 3-sided cells, a reference law that reproduces (n−6) by construction, and a "settling" variant where each boundary's charge is join_k(_posit(n_i),_posit(n_j)) with _posit(n)=⟨1−|n−6|/6, |n−6|/6⟩ and area flux k·conflict_degree·sign(n_i−n_j); the results note honestly reports partial (n−6) reproduction (root 4.9–5.6, systematic kink), seed-unstable Aboav–Weaire, and outright failure on T1 avalanche statistics (85–103-event synchronized bursts from a global scalar threshold). SignStuff (JS) supplies the settlement surface the theory presupposes: settleCells, firstCohomology, causalCone/causalOrder, filtration, membrane.mjs (an utterance ⟨token,claim⟩ becomes a cell told for/against by negation parity), and inference/generate.mjs (Gibbs descent in the enabled cone of an FCA implication basis with energy ΔF=α·opened−β·closed+T·ΔE where ΔE is a graph-Laplacian disagreement energy). Holofoam, despite its name and the smartgoo program's treatment of it as a lost "foam simulator", is a deterministic boids-style agent model on a torus (analytic sin/cos+whirl flow field, 24×24 diffusing signal grid) whose only SignStuff content is a 16-row policy lookup table compiled by settleCells, plus unrelated rocket/orbit mechanics; its events are recorded as a content-addressed SignCraft history and replayed. Lean anchors cited by the Witnessed Field theory do exist (FCALambekCircle.lean: fca_closure_laws, concept_lattice_is_complete, the_circle; CurryHowardLambek.lean: church_rosser l.717, chl_quartet_is_nuclear l.944; YonedaArrowsFirst.lean; LambekCalculus.lean) but prove facts about formal objects only. None of the three code islands (Python foam, JS SignStuff, Lean) call each other.

The "living language-and-inference medium" picture assembles as follows, and is a design image rather than a built thing. Cells-as-bubbles: a behaviour is an equivalence class of designs under "same orthogonal" (same counter-designs relax its walls), i.e. a phase; in SignStuff terms a bulk region is a set of cells all told one way under a chosen witness set D. Membranes: in code, a faculty's set of utterances read into cells (membrane.mjs), and in the Physarum spec a "sovereign membrane" whose crossing turns a wit (one 0→1 evidence flip) into a tally; in the smartgoo program, literal mineral membranes (chemical gardens) that must show transport before being called semipermeable. Settling: FCA closure = settling closure (Lean), lignification in the bamboo mapping, precipitation in the silica/water reading, and in foam.py literally a meet(pro,con) per edge. Autocatalysis: the only rigorous house account is the CRN note (catalysis = zero entry of N; conservation = left kernel P-invariant; regeneration = right kernel T-invariant; growth = Perron–Frobenius eigenvector, autocatalysis = λ>1), plus the axiality note's reading of Frank amplification as repair of an orientation sheaf's H¹; the sociology glossary's β⁺ "autocatalytic attractor" is undefined notation. RelConLang: a conlang whose stance particles ta/na/sha/mu are indexed by FOUR through a Chu matrix particle×{truth,falsity}→FOUR, whose dialogue particles ku/du/se/ha are state transitions (open/close relevance), and whose central design law—"report, do not inject": grammar produces stance/denial/repair reports with evidenceEffect none-by-language-alone, and only an explicit evidential policy promotes a report to a SignCell for/against witness—is the one place where the house has actually specified the membrane between language and evidence. The corpus behind it is 42 JSONL lines, the parsers tag particles as determiners, and no realizer exists.
