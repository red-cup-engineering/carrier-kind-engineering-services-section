# PRIMORDIUM: an autocatalytic proof soup (RAF-settling protocells over FOUR)

*A population of content-addressed predication cells with evidence tallies is the soup; Fitting rule firings (SignChain `fireRuleIn`) are the reactions whose products carry their own proof terms; witness decay is the heat bath; a protocell is a formal concept (Galois-closed set of cells) whose membrane is the closure operator `closeAttrs`/`delta`; the theories that survive are exactly the Kauffman RAF sets (every reaction catalyzed and food-reachable) whose settlement flux (`witFlux`, Lean `ledger_closes`) outruns decay; protocells talk by exchanging beads (two-hand utterances via `pam.mjs` messages), and a knowing organism is a multicellular foam of protocells whose shared contested cells are the films.*

This is an unjudged round-one vision. It is kept as source material, not as an adopted design.

## Image

THE MEDIUM. A soup is a cell set in the SignCell sense: frozen triples (subject, predicate, object) each carrying two grow-only witness sets and therefore a FOUR reading (cell.mjs `cell`, `merge`, `witnessFor/Against`; four.mjs `readingOfSides`). Replace the Boolean 'is the side non-empty' with the counting carrier that already exists: `record(pos, neg)` and `recordAdd` (four.mjs; Lean Language/Positing/Record.lean). A cell's record IS a concentration: how many witness-molecules of each sign are present. The soup is therefore a vector in N^2 per triple, with FOUR as its >0 shadow (`readingOfRecord`). This is mechanism, not metaphor: the Record twist product is already proved to carry both orders.

THE MOLECULES. A molecule is a typed term in the CHL sense, and the house already has it in two halves that have never been joined: (a) the proposition is the triple with its reading; (b) the proof term is the derivation witness `{rule, from: [premise addresses]}` that `rules.mjs derivation()` writes into the `for`/`against` side when `fireRuleIn` fires. So every derived cell is already a (proposition, proof) pair; the proof is a content-addressed DAG over premise addresses. AlChemy (Fontana–Buss) collisions are applications; here a collision is a rule meeting a valuation: `fireRuleIn(rule, valuation)` returns the consequent cell on the side its reading tells, witnessed by the derivation. Composition of two rules whose codomain/domain coincide is already `composeRule` in signchain/typed-theory.mjs (trace concatenation) — that is cut.

THE HEAT BATH (the one thing that must be added for abiogenesis to be non-trivial). Kauffman's RAF condition is trivial in a monotone logic: nothing is ever consumed, so 'every reactant reachable from food' is just derivability, and `closureOrbit` (settle.mjs) hits its fixed point in at most rules.length+1 stages — every soup is a crystal within one settlement. The Lean library proves exactly this (`converges_in_bounded_steps`, `kappa_bounded_by_initial_free_energy`, FreeEnergyLyapunov.lean): the free energy only drops, and when it hits zero the system is dead ('a finished game is a crystal, and a crystal is dead' — the 2026-10-02 two-hands note). To get life we need a source term: WITNESS DECAY. A witness has a half-life; when all witnesses on a side are older than the Deborah period τ (the house's own De = τ_relax/τ_obs from the FOAM notes and `observeProofKinetics` in metrologic proof-foam.mjs) they are archived — `checkpointHistory` (history.mjs) literally does this cut — and the side's record count drops. A cell stays 'potential' only if it is re-derived faster than it decays. Now derivability is a METABOLISM: `stepLedger` flux (commits + mints + 2·jumps, nuclei.mjs; Lean `stepFlux_decomposition`) is energy spent per firing, decay is energy lost, and the net is a balance law instead of a Lyapunov descent.

THE PROTOCELLS. A protocell is a formal concept of the soup read as a formal context (objects = subject tokens, attributes = (predicate, object) claims — exactly `mesh.claimOf` and `mesh.generatedConcepts` in signgraph/mesh.mjs, built on metrologic `closeAttrs`). Its extent is the set of molecules inside; its intent is what they all share. The membrane is the closure operator itself: a molecule 'enters' a protocell iff it lies in the Galois closure of the protocell's profile — which is literally `delta` in signcraft/inference/nuclei.mjs (Galois transfer along a realized profile). Semipermeability = `lawfulBasis`'s non-empty-extent guard: implications with unrealized premises do not cross, so a contradiction (⊤ cell) stays local (the paraconsistent membrane). Inside the protocell, `sigma` (Duquenne–Guigues implication closure) is the cytoplasmic reaction network: the DG basis induced by `dgBasis` (metrologic formal-context.mjs) is the protocell's genome of ground rules, and `learn.mjs induceBasis` re-induces it from what the protocell currently holds — that is heredity with variation (one counterexample deletes a rule).

THE FOAM. Two protocells share a cell when the same triple is in both extents. If they tell it the same way the shared cell is a transparent film (knowledge join leaves it one-sided); if they disagree the join is ⊤ — a contested cell — and that is the interface film carrying tension. This is the game-foam Prop 3.2 reading (void ⊥, two bulks t/f, interface ⊤) and it is computed by `knowledgeJoinReading` and counted by `pricedNerve` (signgraph/cohomology.mjs: top, mass, rank) and `pairwiseLenticleCensus` (Lean LenticleIdentification.lean). Plateau's rule — three films meet at a vertex — is three protocells disagreeing on one cell, and the tally pattern in computer-data-engineering tally.mjs (per-field agree/disagree joined in FOUR) is the local compatibility check. The von Neumann law ported by the 'what the foam knows' note (a protocell's fate depends only on its count of contested borders n, not on size) is a FALSIFIABLE prediction in this design, not an axiom; pymetrologic foam.py's `settling` variant partially reproduced (n−6) and failed on T1 statistics, so we do not assume it.

HOW IT MOVES. Molecules move only by messages: a PAM message `{sender, claims[{s,p,o,polarity,evidence}]}` (signgraph/pam.mjs `reifyMessage`, `createPam().dispatch`) is a packet of beads thrown from one protocell to another; the two-hand utterance (signcell/utterance.mjs `Token` left, `Claim` right) is the bead. T1 (neighbour swap) is dissensus: a newcomer whose claims change a protocell's biorthogonal closure — concretely, a message whose cells change `closeAttrs(profile)`. T2 (cell death) is a protocell whose intent has become all-⊤ (daimon): every reaction yields contested products, flux goes to zero, decay wins, the extent empties. Coarsening is `mergeHistories` (history.mjs): two protocells whose concept extents become one concept. Fission is concept-lattice refinement: when `generatedConcepts` of a protocell's own extent yields two concepts with disjoint non-trivial extents, the protocell divides along the new intent boundary.

WHAT IS METAPHOR VS MECHANISM. Mechanism: records as concentrations; rule firing as reaction with a proof-term product; closure as membrane; flux ledger as energy; witness decay as heat; FOUR join as film; cohomology counts as obstruction; message exchange as molecular transfer; CRDT merge as fusion. Metaphor until measured: surface tension (no cost functional exists; `continuationEnergy` ΔF in generate.mjs is the candidate), von Neumann (n−6), Plateau 120°, 'life'. Not even metaphor yet: foam geometry (no Voronoi, no areas), Perron growth of a theory (computable from a stoichiometric matrix of the rule set — see mathematics — but not yet built).

## Mathematics

OBJECTS AND LAWS.

1. Value algebra. FOUR = Two^twist (four.mjs `twistProduct`, Lean Four.lean `four_initial`): two orders ≤k (knowledge) and ≤t (truth), negation and conflation forming Klein-4. Record = N^2 twist (four.mjs `record`, Lean Record.lean): concentration carrier; `topMass = min(pos,neg)` is contradiction mass that `recordAdd` never destroys (Lean `topMass_add`). We USE Record as the molecule count and FOUR as its phase readout. IMPROVEMENT: instantiate `twistProduct` over N with min/max and over the Łukasiewicz [0,1] chain so graded consistency exists; nothing in the cell code changes (the maps confirm the twist is generic and untested beyond Two).

2. Molecules (CHL). A molecule m = (c, π) where c is a cell (triple + record) and π is a derivation term. Terms are generated by: axiom (a told witness: opaque JSON today), cut (`fireRuleIn` writes `{rule, from}` — rules.mjs lines 46–60), composition (`composeRule` in typed-theory.mjs: trace concatenation when codomain meets domain). The hom-set Hom(Γ, c) = set of derivation terms of c from premises Γ. Today signchain collapses this to reachability (`sameGeneratedRule` compares domain/codomain only); we restore proof relevance by keying on the derivation address `semanticId({rule, from})`. Normal form: the proof DAG with duplicate sub-derivations content-address-deduplicated; `church_rosser` (Lean CurryHowardLambek.lean l.717) is the template theorem for 'normalization is canonical', but it is proved for STLC only, not for this rule calculus — flagged as work.

3. Reactions = rule firings = Fitting immediate consequence T_S(v) = v ⊔k fire_S(v) (rules.mjs `step`; Lean Closure/RuleSystem.lean `Tstep_infl`). Each firing costs flux ≥ 1 (Lean `deficit_strict_of_fires`); total flux equals initial deficit (Lean `ledger_closes`, `rule_ledger`; JS `stepLedger` in nuclei.mjs and `witFlux` in four.mjs). Decay D_τ: for each side, drop witnesses older than τ (implemented as `checkpointHistory` with archive). The soup dynamics is x_{t+1} = D_τ(T_S(x_t)). THEOREM TO PROVE (new): with decay, deficit is no longer a Lyapunov function; define balance B_t = flux_t − decay_t; a protocell persists iff liminf B_t ≥ 0.

4. RAF sets (Kauffman; Hordijk–Steel algorithm). Food F = cells told by external witnesses (membrane.mjs `tellingOf` output, PAM dispatched claims). Reactions R = rules. Catalysis: rule r is catalyzed by cell c iff c ∈ antecedent(r) with `atLeast = 'potential'` and c is itself derived (its witnesses are derivation terms, not axioms). Reachability: all antecedents of r in the closure of F under R. RAF(F, R) = the maximal subset R' ⊆ R such that every r ∈ R' is catalyzed by a cell in Cl_{R'}(F) and reactant-reachable. Hordijk–Steel: iteratively delete uncatalyzed/unreachable rules; terminates in ≤ |R| passes; the maxRAF is unique (it is a closure operator in the dual sense). WHY NON-TRIVIAL HERE: with decay, a derived catalyst that is not itself re-derived within τ disappears, so only rule sets that close on themselves survive — exactly the autocatalytic theories. This is the central mechanism of the vision and the central new definition for the house theory (an autocatalytic theory = a RAF set over a Fitting rule system with decay).

5. Stoichiometry (from the CRN note in the foam maps, now made executable). Build N ∈ Z^{cells × rules}: N[c, r] = +1 if c is the consequent of r, −w if c is an antecedent weighted by its decay cost, 0 if c is a pure catalyst (unconsumed). Left kernel P-invariants = conserved quantities of a theory; right kernel T-invariants = regenerating cycles (the RAF cores); Perron root of the positive part λ: λ>1 autocatalytic growth, λ=1 catalytic maintenance, λ<1 depletion. Exact rank/kernels are available (metrologic number/rational-matrix `ratRank`, used by `cellularSheafCohomology`).

6. Membranes (FCA). Formal context K = (G = subject tokens, M = (predicate, object) claims, I = told-for); closure A ↦ A'' (`closeAttrs`); protocell = concept (A, B) with A = A'', B = A'. Transport across the membrane = `delta` (Galois transfer) — a knowledge-order nucleus; internal metabolism = `sigma` (DG-basis closure). The two commute only numerically today (commutation.test.mjs, 60 random contexts). IMPROVEMENT: relax restrictions (2) and (4) of the 2026-05-02 'five restrictions' note: multiple predicates and multiple scopes = a FAMILY of contexts indexed by protocell, with transfer along `Chu maps` (metrologic chu.mjs `chuMap`, adjoint pairs) between contexts — the membrane between two protocells is a Chu map, and its adjointness condition is the semipermeability law.

7. Gluing (sheaves). Overlapping protocells form a cover; local sections are their valuations; gluing is `mergeCellSets`/`mergeHistories` (join), obstruction is counted by `pricedNerve` (top, mass, H1 rank) and `glueSignGraphs` obstructions; numeric transport is `cellular-sheaf.mjs diffuse/solveNormal` with exact H0/H1 (`cellularSheafCohomology`). Lean: `glue_coboundary`, `glue_unique` (H1 = 0 in the strict regime), `crdt_merge_order_independent`. The living regime is where H1 ≠ 0: contested films exist.

8. Light cones. The causal order on firings must be the derivation DAG (`derivation().from` addresses), NOT settle.mjs's heuristic (`causalOrder`: later stage AND shares a term). Past cone of a molecule = transitive closure of its `from` addresses = its premises; future cone = molecules whose derivations cite it = consequences. Horizon of a protocell = `HoleRegion` (ObsmetrologiaPhysicalScience BlackHoles/Hole.lean) relative to its readout: cells no derivation can carry to it read ⊥ (the horizon theorem sketch of the cosmology notes, made computable). At the value level, FOUR's knowledge order IS a 1+1 light cone (Lean LightCone.lean `kle_iff_lightcone`: time = pos+neg, space = pos−neg); with Record counts this generalizes verbatim: time = total witness mass, space = net sign, and decay is literally time running backwards on a cell (conflation = time reversal, `time_conflate`).

9. Tiles. A settlement orbit is a strip tiling: row t = valuation v_t, tile at (t, c) = (reading of c at t, reading at t+1, the rule that fired); vertical edge colour = reading continuity, horizontal edge colour = premise/consequent matching (codomain=domain, exactly `composeRule`'s guard). `closureOrbit` is therefore a row-to-row Wang transducer (the missing 2-D Path that the tiles map says does not exist — it does, implicitly, in settle.mjs). Strict regime = eventually constant rows = periodic = crystal. Living soup = non-eventually-periodic strip, guaranteed by the residue theorem: adding a nonzero record each lap never repeats (Lean RegimeCycle `residue_dichotomy`, Worlds.lean `kinetic_forced_by_residue`) — decay plus food inflow is exactly a nonzero residue. Hidden order: emission uses a decorated sofic cover (`fib_deep_sofic`, `sofic_of_decorated`, SoficShift.lean; JS analysis/sofic.mjs `fibDeepSofic`): the protocell's internal state (which supertile/which concept it is extending) is the decoration, the emitted claim is `forgetDecoration`.

10. Beads / two hands. A bead = a two-hand utterance (Token left, Claim right; utterance.mjs). A thread pass = (bead, in-port, out-port), legal iff in ≠ out (Lean PortGraph.lean `legal_iff_distinct`): a molecule enters a protocell on one port (premise role) and leaves on another (conclusion role); reflexive cells (s,p,s) are illegal passes, which is why `predicationSeed` is only a seed. Left hand checks law (SFT admissibility of the derivation row, ≤t), right hand checks the accumulated record (≤k only climbs, Lean History.lean prefix order). The lenticle census (`pairwiseLenticleCensus_eq_h0top`) counts where the hands disagree; a dialogue between two protocells is a weave whose crossing reading is `crossingReading(warpOver, weftOver)` = `readingOfSides` (metrologic weave-draft.mjs), hole = ⊥, double-over = ⊤. Closing a thread into a bracelet (an argument that returns to its premise) is the seam check `h1_vanishes_iff_forest` (cycle rank).

## Cycle

PERCEIVE. Food arrives as utterances. membrane.mjs `cellOfUtterance`/`tellingOf` turns [token, claim] into a cell told for/against by negation parity; PAM `reifyMessage` + `dispatch` records the claims with sender and polarity and returns read witnesses. Operation on the math: F_t := F_{t−1} ⊔k (new told cells), each witness stamped with arrival time (history.mjs `observeHistory` revision id = content address of {parents, source, cells}). Report-do-not-inject: a stance particle produces a stance report; only the protocell's evidential policy promotes it to a witness — grammar never fabricates concentration.

INFER (react). For each protocell P with rule set R_P: run one Fitting step inside the membrane, x ↦ D_τ(T_{R_P}(x)) (rules.mjs `step`, then decay by archiving witnesses older than τ). Record flux per firing (`stepLedger`) and decay per cell; compute balance B. Products carry `{rule, from}` proof terms. Membrane transport: `delta` moves a molecule into P iff it lies in P's Galois closure; `lawfulBasis` keeps implications with unrealized premises out. Contradictions stay as ⊤ cells with `topMass` as their mass. Causal bookkeeping: append the derivation edges to the causal DAG (premise addresses → product address). Replace `closureOrbit`'s once-per-rule `fired` guard: with decay a rule must be allowed to re-fire whenever its antecedent evidence is refreshed, otherwise metabolism stalls.

GENERATE (emit beads). For a protocell with hidden decoration state d (which concept intent it is currently extending), compute `enabledMoves(ctx, basis, v, g)` restricted to the FUTURE CONE of its current premises (filter by causal order: only consequences inside the cone of what P holds) and to decorated-SFT admissibility ((claim, d) → (claim', d') allowed per `decoratedGoldenGraph`-style step). Score by `continuationEnergy` ΔF = α·opened − β·closed + T·ΔE, draw Gibbs at temperature T, log excluded mass as a wit (`generate`). Emit `forgetDecoration(claim', d')` as a bead: a two-hand utterance (`utterance(Token(mark), Claim(mark))`) packed into a PAM message addressed to the neighbours whose extents overlap P's. The two hands assay the bead before it leaves: left = `sofic.path` admissibility of the appended row; right = ≤k monotonicity against P's record; disagreement is held as ⊤ (lenticle census), never backtracked.

LEARN (heredity with variation). Every k periods, re-induce the protocell's genome from what it now holds: `induceBasis` (DG basis via `dgBasis`, filtered by `lawfulBasis`) for exact rules; `induceWeightedImplications` for soft ones; `adapt` (Physarum conductance from `fluxFromTrace`) tunes ΔE; `residualLeft`/`trainTwoLayer` fits any relational layer in one exact pass against the protocell's (premise, conclusion) record. Fix in kernel.mjs: `makeMachineState` builds the basis once and `runMachineAction('tell')` never re-induces it; the learn step must be a first-class action. A rule that is deleted by one counterexample is a mutation; a rule that is re-induced identically across periods is heritable.

REPRODUCE / SELECT. Compute RAF(F_P, R_P) by Hordijk–Steel elimination. If empty: P's catalysts decay without replacement, flux → 0, extent empties = death (T2). If nonempty and the stoichiometric Perron root λ > 1: the protocell's concept lattice refines (`generatedConcepts` on its own extent yields two concepts with disjoint non-trivial extents) = fission into two protocells each inheriting the sub-basis supported on its extent. If two protocells' overlap has H1 = 0 (`pricedNerve.top` = 0, `cellularSheafCohomology.h1` = 0) their histories `mergeHistories` = fusion (coarsening). Selection is not a fitness function: it is persistence under decay, measured as RAF non-emptiness and λ ≥ 1 per Deborah period. The soup as a whole is a MAPE-K node: Monitor = perceive, Analyze = infer, Plan = generate, Execute = emit, K = the history DAG (`observeHistory`, `historyDerivation` with frame-rule caching for incremental re-settlement).

## Language surface

TOKENS. A token is a Mark (signcell/mark.mjs `Mark(v)`): an opaque present datum. A bead is a two-hand utterance (utterance.mjs `Token` left-hand, `Claim` right-hand); the same mark in the two roles never coincides (`tokenNeClaim`), which is the only structural asymmetry between 'what is named' and 'what is said of it'.

SENTENCES. A sentence is a row of beads threaded through (s,p,o) passes, i.e. a word in predicates with coincident endpoints: `predicateWordArrow`/`composePredicateWords` (signchain/typed-theory.mjs) and `paths.compose` (signgraph/paths.mjs) already build exactly this path category (one round; must be iterated to closure). Why a sentence is a PROOF: a row is a chain of rule firings whose derivation terms `{rule, from}` compose by `composeRule`; the sentence is the proof term of its last cell from its first (Lambek: a·(a\c) ≤ c, Lean `modus_ponens_left`; the left residual a\c is 'what must follow a to reach c' = future cone, the right residual c/b the past cone, their disagreement = non-commutativity = curvature, Lean `residuals_coincide_iff_commutative`). Why a sentence is a WEAVE: at every bead the two hands cross — the law hand (SFT admissibility of the row, ≤t) over or under the record hand (≤k monotone history) — and the crossing reads in FOUR via `crossingReading`; a hole is a bead neither hand touches (⊥), a double-over is a bead they fight over (⊤). Why a sentence is a TILING ROW: the row is a Wang row of the derivation strip, each tile (reading before, reading after, rule) matching its neighbours on premise/consequent colours; the row is admissible iff `sofic.path(graph, row)` returns a witness; aperiodic order comes from the hidden decoration (concept being extended) that `forgetDecoration` strips before emission (`fib_deep_sofic` is the worked template).

DIALOGUE. A dialogue is molecular exchange between protocells over PAM messages (`reifyMessage`, `dispatch`, `replayPam`). Conditional relevance is a state transition, not a truth value (RelConLang dialogue-transition-laws-v0.1.json): a question (ku/du) is an Opponent move that receptivity forces to be answered, an answer/ack (se/ha) a Player move with at most one per history — the polarity that game-foam §10 asks about, and that Theorem 6.8 (receptive closure R(σ)) needs. The 'report, do not inject' law is the language↔evidence membrane: particles produce stance/denial/repair reports; an explicit evidential policy, carrying speaker provenance as the witness, promotes a report to `witnessFor`/`witnessAgainst`. Today SignCraft intake violates this (defaults every clause to 'potential' and copies particle→reading); the kernel must not.

READING. Comprehension = `comprehend` (speak.mjs) parsing to a cell or a typed 'unsaturated' question; then `delta` transports the cell into the receiving protocell iff it lies in the closure; `sigma` fires consequences; the lenticle census reports what the two hands disagree on. Understanding = reaching a normal form (the dedup'd derivation DAG) — Church–Rosser as a proved template, not yet for this calculus.

HONEST SCALE. RelConLang is 14 noun roots, 12 verb roots, a 42-line corpus, parsers that tag particles as determiners, and masked-object prediction that ties the frequency baseline. This surface is a controlled sandbox with exact ground truth, not a competitor to natural-language models; its value is that every emitted bead has a proof term, a cone and a receipt.

## Why a new class

WHAT IT DOES THAT A TRANSFORMER STRUCTURALLY CANNOT.
(1) Every output carries a proof term: the derivation DAG `{rule, from}` from rules.mjs, content-addressed, checkable step-by-step (`derivationValidity`) rather than by re-running the whole model. A transformer's output has no derivation; attributions are post hoc.
(2) Contradiction is a first-class state, not noise: ⊤ cells with `topMass` localize inconsistency to triples instead of blending logits; the H-theorem ledger (Lean `ledger_closes`) accounts for every unit of knowledge gained.
(3) Coordination-free multi-agent knowledge: `mergeHistories`/`mergeCellSets` are semilattice joins (Lean `crdt_merge_order_independent`), so protocells exchange molecules without consensus and disagreement shows up as H1, not as a crash or an averaged answer.
(4) Monotone, auditable learning without gradients: `residualLeft` is an exact adjoint (greatest W with A⊗W ≤ B), `dgBasis` is a canonical minimal rule set; the 'weights' are readable implications and one can say which datum created or killed a rule.
(5) It knows what it cannot know: horizon = `HoleRegion` relative to its readout; cells outside its past cone read ⊥ by construction, not by hallucinating.
(6) It is alive in a precise sense a transformer is not: a persistent stateful population with metabolism (flux vs decay), heredity (re-induced bases), variation (counterexample deletion, Gibbs emission), selection (RAF persistence), and a never-repeating orbit (`residue_dichotomy`) — a transformer is a frozen function ('glass', per the 2026-08-16 note).
(7) Every sample is priced: excluded probability mass is logged as a wit (generate.mjs), so creativity is budgeted, not silent.

WHAT IT STRUCTURALLY CANNOT DO (and vice versa).
(a) No perceptual grounding and no distributed representation: terms are opaque content-addressed JSON; identity is syntactic (canonical bytes); 1 and 1.0 coincide but 'dog' and 'hound' never do unless a rule says so. Transformers get similarity for free; here similarity must be derived (FCA concept-mates are a 1-step neighbourhood today).
(b) Propositional ground rules only: Fitting rules are ground cells; no variables, unification or function symbols (Datalog power at best). A transformer generalizes across unseen tokens; this kernel reads unseen columns as ⊥ (`restrictRows`).
(c) Exponential induction: `dgBasis` enumerates closed sets in lectic order (guard 2^min(|A|,20)); FCA concept enumeration is exponential; `cellularSheafCohomology` is dense BigInt rank; `complex.mjs` Jacobi is O(n^3). Vocabularies beyond ~20 attributes cannot be induced exactly; the soup must use per-protocell (local) contexts and sampled/weighted bases.
(d) No demonstrated benefit yet: the only benchmarks (evaluate.mjs, speak.mjs saturation) tie or barely beat a frequency baseline on toy fixtures, and one leaked held-out features.
(e) Natural language at scale is out of reach: no Lambek grammar exists in code, the parser is a hand-written recognizer for a conlang; the system will not write essays or chat fluently. Its 'language' is molecular exchange of typed claims.
(f) The monotone theorems (free-energy descent, bounded κ, H1 = 0 in the strict regime) stop holding once decay is added; the living regime is exactly where the proved guarantees weaken to balance laws that still need proving.

## Minimal kernel

All ESM JS, on top of SignStuff + metrologic, no new identity scheme (semanticId everywhere), layered data → code → networking → systems. Package `soup/` with one module per concern; each module cites the Lean name it realizes and extends `obsmetrologiaRealization` with its bindings.

DATA (uses SignCell + metrologic only)
- `soup/data/molecule.mjs`: `molecule(cell, term)`; `term` ∈ {axiom(witness), cut({rule, from}) as written by rules.mjs `derivation`}; `moleculeAddress = address(cell)`; `termAddress = semanticId(term)`. Replaces opaque witnesses by derivation terms so witnesses become cells-as-proofs (the 'make the witness a first-class cell' hook).
- `soup/data/concentration.mjs`: Record carrier per cell using four.mjs `record`, `recordAdd`, `topMass`, `readingOfRecord`; `twistProduct` instantiated over (N, min, max) and over Łukasiewicz [0,1] for graded consistency. Stamped witnesses `{term, at}` so decay can read age.
- `soup/data/bead.mjs`: `bead = utterance(Token(mark), Claim(mark))` (utterance.mjs) plus a thread pass `(bead, inPort, outPort)` legal iff `inPort !== outPort` (PortGraph.lean `legal_iff_distinct`).

CODE (uses SignChain + data)
- `soup/code/reaction.mjs`: `react(rules, valuation)` = rules.mjs `fire`/`step` with flux accounting (`cellFlux`, `stepLedger`) and derivation-DAG edge emission; `cut(r1, r2)` = typed-theory `composeRule` keyed by term address (proof-relevant hom-sets, dropping `sameGeneratedRule`'s domain/codomain-only dedup).
- `soup/code/decay.mjs`: `decay(valuation, now, tau)` archives witnesses older than τ via history.mjs `checkpointHistory`/`bindHistoryArchive` semantics and recomputes records; emits decay ledger. Deborah number from metrologic proof-foam.mjs `observeProofKinetics`.
- `soup/code/raf.mjs`: `stoichiometry(rules, food)` → integer N; `pInvariants`, `tInvariants` via metrologic number/rational-matrix rank/kernel; `perronRoot` (power iteration on positive part, exact rational bound, as substitution/index.mjs `perronConvergent` does); `maxRAF(food, rules, valuation)` Hordijk–Steel elimination; `isAlive(protocell)` = maxRAF nonempty ∧ balance ≥ 0.
- `soup/code/genome.mjs`: `induce(protocell)` = learn.mjs `induceBasis` (dgBasis → lawfulBasis) + `induceWeightedImplications`; `mutationLog` = diff of successive bases by rule address.
- `soup/code/cone.mjs`: `causalOrderOfDerivations(dag)` (replaces settle.mjs heuristic `causalOrder`, keeps `causalCone`/`causalSeparation`/`orderingFraction` API); `horizon(protocell, readout)` = HoleRegion over the derivation DAG; `inCone(premises, candidate)` filter for generation.

NETWORKING (uses SignGraph + code)
- `soup/net/membrane.mjs`: `protocellOf(soup, seedTokens)` = concept via metrologic `closeAttrs` / mesh.mjs `generatedConcepts`; `transport(protocell, molecule)` = nuclei.mjs `delta` admission (enters iff in Galois closure) with `lawfulBasis` guard; `chuMembrane(P, Q)` = metrologic chu.mjs `chuMap` between the two contexts, checked adjoint.
- `soup/net/film.mjs`: `films(P, Q)` = overlap cells; `tension` = `pricedNerve` (top, mass, rank) + `cellularSheafCohomology(h1)` on the overlap; `glue(P, Q)` = `mergeHistories` iff h1 = 0 else obstruction report (semantic-graph.mjs `glueSignGraphs` pattern).
- `soup/net/exchange.mjs`: beads → PAM messages (`reifyMessage`, `createPam().dispatch`, `replayPam`); report-do-not-inject: `stanceReport` → `evidentialPolicy` → `witnessFor/Against`.
- `soup/net/weave.mjs`: two-hand assay of each row: left `sofic.path(graph, row)` (metrologic analysis/sofic.mjs), right ≤k monotonicity vs record; `lenticleCensus` = count of ⊤ on overlap (`pairwiseLenticleCensus_eq_h0top`); `seamCheck(cycle)` = cycle rank (`h1_vanishes_iff_forest`); `crossingReading` from weave-draft.mjs.

SYSTEMS (uses SignCraft + net)
- `soup/sys/protocell.mjs`: pure reducer extending kernel.mjs `runMachineAction` with actions `perceive | react | decay | generate | induce | divide | fuse`; state = {ctx, basis, cx, v (records), dag, history, decoration}; every action returns {state, effects, receipt}; basis re-induction is an action (fixes the frozen-basis bug).
- `soup/sys/emit.mjs`: `enabledMoves` ∩ future cone ∩ decorated-SFT admissible; `continuationEnergy` + `generate` Gibbs with excluded-mass wit; emit `forgetDecoration` bead.
- `soup/sys/soup.mjs`: population of protocells with a shared decay clock; per period: perceive → react → decay → generate → exchange → induce → RAF test → divide/fuse; receipts appended to one SignCraft history (`observeHistory`), replayable (`replayHistory`) and mergeable across nodes (`mergeHistories`).
- `soup/sys/assay.mjs`: the experiments below as JSON CLI commands in the scripts/inference `jsonCommand` style; emits BIT-style receipts (adjacency, FOUR colouring, inflation level, seam rank, autocorrelation from aperiodic-autocorrelation.mjs, bits of remaining choice from `coloringEntropy`/`sftEntropy`).

CORE CLAIM THE KERNEL DEMONSTRATES: seed a soup with random ground rules and a food set; without decay every protocell settles to a crystal in ≤ |R|+1 stages (as `closureOrbit` guarantees today); with decay only protocells whose rule sets contain a nonempty maxRAF with λ ≥ 1 persist, and those protocells keep emitting non-repeating, locally lawful bead rows while holding their contradictions as ⊤ films. Prerequisite cleanup before building: unify the three 'cell' notions (cell.mjs triple vs typed-mark 'sign.cell' vs semantic-carriers cellSchema), the two cell-set implementations, and the four FCA implementations into metrologic formal-context.mjs.

## Experiments

- RAF PHASE TRANSITION. Random soups: n cells, m ground rules, each rule catalyzed with probability p by a derived cell, witness half-life τ. Prediction (Kauffman/Hordijk–Steel): the fraction of protocells alive after 50τ jumps from ≈0 to ≈1 at a threshold in p·m/n; alive ⇔ maxRAF(food, rules) nonempty. Falsified if persistence is uncorrelated with maxRAF non-emptiness or no threshold appears in 10^3 seeds.
- BALANCE LAW REPLACES THE H-THEOREM. For each protocell record per period flux f (stepLedger: commits+mints+2·jumps) and decay loss d. Prediction: without decay Σf = initialDeficit exactly (Lean ledger_closes; test must pass bit-for-bit); with decay, survival over 100 periods ⇔ Σ(f−d) ≥ 0, with the Perron root λ of the stoichiometric positive part > 1 for growing protocells and < 1 for dying ones. Falsified if λ fails to separate survivors from non-survivors (AUC < 0.8 over 500 protocells).
- COORDINATION-FREE GLUING. Two protocells exchange beads in all 2^k interleavings of k messages. Prediction: settle(merge(a,b)) is identical by content address under every order (crdt_merge_order_independent), and the number of contested cells after merge equals pricedNerve.top and equals cellularSheafCohomology(h1) of the overlap sheaf with constant stalks. Falsified by any address mismatch or any disagreement between the three obstruction counts.
- DERIVATION CONES BEAT THE HEURISTIC. On recorded settlements, compute settle.mjs causalOrder (stage + shared term) and the derivation-DAG order. Prediction: the DAG order is a strict sub-relation (every DAG edge is a heuristic edge, not conversely), orderingFraction drops, and a horizon query (HoleRegion relative to a protocell's readout) returns ⊥ for exactly the cells no derivation reaches. Falsified if any derivation edge is missing from the heuristic order (would indicate the heuristic is unsound, not merely loose) or if the horizon query returns ⊥ for a reachable cell.
- ALIVENESS = NON-RECURRENCE. Run plain generate.mjs/closureOrbit vs the decorated-sofic emitter with decay for 10^4 periods. Prediction: the plain orbit becomes eventually constant within rules.length+1 stages (crystal); the living soup's row sequence has no period ≤ 10^3 (refutePeriod from cut-and-project.mjs on the emitted gap word returns a witness for every tested period) while every emitted row passes sofic.path admissibility. Falsified if the living soup's rows become periodic or fail admissibility more than 0 times.
- FOAM LAW TEST (metaphor → mechanism or discard). For protocells in a soup, record n = number of distinct neighbours on contested films and the change in extent size per period. Prediction to test, not assume: d|extent|/dt depends on n (von Neumann port) more than on extent size or total witness mass (partial correlation). Falsified (and the foam vocabulary demoted to metaphor) if n explains < 10% of variance across 500 protocells × 100 periods.
- TWO-HAND ASSAY CATCHES INJECTION. Inject k false claims (for- and against-) into a protocell's incoming messages; run the left (law) and right (record) hands and the lenticle census. Prediction: census H⁰_⊤ = k exactly for claims that contradict held cells, and injected claims outside the protocell's Galois closure are refused by delta (count = injections not in closeAttrs(profile)). Falsified if the census over- or under-counts.
- LANGUAGE BENCHMARK WITH A REAL BASELINE. RelConLang masked-object prediction (speak.mjs evaluateSaturation, held-out features collected AFTER the split), 5 candidates, ≥ 5 seeds, compared against per-predicate frequency and a 2-layer n-gram. Prediction: protocell exchange with delta concept transport beats frequency by ≥ 10 points only once the corpus exceeds ~500 lines; at 42 lines it ties (as today). Falsified if no corpus size shows separation, in which case the FCA-transport claim for language is dropped.

## Theory improvements

- NEW DEFINITION (FCA + CHL): an AUTOCATALYTIC THEORY over a Fitting RuleSystem S with food F and decay D_τ is a rule subset R ⊆ S such that every r ∈ R is catalyzed by a cell in Cl_R(F) that is itself derived, and all antecedents are reachable from F. Lean target (Obsmetrologia/Closure/RuleSystem.lean): maxRAF(F, S) exists, is unique, and the map S ↦ maxRAF is an interior operator (deflationary, monotone, idempotent) — the dual of the settling closure.
- NEW THEOREM TARGET (dynamics): with decay the deficit is not Lyapunov; prove the balance law deficit_{t+1} − deficit_t = decay_t − flux_t (extending stepFlux_decomposition/ledger_step in FreeEnergyLyapunov.lean with a source term) and the persistence criterion liminf (flux − decay) ≥ 0 ⇔ the orbit is bounded away from ⊥ on the RAF core.
- NEW THEOREM TARGET (CRN on rules): for a RuleSystem, define the stoichiometric matrix N; prove T-invariants (right kernel) are exactly the regenerating closure cycles and that a nonempty RAF has a T-invariant with Perron root ≥ 1 on its positive part. Realizes the 2026-07-25 CRN note's λ=1/λ>1/λ<1 trichotomy on SignChain instead of prose.
- CHL repair: make derivation terms {rule, from} the morphisms. Define Hom(Γ, c) as the set of derivation DAGs; prove composition (composeRule) is associative up to content-address equality and that identity/projection/converse laws (converse∘converse = id) hold, giving a genuine category; state and prove typing soundness HasTy Γ t A → propOfTy v φ A (missing today) and Church–Rosser for the rule calculus (CurryHowardLambek.lean has it only for STLC). Then 'typing judgment' stops meaning reachability.
- FCA relaxation of restrictions (2) and (4) of the 2026-05-02 'five restrictions' note: a protocell family is a family of formal contexts with Chu maps between them (metrologic chu.mjs adjoint pairs); prove delta (Galois transfer) is a knowledge-order nucleus whose Fix is exactly the concept extents (membrane theorem), that sigma∘delta is a nucleus iff sigma and delta commute (NucleusAndCommutingNuclei.lean composite theorem), and measure/prove the commutation that commutation.test.mjs only samples.
- FCA + sheaves: fission = concept-lattice refinement, fusion = concept join; prove that the glued valuation of two protocells is the concept generated by the union of extents iff the overlap's lenticle census is zero (H⁰_⊤ = 0), connecting pairwiseLenticleCensus_eq_h0top with glue_unique.
- LIGHT CONES: replace settle.mjs's term-adjacency causal order by the derivation-DAG order; prove it is a sub-order of the heuristic one; prove the horizon theorem for FOUR-valued valuations (cells outside the past cone of the readout are ⊥) by porting BlackHoles/Hole.lean HoleRegion from Bool to Reading; generalize LightCone.lean from Bool bits to Record counts (time = pos+neg, space = pos−neg) so kle_iff_lightcone holds for concentrations.
- TILES: formalize the derivation strip as a Wang row transducer (tile = (r_t, r_{t+1}, rule), horizontal colour = premise/consequent coincidence); prove: settlement exists iff the strip tiles; strict regime iff rows are eventually constant; a nonzero residue (decay + inflow) forces a non-eventually-periodic strip (lift residue_dichotomy to valuations). Build the first actual Wang tile type in metrologic/models/tiling (currently none exists) with Jeandel–Rao's 11 tiles as a fixture.
- TILES + generation: prove a decorated-sofic-cover theorem for emission: if the protocell's concept-extension state is the decoration and the emitted claim its forgetful image, the emitted language is sofic (sofic_of_decorated) and, with a primitive substitution planner (Fibonacci/Padovan matrices), zero-entropy aperiodic; bound realized bits per bead between 0 and log₂(Perron) via substitutionCoverSFT/sftEntropy.
- BEADS: define the two hands as two closure operators on the same row — law (SFT admissibility, ≤t) and record (prefix-extension, ≤k) — and prove the lenticle census equals the count of contested cells of their knowledge join (definite_joinK_inconsistency_iff_disagreement lifted to rows); prove a thread pass is legal iff the molecule enters and leaves a protocell on distinct ports (legal_iff_distinct), i.e. reflexive cells are seeds not sentences; connect weave-draft crossingReading to tally.mjs so a dialogue row is a tally.
- BEADS + seams: prove that closing a dialogue into a cycle (argument returning to its premise) glues iff the cover nerve of overlaps is a forest (h1_vanishes_iff_forest) and return the responsible cycle (the 2026-06-27 Problem 3 seam calculator), making 'this argument does not close' a computed signal.

## Risks

- Trivialization: in monotone logic RAF = derivability and every soup crystallizes in ≤ |R|+1 stages; the whole vision stands on adding witness decay, which breaks the proved monotone theorems (deficit Lyapunov, bounded κ, H1 = 0) and may make the Lean library's strongest results inapplicable in the living regime until the balance laws are proved.
- Heat death by contradiction: with decay and exchange, protocells may drift to all-⊤ (every film contested) and die, or to all-⊥; there is no evidence the parameter window (τ, T, p) where theories persist is non-empty for anything larger than toy soups.
- Exponential cores: dgBasis (2^min(|A|,20)), concept enumeration, dense BigInt rank in cellularSheafCohomology, O(n^3) Jacobi in complex.mjs, O(|v|) Map copies per tell, O(history) rescans in pam.valuation. Nothing in the maps scales past tens of attributes; per-protocell locality is the only mitigation and is unproven.
- Propositional only: ground Fitting rules give Datalog power; no variables or unification; unseen columns read ⊥. Generalization must come from FCA concept transfer, which is a 1-step neighbourhood today and has never beaten a frequency baseline.
- Three code islands (Python foam, JS SignStuff, Lean) never call each other; Holofoam is a boids model misnamed as foam; building on 'foam' vocabulary risks inheriting metaphors that pymetrologic's own settling experiment partly falsified (T1 avalanche failure).
- Identity slop will propagate: three incompatible 'cell' notions, three 'claim' notions, two cell-set implementations, four FCA implementations, and 'sign.cell' frozen into stored addresses; any kernel built before unification inherits the collisions permanently.
- Grandiose naming over trivial computation is endemic (potentialAperiodicArticulationCell = a fixed pair; 'Laplacian' = the closure; 'H-theorem' = monotone height; receipts returning literal true); a new AI narrative on top of unrenamed slop will cite theorems that do not exist (as the 2026-10-02 note already does with Autopoiesis.history_separation_aperiodicity).
- closureOrbit's once-per-rule `fired` guard and kernel.mjs's never-re-induced basis mean the existing engines cannot metabolize or learn as written; both must change, and the lfp claim ('each rule has one derivation') is not a theorem for the decayed system.
- No perceptual grounding, no natural-language competence, no benchmark at scale: the honest deliverable is a verifiable, alive, symbolic soup on a conlang, not a replacement for LLMs; overselling it as 'the next-generation language model' is the same slop the AGENTS.md warns against.
- Selection without a fitness function may still be selection toward triviality: theories that persist may be those that assert little (small extents, few films) rather than those that know much; a persistence criterion needs a knowledge-content term (deficit reduced per unit flux) or the soup will evolve inert crystals.
