# Logical inference model survey: metrologic-math

## Summary

Metrologic (data-layer math, ESM, exact integers/BigInt, no floats) is a mixed bag. A genuine mathematical core exists and is the part to build on: (1) the FCA kernel closure/formal-context.mjs (real Galois connection, attribute closure, Duquenne-Guigues canonical basis via Ganter Next Closure, with a denial lane M + not-M and a 'lawfulBasis' that drops implications with unrealized premises so a contradicting seed does not explode); (2) the finite Lorentzian causal-set suite causal.mjs (+ causal-*.mjs): transitive closure with closed-timelike-curve obstruction, Alexandrov intervals, past layers, Mobius function of the incidence algebra, Benincasa-Dowker-Glaser d'Alembertian with the correct hardcoded d=2 (1,-2,1) and d=4 (1,-9,16,-8) layer coefficients, Hall sums, Myrheim-Meyer ordering fraction, causal cohomology/microsupport/polarization (proper colouring of causal edges = nondegenerate polarization, Liouville 1-form = coboundary), all as exact integer arithmetic; (3) a coherent finite symbolic-dynamics stack: sofic.mjs (SFT = adjacency matrix, admissible word = walk, sofic = 1-block factor, golden-mean and decorated Fibonacci), substitution/index.mjs (substitution rules, orbits, abelianization, primitive/Perron, Smith-normal-form colimit invariant, gauge duality), substitution/mld.mjs (MLD = equality of the Anderson-Putnam-style invariant), substitution/hull.mjs (finite inverse systems/odometer), substitution/sofic.mjs (1-block follower SFT cover + entropy), substitution/tiling.mjs + coloring.mjs (2D block substitution, tile adjacency graph, exact backtracking chromatic number, factor-to-palette), jeandel-rao-witness.mjs (one-vertex AP cochain complexes, delta1, H1 rank, pullback tower), aperiodic-autocorrelation.mjs (finite Patterson autocorrelation, homometry); (4) chu.mjs (Chu spaces over FOUR with transpose, dual=transpose+negate, adjoint-pair Chu maps, with/plus, exhaustive law receipt), laplacian.mjs (exact Z Laplacian, harmonic judgment, component count by rank deficiency, Matrix-Tree, Hodge), persistent-h0.mjs, lambek-calculus.mjs/residuated-lattice.mjs (finite table law checkers).

Most of the rest is 'executable shadows' of Lean modules (RelMath.*): finite receipts that exhaustively check laws on a hardcoded tiny example (diamond causal set, 2-element Boolean lattice, 4-event chains) and return a frozen object full of constant 'true' flags (e.g. receipt() returns holeIsKBot:true after throwing if false). These are regression witnesses, not algorithms that take a data stream and infer. Nothing here learns, scores, or predicts; there is no probabilistic or gradient component, no dataset ingestion, no search over tilings. Naming is grandiose in several places (proof-foam, weave-draft, chromatic-marking, fca-lambek-circle).

Specific verdicts on the flagged files. proof-foam.mjs (122 lines) is NAMING OVER TRIVIA: no foam physics, no cells, no pores geometry. responseConstraintPair counts set differences (deficiency = #required opponent moves not admitted; redundancy = max(0,|players|-|required|)) of design/transitionKey strings from supplier-demanded/delivered/refused event logs; proofIncarnation partitions an event log into material vs ballast by whether a verifier-class transition (assay-admitted/refused/obstructed) visited the candidate; observeProofKinetics computes (settled.observedAt - demanded.observedAt) ms divided by an observation window as a reduced BigInt fraction ('proofing Deborah number'), 'permeable' if <=1 else 'kinetic-obstruction'. It is a log-analytics function over a supplier/verifier acceptance workflow, with a Deborah-number metaphor. weave-draft.mjs (130 lines) is bead/weave in name only: it models PLAIN weave only, the 2-colour complete graph [[F,T],[T,F]] as a sofic graph; a warp word is an over/under bit string, noFloat = alternation, crossing (warpOver,weftOver) maps to a FOUR reading via readingOfSides (hole F,F=absurd, T,T=stable/inconsistent), row complement, entropyCollapse says every valid swatch has only 2 distinct rows. It is the 1-bit alternating shift, trivially periodic; no twill/satin, no aperiodic drafts, no bead patterns, no two-hand structure. Also internal slop: the receipt flag 'mapNotInvolutive' actually asserts complementRow IS involutive. models/tilings/chromatic-marking.mjs (193 lines) is NOT tile colouring: it propagates symbolic 'colour' marks to the closed causal future of an event in a causal set (per-colour upward closure via settleCausalSet) and reports a 'spacelike frontier' = colours that exist but have not reached the event; it checks idempotence and monotonicity and agrees with chromatic-lorentzian.settle (which is merely a prefix-OR on a boolean array, called 'past-closure'). Real tile colouring (proper colouring of tile adjacency, factor map to palette) lives in coloring.mjs + substitution/tiling.mjs. The header says 'analysis/chromatic-marking' but file sits in models/tilings: location/header mismatch. Mathematically it is the Alexandrov upset/sheaf-of-sets-over-poset of marks, which IS the right shape for 'light-cone propagation of labels' and is a good hook. causal.mjs is the real light-cone math (substantial, 1169 lines, but monolithic: ~30 receipts, many hardcoded to diamondCausalSet). fca-lambek-circle.mjs (213 lines): finiteFcaContext + formalConceptAnalysisReceipt (brute-force checks of prime adjunction, closure extensive/monotone/idempotent, concept meet/join closure, implication characterization over ALL 2^n subsets, MAX_CONTEXT_SIZE 16) are real but exponential FCA; circle(context, residuated) is the 'circle' and is slop: it checks (a) the FCA closure laws on the context, (b) residuated-lattice laws on an UNRELATED table, (c) Lambek laws on the same table with lres/rres derived by swapping imp args, and (d) commutative collapse. The residuated lattice is never constructed from the concept lattice (no residuation on concept extents, no Dedekind-MacNeille/quantale structure on the context); the three are conjoined, not composed, and the Wurm/Pentus closing edge is 'documented-boundary' i.e. not done. Kernel FCA (formal-context.mjs) and the fca-lambek-circle FCA are duplicated implementations (the latter reuses kernel intent/extent only).

## Abstractions

### Formal context + Duquenne-Guigues basis (the FCA kernel)

- **File:** `lib/emsenn/services/561-group/services/engineering-services-section/services/computer-engineering-services-section/services/computer-data-engineering-services-section/lib/metrologic/src/closure/formal-context.mjs`
- **Computes:** makeContext(objects, attributes, incidencePairs, denialPairs) -> ctx{G,M,A,I}; intent(ctx,Aset), extent(ctx,Bset), closeAttrs (B''), impClosure(implications,X) (least fixpoint via shared 'settlement' node), dgBasis(ctx) (Ganter Next Closure in lectic order, canonical stem base), lawfulBasis(ctx) (drops implications with empty-extent premises). Denial lane doubles attributes to M + not-M so one closure propagates both (penguin -> not-flies). Worst-case exponential in |A| (guard 2^min(|A|,20)).
- **Theory:** Real FCA: Galois connection (G,M,I), closure operator, canonical implication basis. The denial lane is standard dichotomization (attribute negation). lawfulBasis is a sensible paraconsistent fix (avoid ex falso on empty extent). Genuinely realizes the theory; no concept-lattice enumeration, no stability/iceberg, no attribute exploration with an expert oracle, no incremental update.

- **File:** `lib/emsenn/services/561-group/services/engineering-services-section/services/computer-engineering-services-section/services/computer-data-engineering-services-section/lib/metrologic/src/articulation/predicating/positing/settling/analysis/fca-lambek-circle.mjs`

- **File:** `lib/emsenn/services/561-group/services/engineering-services-section/services/computer-engineering-services-section/services/computer-data-engineering-services-section/lib/metrologic/src/articulation/predicating/positing/settling/analysis/lambek-calculus.mjs`

## Capabilities a logical inference model can build on

- Exact FCA kernel (formal-context.mjs): contexts with a denial lane, closure, Duquenne-Guigues implication basis, and the implication closure as the saturation operator; a predication model could be 'learned' as the canonical basis of an evidence context (attribute exploration without a float).
- Chu spaces over FOUR with adjoint-pair morphisms, dual, with, plus, and truth-dominance frontier: a ready many-valued context/duality category; FCA contexts and Galois connections are Chu objects and morphisms.
- Finite causal-set machinery with Mobius inversion, BDG d'Alembertian, Alexandrov intervals, cone microsupport (closed future), polarization = proper colouring of causal edges, and cochain checks: a usable substrate for 'past cone = premises, future cone = consequences' where events are claims.
- settleChromaticMarking: per-colour upward-closure of labels on a causal set with a spacelike frontier (what exists but has not yet reached me), obstruction on closed timelike curves: a ready propagation semantic for typed claims.
- knowledge-order fixpoint dynamics (flow.mjs): monotone reading operators on FOUR^cells with a height potential (H-theorem), plus impClosure through the shared settlement node; this is the settle loop of an inference model.
- Tile/shift stack: substitution rules, abelianization to incidence matrices, Smith-normal-form colimit invariant, 1-block SFT cover with entropy, sofic factor images, 2D block substitution with orthogonal/triangular/king tile-adjacency graphs, exact chromatic number and colouring-as-factor-map: a working finite pipeline from a substitution to a coloured patch to a shift entropy.
- Exact Laplacian/Hodge/Matrix-Tree over Z and persistent H0: exact diffusion and connectivity on a cell graph without floating point.
- finite Lambek/residuated-lattice law checkers (tables) usable as acceptance tests for any candidate implication/fusion operation, including those built from concept lattices.
- Patterson autocorrelation and homometry on finite cyclic Boolean patterns: a genuine bead-pattern invariant (distinct patterns with the same autocorrelation are indistinguishable by pair statistics).
- Hardened witness/obstruction idiom (kind:'...:witness' vs '...-obstruction') with named reasons and offending items throughout: results are inspectable, composable, and suitable as typed proof-ish objects.

## Gaps and slop

- No learning, inference, or search in the area: every module is a checker or a transform over a fixed hardcoded example (diamond causal set, 2-element Boolean lattice, 4-cell chain). Receipts return constant true flags after throwing on failure; they are regression witnesses, not computed results.
- fca-lambek-circle.circle() does not build the circle: the residuated lattice is supplied independently of the formal context and is never derived from the concept lattice (no residuation of concept extents, no Dedekind-MacNeille completion, no quantale of concepts). fcaClosureIsSettlingClosure:true is a literal constant. Wurm/Pentus completeness is a 'documented-boundary'.
- No Curry-Howard-Lambek code: lambek-calculus.mjs is algebraic semantics only (tables). There is no sequent calculus, proof terms, typed lambda calculus, cut elimination, or functorial semantics from proofs to the residuated/Chu model. The hyperdoctrine is in a different module outside this area.
- proof-foam.mjs has nothing to do with foam physics: it is a flat log summarizer (deficiency/redundancy counts, material/ballast partition, glue time / window ratio). 'Foam', 'pore', 'incarnation', 'proofing Deborah' are metaphors over set differences and a division. Hardcoded transition names (supplier-demanded, supplier-delivered, assay-admitted...).
- weave-draft.mjs is the 2-colour alternating bit shift (plain weave) with a FOUR labelling. No bead weaving, no twill/satin drafts, no aperiodic or Fibonacci drafts, no two-hand/over-under pair structure beyond a bit pair; entropyCollapse is the statement that valid swatches have two rows. Internal flag mapNotInvolutive tests that complementRow IS involutive (name contradicts content). warpAlt duplicated in port-graph.mjs.
- chromatic-marking.mjs is mislabeled as tile colouring: it is per-colour upward closure on a causal set, filed under models/tilings with header 'analysis/chromatic-marking'. chromatic-lorentzian.settle is a prefix-OR called 'past-closure'. The true tile colouring (coloring.mjs + substitution/tiling.mjs) is only on rectangular block substitutions.
- Four-way duplication of the same mathematics: Boolean law-checkers are re-implemented per module (sameSet/subsetOf/intersection in fca-lambek-circle vs formal-context; graphHom in both coloring.mjs and sofic.mjs and causal-polarization.mjs; warpAlt in two places; linkKey/directLinkSet in several causal files). FCA exists in two disjoint implementations (formal-context.mjs kernel and finiteFcaContext in fca-lambek-circle).
- Exponential brute force presented as theorem checks: formalConceptAnalysisReceipt enumerates all 2^|G| and 2^|M| sets (MAX_CONTEXT_SIZE 16); chromaticNumber is backtracking; chainCountFromClosure is recursive exponential; dgBasis guards at 2^20. Fine as tests, unusable on real corpora.
- causal.mjs is a 1169-line monolith with ~30 hardcoded-to-diamond receipts and physics names (einsteinHilbert, lambekRicci, sprinklingAverage, myrheimMeyerRealShadow, benincasaDowker) that are small exact sums on 4 events; the Lambek-curvature and Ricci 'tensor' (antisymmetric, diagonal-vanishing) are labelled constructs without a demonstrated geometric meaning.
- Microlocal vocabulary (wavefront, bicharacteristic, involutive, Liouville) over finite cochains is analogy, not microlocal analysis; coboundary/cocycle/upset closure is all that is computed.
- Jeandel-Rao witness is transcribed constants from a Lean file: no Wang tile type, no tiling solver, no aperiodicity proof; the module disclaims JR aperiodicity itself. aperiodic-classification.mjs (695 lines) is largely a catalogue of regime tags and receipts of Lean statements.
- MLD here is invariant equality (a necessary condition): equal Smith forms do not imply MLD tilings. The inverse hull is finite-depth cylinders, not a Cantor hull. The substitution sofic cover is the trivial identity-decorated 1-block SFT; the genuine decorated cover (Mozes/Goodman-Strauss) is not built.
- No light-cone-as-logic bridge actually wired: no code maps a causal set to premises/consequences with FOUR-valued claims through chu.mjs or formal-context.mjs (except causal-microsupport emit with readings). No FCA<->Chu bridge, no sheaf of formal contexts over a causal poset.
- README.md covers none of the FCA/tile/cone/weave modules and describes the package as three families; most of the analysis modules are reachable only through long subpath exports; several imports mix three styles (relative, package subpath, sibling package names like @red-cup-engineering/computer-data-engineering-services-section/four), which looks fragile.
- Everything is exact-integer/rational with no probability or floats: this is good for proof receipts but means no graded similarity, no Boltzmann/foam energy minimisation, no gradient or relaxation with real numbers; the Laplacian cannot do eigendecomposition numerically.

## Attachment points

- Claims as events of a causal set: build a causal set whose events are SignCell claims (link = 'derived-from'), use settleCausalSet for acyclicity (closed-timelike-curve obstruction = circular justification), past cone via closure = premises, closed future = consequences, and settleChromaticMarking for propagating typed corrections/rulings; the spacelike frontier = unrelated-yet-known evidence (concurrent claims, merge points like the diamond top).
- FCA-as-learning: treat a stream of evidence rows as a formal context with a denial lane; maintain dgBasis/lawfulBasis incrementally as the model's 'weights' (the implication list IS the learned rule set); prediction = impClosure of a seed; 'surprise' = a row that violates a basis implication (new pseudo-intent). Connect each row to the Chu space of the same matrix (chuSpaceOfMatrix) so contexts compose by Chu maps (adjoint pairs) and negation by dual.
- Close the circle for real: derive a residuated structure on the concept lattice (concepts as a quantale with product from a context relation, or implication A->B as the concept of objects mapping A-extents into B), feed it to lambek-calculus.laws as a test oracle, and add a proof-term layer (typed lambda terms for Lambek derivations) so CHL has both sides; use fca-lambek-circle.circle only as the acceptance test.
- Tile colouring as typing: use coloring.properColoring on gridAdjacency of a tilingPatch to assign each tile a colour from a palette of claim types, then the coloring factor map is the type-assignment; sofic.soficImage checks that word-level labels are an admissible 1-block image of an SFT (matching rules = typing rules). Entropy via substitutionCoverSFT/sftEntropy bounds the information content of the label stream.
- Weave a bead-string as a two-hand pair: replace the plain-weave bit-pair with a sofic graph from a substitution (Fibonacci via fibSubstitute/decoratedGoldenGraph) so a bead sequence is a path of the aperiodic sofic shift; use aperiodic-autocorrelation.homometric as the observable-equivalence test between two bead patterns and mldInvariant as the substitution-level equivalence; keep the two-hand structure as the (warp,weft) pair in crossingReading with FOUR deciding conflict.
- Foam as relaxation on a cell graph: use laplacian.mjs (exact L=D-W, harmonic judgment, Hodge) plus flow.mjs knowledge-order operators for a pure-integer relaxation step; define pore = cell with a local residual (obstruction), foam coarsening = merge of cells along persistent-h0 (components merge only), Deborah number from proof-foam.observeProofKinetics as the time-scale gate between 'permeable' and 'kinetic-obstruction'. Plateau-like vertex rules can be encoded as a SFT/sofic matching rule on the cell graph.
- Replace proof-foam's flat log summarizer with a true cell structure: events -> cells of a cell-complex (transfinite-cell-complex.cellComplex with face dimension drop), deficiency/redundancy as the two signed parts of a coboundary (computed with jeandel-rao-style delta1/isCocycle), H1 rank as number of independent unresolved disputes.
- Chu-valued sheaves on the causal poset: assign a Chu space over FOUR to every event and a Chu map along every link; settle by sections that are consistent on intervals (Alexandrov topology receipts already exist); the spacelike frontier is where sections are not yet glued.
- Use Mobius inversion on the causal poset as the credit-assignment primitive: causalMobius/dalembertianStrict give exact inclusion-exclusion of influence of past claims on an event (what each premise contributes after removing overlap), and bdCoeff4 gives a local curvature that detects where the dependency graph departs from a manifold-like (tree-like) shape.
- Make a small trainable layer without floats: parameters = implication sets (dgBasis), a sofic graph (SFT transition matrix), a substitution rule, or a colouring palette; loss = exact counts (obstruction/cardinality), update = add/remove one implication or transition (Next-Closure step), search = lectic order or backtracking in coloring.properColoring; use settle with a Lyapunov height (flow.ledgerCellStep) as the convergence certificate.
- Unify duplicate kernels first: one set-algebra helper, one graph-hom, one linkKey, one FCA implementation; move chromatic-marking into causal-label propagation; rename weave-draft to plain-weave-parity and port-graph.warpAlt to a shared import before the new AI cites them.
