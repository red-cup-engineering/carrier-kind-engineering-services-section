# Leader-supplied free compute and inference catalog and prior-research recovery direction

## Status and purpose

This note preserves a resource catalog supplied by the Leader and gives a
non-binding operational reading for later verification and implementation.
Every provider, model, quota, price, availability, and permitted-use claim in
the catalog is **Leader-supplied and unverified as of 2026-07-17**. No catalog
entry is adopted here as a fact, provider offer, contract, service-level
commitment, resource grant, credential, witnessed capacity, or authorization
to execute.

The intended business direction is to recover value from prior research that
the conglomerate has already paid to produce but cannot presently retrieve and
reuse reliably. The proposed provider is the existing corpus-management-team,
enriched to furnish conglomerate-wide prior-research recovery through explicit
contracts with each sovereign micro-enterprise whose informational territory
is involved. Koios News is the proposed near-term customer-value circuit: its
observed editorial demand can ask for bounded research recovery, and its
claim-accountable publishing surfaces can test whether recovered work reaches
a customer without inventing warrant.

This note does not authorize that enrichment, amend the corpus-management-team,
grant access to another enterprise, allocate provider quotas, transmit a
source, or direct Koios News to publish anything.

## What the direction means in established management terms

### The Rendanheyi relation

The proposed work begins from a customer, not from spare model calls. Koios
News has a concrete need for research that can support warranted claims,
correctable attention surfaces, and useful coverage. The relevant value is not
the count of generated artifacts. Value is created when already-held research
closes an observed Koios information gap, and value is captured only when the
result produces a separately witnessed customer or economic outcome.

The corpus-management-team would remain a provider micro-enterprise. It would
not become a capital above the conglomerate or the owner of every enterprise's
content. Each source micro-enterprise would retain its customer relation,
territory, books, authority, and right of refusal. Conglomerate-wide reach
would mean that the team can solicit lawful access across many borders, not
that an ancestor path or shared operator account gives it universal access.

### The Huidu Guanli relation

The corpus pile is grey material: valuable research, duplicates, abandoned
drafts, contradictory readings, historical projections, private material,
implemented findings, and open hypotheses coexist without one global status.
The corpus-management-team's service should form a bounded grey hull around
one customer question. It should derive what is present, what overlaps, what
is current, what conflicts, what is inaccessible, and what is missing without
forcing all of those conditions into a false binary judgment.

Cheap inference can assist observation, comparison, classification, and gap
description. It does not decide authority, source truth, publication, or
whether two conflicting records should be identified. Refusal and unresolved
contradiction are valid service outcomes.

## Proposed service: conglomerate-wide prior-research recovery

The smallest faithful service would accept one bounded recovery request and
return one contextual bundle plus an explicit gap bundle.

### Preconditions

- Koios News or another identified customer supplies one concrete information
  demand and the decision or publication boundary the research must support.
- The requested informational territories and source classes are named.
- Each sovereign source micro-enterprise is approached through its own
  admission interface and may offer terms, ask a question, or refuse.
- Every external inference or compute account is represented by an exact
  resource grant. A credential is never copied into the request or record.
- Any source egress is separately identified and granted for the exact source,
  provider, purpose, and bounded encounter.

### Move

1. Deterministically inventory the admitted source set and preserve source
   identifiers, owning settlements, paths, byte digests, dates, and declared
   status before inference begins.
2. Derive a source and reference graph without moving or rewriting the source
   records.
3. Form a contextual bundle containing the smallest source excerpts and
   references needed to answer the customer question.
4. Form a gap bundle naming missing evidence, inaccessible territories,
   unresolved contradictions, stale references, and questions that require a
   human or owning-enterprise decision.
5. Obtain specialist work from existing characters, offices, duties, and
   skills through their lawful projections and contracts. Do not invent a
   persona or prompt where an existing office already carries the duty.
6. Route eligible inference from the cheapest verified and authorized tier,
   while preserving provider, model, surface, source-egress scope, observed
   usage, wall time, refusal, and any available monetary rate.
7. Return the contextual and gap bundles to the customer with exact source
   bindings and provider-attributed readings. Generated summaries remain
   derivative evidence, never replacement source records.

### Postconditions

- The customer can locate and reuse relevant prior research without rerunning
  an unbounded search of the corpus.
- Every recovered claim points to exact admitted source evidence.
- Every gap or refusal remains visible rather than being filled with plausible
  prose.
- Each source enterprise and service provider has written only its own ingress,
  decision, execution, outcome, and receipt records.
- Costs, customer value, and any revenue remain separate observations.
- No publication occurs merely because a contextual bundle exists.

## Koios News as the near-term customer-value circuit

Koios can supply real demand through its desks, source wires, coverage gaps,
correction state, and accountable-attention surfaces. A bounded pilot should
begin with one Koios assignment whose supporting research is believed to exist
but is difficult to recover.

The recovery service would return source-bound claims and gaps. Koios would
apply its own editorial authority, provenance, modality, harm, publication,
and correction gates. Only then could it project an article, headline, feed
item, card, JSON-LD record, or other circulation surface. Aggregate attention
and provider-settled revenue may later supply customer and economic evidence;
they may not be manufactured, inferred from publication, or replaced with
synthetic traffic.

This makes the work cost recovery rather than content production: the primary
deliverable is a reusable map from an existing customer question to research
already owned, its evidence status, and its gaps. New prose is incidental and
disposable. Recovery is not economically witnessed until a later encounter
shows avoided procurement, customer value, or captured revenue in a lawful
denomination.

## Resource-routing and compliance boundaries

The following constraints govern any later verification or implementation:

- **Availability is not authorization.** A reachable endpoint, installed CLI,
  environment variable, account session, or cloud console does not grant an
  enterprise permission to use it.
- **An advertised free tier is not witnessed capacity.** Provider terms,
  model availability, rate limits, quotas, geography, data handling,
  commercial-use permission, and account eligibility must be rechecked at the
  time of the proposed engagement.
- **Accounts and keys remain resource grants.** They remain in the supplying
  person's or enterprise's custody and cross a settlement boundary only as an
  attenuated capability. Credentials never enter packets, traces, bundles, or
  source records.
- **No quota rotation or evasion.** The catalog's phrases "route traffic
  around individual provider bottlenecks," "without key exhaustion," and
  "API-key rotation arrays" are preserved Leader-supplied proposals, not
  adopted practice. No implementation may rotate projects, keys, accounts,
  identities, or endpoints to evade a provider limit, suspension, price, or
  intended free-tier boundary.
- **No compliance gaming.** The proposed image use "to pass manual ad network
  compliance" is preserved as source wording and explicitly not adopted.
  Visuals must serve an independently warranted editorial or customer purpose;
  they must not be generated to deceive a reviewer, inflate apparent quality,
  or bypass an advertising policy.
- **No mass-production assumption.** Bulk text, hyper-local target pages, and
  "2,000 blog posts" are preserved proposals, not authorized workloads.
  Koios's warrant, provenance, correction, editorial, and customer-value gates
  apply to every public artifact regardless of marginal generation cost.
- **Source egress must be granted.** Sending content to a local CLI backed by a
  remote provider, an API, NotebookLM, a hosted model, or an unauthenticated
  public endpoint is egress. Private, personal, contract-restricted, secret,
  or sovereign source material remains local unless the owning authority grants
  that exact crossing.
- **Unknown cost is not zero.** A zero advertised price does not erase human
  attention, compute, bandwidth, storage, energy, latency, quota pressure,
  privacy exposure, provider dependency, or opportunity cost. Record the
  observations available and leave unavailable measurements unknown.

## Verification program before implementation

For every catalog entry proposed for use, verification should establish:

1. current provider and model identity;
2. current account eligibility and authentication state without exposing a
   credential;
3. current quota and the unit in which it is measured;
4. whether the quota is per account, organization, billing project, API key,
   model, region, or another boundary;
5. allowed commercial and automated use;
6. provider rules on rate-limit handling and multiple accounts or projects;
7. source retention, training, privacy, confidentiality, and deletion terms;
8. content, copyright, image, advertising, and acceptable-use restrictions;
9. observable token, request, compute, storage, network, and monetary costs;
10. a provider-owned executable offer or an explicit refusal under the
    consuming enterprise's exact requested effects.

Verification should produce an evidence-backed provider observation, not edit
this preserved catalog into apparent correctness. Changed or disproved claims
remain visible as historical Leader-supplied source material alongside the
new observation.

## Implementation guidance and stopping rule

The first implementation should stop after one bounded Koios recovery
encounter. It should not begin by installing every provider, crawling every
settlement, or scheduling background generation.

Success means that one admitted customer question received a source-bound
contextual bundle and honest gap bundle, under contracts and resource grants
that can be independently replayed. The next allocation should be considered
only after the owning enterprises and the Leader can inspect source treatment,
provider behavior, costs, customer usefulness, refusals, and any captured
value.

The catalog remains a valuable hypothesis about available productive capacity.
It becomes an operating asset only one verified, authorized, customer-facing
engagement at a time.
