# Named technology peers and meet-then-beat acceptance

## 1. Purpose, authority and scope

The Group needs its existing technologies to provide familiar, useful alternatives to named software while realizing its own content-addressed, evidence-bearing organization of work. The limiting condition is not a shortage of package names: current capability placement, historical descriptions and available exports do not establish a coherent delivered system. Success is a reproducible comparison on declared common behavior, followed by demonstrated additional value for an actual Group consumer.

This specification refines section 5 of [Software stratified over one substrate](../articles/notes/2026/10/10/2026-10-10-specification-of-software-stratified-over-one-substrate.md) and its existing [R11](../../../requirements/software-composed-over-one-shared-substrate.json). That source's dependency order, pure/effectful boundary and migration requirements continue to govern. The [one-implementation requirement](../../../requirements/obsmetrological-functions-have-one-implementation.json) governs Metrologic: use the scientific owner's functions; do not recreate their mathematics in each application.

The named-target instructions for SignChain/LangChain and SignGraph/LangGraph are explicit in the current [SignChain](../../glossary/terms/signchain.md) and [SignGraph](../../glossary/terms/signgraph.md) definitions. FluffOS is an explicit FeltOS predecessor. SymPy and PySheaf are the Leader's examples in the current assignment. The remaining pairings are proposed engineering selections, not reconstructed quotations or claims that the Leader previously chose them.

Science retains the mathematical and domain theories; Engineering owns product requirements, adapters, measurements and executable receiving. Work, Mission, Endeavor, capability, obligation and acceptance must be interpreted from their existing Science owners. This specification supplies a synthetic consumer fixture; it does not define a competing work ontology or turn a package census into one. It creates no new scheduler, ledger, capability catalog, hash format or access grant.

Every acceptance item below is a proposed refinement with Computer Engineering as decision owner and the named existing application/faculty owner as realization owner. The actual consuming organization receives the result independently. No comparative benchmark has been run, no external feature absence has been demonstrated, and no superiority is claimed here.

## 3. Common comparison and evidence rules

**R11-COMMON-1 — identify the comparison.** Each run shall retain the house revision and recursive provider closure; competitor release/commit; runtime and dependency lock; adapter source; fixture identity; storage/durability settings; hardware/OS and process limits; metric boundary; raw observations; output comparison; and receiver judgment. A moving documentation page is a discovery source, not a pinned executable baseline. Pin the actual software before execution.

**R11-COMMON-2 — compare equivalent work.** Distinguish a role peer from a facet substitute. A role peer serves a comparable user task; it need not share the house architecture. A facet substitute covers only the named operation. The adapter must declare loss, extra modeling, setup, policies and missing features. Charge adapter overhead to the composed system. No silent conversion of absent information to false, contradictory evidence to a winning scalar, ordered triples to unordered maps, exact rationals to floating-point values, or attempted effects to accepted results.

**R11-COMMON-3 — meet before claiming a beat.** “Meet” requires all mandatory common semantic cases to pass, including recovery/refusal cases. Report each unsupported case as a gap. A house-only feature is a proposed differentiator, not evidence the peer cannot implement it. A behavioral “beat” requires both systems to run the extended scenario under a stated implementation budget and a measured better outcome; a peer configuration omission is not a product limitation. Never trade correctness, source custody or authorization for speed.

**R11-COMMON-4 — quantitative profile, proposed.** Freeze workload and resource limits before measurement. Use 5 process-cold observations, 10 untimed warmups, then 30 paired repetitions in alternating order on an otherwise idle equivalent machine. Each repetition processes the complete declared workload. Report setup time, p50/p95 operation latency, total elapsed time, peak resident memory, persisted bytes, logical completions, retries, refused inputs and operator interventions separately. Retain individual observations; calculate uncertainty by paired-run resampling, not by pretending correlated operations are independent trials.

For a selected lower-is-better primary measure, the proposed meet margin is house/peer ≤1.10 and beat margin ≤0.80; the upper bound of a 95% paired-bootstrap interval must meet the selected margin. All correctness obligations remain exact. These are proposed product gates, not measured results or industry standards. Computer Engineering and the consumer must accept the metric, margin and maximum resource budget before a comparative claim. Higher-is-better metrics use the explicitly stated inverse ratio. A zero denominator, timeout, failed run or unqualified machine produces an inconclusive comparison, never a win. Do not hide cold-start, compilation, indexing, checkpointing or evidence-retention costs in a warm-only aggregate.

**R11-COMMON-5 — retain evidence in existing owners.** Extend existing tests and receiving records at the module that owns the behavior. Scientific law obligations go to their Science owner; product/adapter checks stay in Engineering; operation/installation receiving stays with Operations and the real customer. The existing requirement's “conditions stated” status is not product acceptance. A prose file's presence, a passing isolated test or a merge shall not mark the competitor comparison complete.

## 4. Shared operational fixture: Group work received by capability

Fixture labels below are synthetic test labels, not new native types or authority. Resolve them through the accepted Work/Agency carriers when implementing. The addressed Group Thing contains an Endeavor and two Missions: produce an inspectable artifact, and establish that its recipient can use it. Their relationships, requirements and work are read from one admitted Semioverse cut. Content identity of a version is distinct from organizational continuity across revisions.

The first step of every Move shall consult Semioverse for the actor's current situation, obligations, relevant requirements and available capabilities. Retain the source cut and selected obligation in the existing work evidence. A stale or unavailable reading yields a specific obstruction or an explicitly permitted read-only action; it must not silently substitute a private checklist as authoritative work. Revalidate effect-relevant standing before execution. A read receipt alone does not authorize an action.

The finite necessary-work fixture has the following obligations, with all incoming dependencies conjunctive:

| Label | Required predecessor result | Capability | Produces |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| S | none | inspect source | qualified source |
| T | S | test artifact | test result |
| P | S | prepare artifact | prepared artifact |
| R | T and P | review artifact | review result |
| D | R | deliver artifact | delivery result |
| A | D | independently receive | recipient acceptance |

With nothing already supplied and every actor eligible, the necessary waves are `{S}`, `{T,P}`, `{R}`, `{D}`, `{A}`. If the qualified source is already supplied, omit S. If P is already supplied, do not regenerate it. If T and P are both supplied, prune their satisfied ancestry and return only `{R}`, `{D}`, `{A}`. The receiving mission still needs A: D is not completion. Give the tester only test capability, the preparer only prepare capability and the receiver only receive capability; their views must be the corresponding eligible slice, while preserving blocked obligations and explanations. Capability, authority, locality/resource availability and predecessor satisfaction are separate predicates.

For the mutation cases, “causal frontier” means the next unsatisfied obligations whose conjunctive predecessors are in the fixture's admitted-result set. “Eligible frontier” further filters those obligations by the fixture's capability, current authority, resources and effect-reconciliation guards. These are explicit finite adapter assumptions, not replacement Work definitions. Restore the stated starting state before each case; do not apply the rows cumulatively.

Retain historical runs, output and assessments: losing access to fetched evidence does not establish that a historical event vanished. Whether that evidence still discharges a revised requirement needs an explicit receiving/reuse law. Engineering must bind the accepted owner revision rather than silently turn a contested knowledge value into a retraction or infer permanent stability from no challenge being present now.

The three blocked rows are named requirements for further refinement, not executable acceptance claims. A comparison cannot report the shared mutation suite passed while any remains blocked. All other rows become executable only after binding the actual adapters and accepted owner carriers. Unknown effect outcomes require reconciliation; no test may infer acceptance from an attempted or merely duplicated delivery.

Use this fixture through all eight layers. Keep causal necessity separate from scheduling policy: a total priority list cannot prove dependence, a chosen proof path need not be the only sufficient path, and a ready set depends on currently admitted resources and permissions. Science must settle the formal interpretation where required; a graph traversal cannot decide it by itself.

## 5. Per-technology requirements and named software

### 5.1 MetroPlat: Apache OFBiz, with Temporal and OPA facets

**Own role and consumer:** the situated enterprise user experience over FeltOS and selected upstream operations, consumed by an actual organization. The platform/instance contract, not the current large operation catalog, defines the boundary.

**Primary evidence:** [Apache OFBiz](https://ofbiz.apache.org/) documents a common enterprise architecture with entity, service and UI engines and business applications. It is a role comparison for the composed enterprise experience, not a mandate to copy accounting or inventory into MetroPlat. [Temporal Activities](https://docs.temporal.io/activities) documents worker/task-queue execution and retries and recommends idempotent activities. [OPA](https://www.openpolicyagent.org/docs) separates structured policy decisions from enforcement. Temporal and OPA are facet substitutes, not full MetroPlat peers.

- **R11-MP-M1 (meet):** Two synthetic enterprise installations shall expose only their selected operations and corresponding resource/work views. One authorized artifact request shall remain discoverable through submission, pending, refusal, delivery and independent acceptance. Exercise 20 interleaved requests per instance with two actors; require zero cross-instance results, zero unauthorized effects and exact agreement between the selected-operation contract and all presented entrances. Compare OFBiz with a bounded service/UI adapter implementing the same artifact-request task; separately record built-in versus adapter behavior.
- **R11-MP-M2 (meet, durability facet):** Run fixture D with a receiver-owned idempotency key. Crash before dispatch, after receiver commit but before acknowledgement, and after recorded acknowledgement, once at each boundary. After restart and reconciliation, require one logical artifact and a retained distinction between attempt, effect, delivery and acceptance. Compare the composed house operation with a Temporal workflow/activity using the same mocked receiver and durability class.
- **R11-MP-B1 (proposed beat):** Through the shared fixture, every selected Move shall link the Group/Endeavor/Mission, current requirement, causal prerequisite and actor eligibility to its result. Measure successful end-to-end receipt, manual interventions and time to explain why the next work is blocked. Require 100% trace coverage and no manual recreation of work identity across web/terminal/agent views. Superiority over OFBiz/Temporal requires a paired run; merely having these fields does not establish it.

**Allocation:** MetroPlat presents and selects; FeltOS hosts; AOE Organizing owns coordinated work; Sign layers own carriers/replay; the existing authority, delivery and domain owners perform their effects. Reuse the platform's distributed-work requirements rather than introduce a second queue.

### 5.2 FeltOS: FluffOS and Evennia

**Own role:** persistent multi-agent MUD and resource environment, composed over AOE and shared programs. [FluffOS](https://github.com/fluffos/fluffos) distinguishes its driver from the mudlib that supplies rooms, commands and game behavior. [Evennia](https://www.evennia.com/docs/latest/) documents accounts, sessions, objects, rooms, exits, commands, permissions and scripts. Both are role peers for persistent worlds; neither comparison assumes full LPC/Python compatibility.

- **R11-FO-M1 (meet):** Author four rooms, three directed exits, two actors, two transferable items and one timed process. Execute the same 40-operation transcript on FeltOS, a pinned FluffOS plus named mudlib, and Evennia with a named world configuration. The transcript shall include look, travel, speech, transfer, denied traversal, disconnect/reconnect, restart and an authored exit withdrawal. Require identical specified observable locations, item custody and visible messages, with no lost admitted state and no travel through the withdrawn exit. Record engine and world code separately.
- **R11-FO-M2 (meet):** At 1, 10 and 100 concurrent synthetic sessions, issue the same deterministic round-robin command workload, 100 commands per session. Measure admitted-command latency and restart recovery separately. Timed progress must depend on the admitted clock, not on how many clients send look commands. Freeze durability settings before comparison; a current-state snapshot is not automatically historical replay.
- **R11-FO-B1 (proposed beat):** Carry one addressed artifact request from an actor in world one to an actor in world two, including one disconnect and one repeated message. Terminal, web and agent entrances shall resolve the same resource revision and outcome. Withdraw the receiving actor's standing after discovery and require refusal at execution with evidence retained. Measure operator interventions and successful received results. Implement peer federation/adapters fairly before claiming an advantage.

**Allocation:** simulation and world composition remain FeltOS; network protocols and persistence primitives remain upstream. Differentiate fictional world consequences from real resource effects. Benchmark only synthetic, nonfinancial, reversible operations.

### 5.3 Agent Organization Engine: JaCaMo

**Own role:** the nine faculties' composition into organized agent activity, above SignCraft and below FeltOS. [JaCaMo](https://jacamo-lang.github.io/) and its [documentation](https://jacamo-lang.github.io/doc) integrate agent, environment and organization dimensions through Jason, CArtAgO and MOISE. For each of the six normal work transitions, require a declared actor/capability, observed precondition, produced result and accepting recipient. Add one unavailable actor and one missing capability: work shall remain allocated or blocked without inventing an eligible replacement.
- **R11-AOE-M2 (meet):** Send two conflicting observations, one duplicate message and a role withdrawal. The composed organization shall preserve who said what and evaluate current eligibility before the next effect. Compare the common role/goal behavior first; separately record any extra evidence model needed in the JaCaMo adapter.
- **R11-AOE-B1 (proposed beat):** Every actor shall obtain the correct capability slice of the shared necessary-work frontier without a human writing a private task list. Require exact eligible sets for every qualified fixture mutation; the source-withdrawal, conflict-policy and requirement-revision cases remain blocked until their owner rules and oracles are bound. ### 5.4 SignCraft: Soufflé and Automerge

**Own role:** the operating/settling system over SignCell, SignChain and SignGraph, with explicit admission, replay and rule-witness provenance. [Soufflé's tutorial](https://souffle-lang.github.io/tutorial) supplies a finite Datalog baseline; its [provenance feature](https://souffle-lang.github.io/provenance) already explains derived tuples using minimal-height proof trees. [Automerge repositories](https://automerge.org/docs/reference/repositories/) supply CRDT synchronization/storage, while [its conflict API](https://automerge.org/docs/reference/documents/conflicts/) retains concurrent values even when one is presented by default. Neither is a verified whole-system replacement.

- **R11-SC-M1 (meet, inference):** For vertices 0…n−1 with edges i→i+1, at n=10,100,1000, compute strict reachability with the same two positive Datalog rules. Require exactly n(n−1)/2 ordered reachable pairs, no self-pairs and identical query answers. Compare SignCraft and Soufflé on this declared positive fragment; charge preprocessing/compilation separately. Enable provenance in both for a second profile and validate every presented derivation against its rules and premises.
- **R11-SC-M2 (meet, replay):** Admit a fixed set of addressed observations in three replica partitions, then merge all six delivery orders with repeated deliveries. Require the same retained observation set and specified reading after restart. Compare Automerge under an explicit evidence-record model, using its conflict APIs where relevant; do not claim it simply discards conflicts.
- **R11-SC-B1 (proposed beat):** Add independent positive and negative witnesses for the same predication and an unrelated query. Preserve both witness sets without deriving the unrelated query. Produce separately source-replayed positive and negative derivations, cached-premise-pruned execution waves, and named open assumptions for the shared fixture. Require replay agreement for every presented edge and exact refusal of tampered ancestry. This is not automatically a gain over Soufflé's existing provenance or a proof of world soundness.

**Allocation:** extend the actual inference/kinematic/settle/history consumers, reusing SignChain rule identity. A finite fixpoint test does not certify infinite or transfinite regimes, and a numerical smoothing step does not silently retract knowledge.

### 5.5 SignGraph: LangGraph, with Neo4j and PySheaf facets

**Own role:** graph structures and transport for SignCells/SignChains, including the explicitly intended agent graphs, state and higher-order/Chu contracts. [LangGraph](https://docs.langchain.com/oss/python/langgraph/overview) is the direct target named in the house definition. Its [checkpointers](https://docs.langchain.com/oss/python/langgraph/checkpointers) preserve super-step state and successful pending writes; [persistent versus in-memory storage](https://docs.langchain.com/oss/python/langgraph/persistence) must be compared honestly. [Neo4j](https://neo4j.com/docs/getting-started/graph-database/) is a property-graph/query facet, not an agent-orchestration equivalent. PySheaf's mathematical facet is specified with Metrologic below.

- **R11-SG-M1 (meet, agent graph):** Execute a graph with conditional branching, two parallel branches, a join, a bounded three-iteration loop, one interrupt/resume and a nested subgraph. Use deterministic stub model/tool results. Compare outputs, state transitions and named pending work with LangGraph. Crash after one parallel branch's successful persisted write; require resume without rerunning that completed branch. Compare equivalent durable configurations, not LangGraph's in-memory example to house persistence.
- **R11-SG-M2 (meet, graph facet):** Preserve and query a directed multigraph containing parallel edges, a self-loop, distinct predicates and typed values. Compare exact neighbor/path results against Neo4j after a declared mapping. Reverse one edge: directional results and incidence must change; symmetric adjacency alone must not be accepted as the causal oracle. No whole-database performance claim follows from this facet test.
- **R11-SG-B1 (proposed beat):** Transport the shared fixture's positive and opposing evidence through two graph regions while retaining source identity and typed forward/backward demand. Verify each proposed finite Chu morphism on every point/state pair of its declared finite carrier; reject a single deliberately mismatched evaluation. Preserve both evidence lanes through interrupt and replay. Measure replayed trace fidelity and operator interventions against an equivalently modeled LangGraph adapter.

**Allocation:** SignGraph owns pure graph/transport contracts. Persistence effects and agent execution compose through SignCraft/AOE/FeltOS. Matching LangGraph's user task does not authorize moving an entire runtime into a pure graph library.

### 5.6 SignChain: LangChain, with OPA as a policy facet

**Own role:** typed code/consequence and evidence ordering over SignCell. [LangChain](https://docs.langchain.com/oss/python/langchain/overview) documents composable agent/model/tool interfaces and configurable middleware; its modern agent harness uses LangGraph. It is the explicit house comparison target. Its broader system behavior must be received through the composed house stack, not by embedding network or model effects in SignChain. [OPA](https://www.openpolicyagent.org/docs) is a policy-decision facet and can return structured outputs, not just booleans.

- **R11-SCH-M1 (meet):** Compose a three-stage inspect → transform → validate chain with typed inputs/outputs, deterministic model stubs, two tool choices, an invalid tool result, one tool exception and one bounded retry. In the composed house path and LangChain harness, require the same valid result, named failure branch, retry bound and caller-visible trace. Record the rules/transformations actually used; compare streaming or asynchronous behavior only if the receiving task requires it and the adapter specifies it.
- **R11-SCH-M2 (meet, policy):** Evaluate a finite policy table covering actor role, resource scope, current grant and input validity. Let `allow = role AND scope AND grant AND valid`; enumerate all 16 Boolean combinations and compare the decision plus the ordered list of false predicate names against OPA/Rego. Decision and enforcement are separate: the actual effect owner must enforce the accepted result, and this test grants no permissions.
- **R11-SCH-B1 (proposed beat):** Retain versioned rule identities and premise witnesses across successful, failed and contradicted inference chains. Adding a negative witness must leave prior positive evidence inspectable; malformed rule provenance or a type-mismatched intermediate shall refuse composition. Require all 16 policy rows and all chain outcomes to be replay-explainable without a live model call. Measure evidence loss, replay success and integration effort against the paired LangChain/OPA configuration.

**Allocation:** typed contracts/rules/order remain SignChain and shared mathematics; model calls, effects, checkpoints and organizational decisions remain their existing higher owners. LangChain itself is not a mathematical theorem prover; neither is an observed house chain a theorem certificate.

### 5.7 SignCell: RDFLib and the JavaScript IPLD libraries
