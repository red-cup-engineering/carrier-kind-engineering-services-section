# Semiotic composition — cells, chains, graphs and crafted operations

**Semiotic composition** is the umbrella term for lawful composition of already admitted semiotic material. It names a *family of typed operations*, not one magical transformation that turns arbitrary information into a useful running enterprise. No new umbrella library or orchestrator is warranted: reuse **SignCell → SignChain → SignGraph → SignCraft**, each with a precise owner and an independent admissibility law.

| Movement | Verb preferred in ordinary engineering | Owner and product | Crucial invariant or refusal |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Cellularization** | cellularize | SignCell data admission, exposed through a competent domain interpreter; witnessed triples plus original occurrence references | Keep source acts, canonical identity, direction and opposed testimony separate |
| **Semiotic chaining** | chain | SignChain composed, typed, ordered transitions/rules with recorded causal/derivational standing | A list or timestamps alone do not demonstrate valid causal composition; codomain/domain schema coincidence alone is insufficient |
| **Semiotic graphing** | graph or graphize | SignGraph vertices, oriented incidences, sites and conditional gluing of existing cells/chains | An undirected adjacency readout cannot substitute for oriented claims; graph edges are not authenticated effects; a source graph is not automatically a topological theorem |
| **Semiotic crafting** | craft, realize or fabricate | SignCraft higher-order generation, closure/settlement and qualified compositions over upstream substrate | No unproved regime lift, no fabricated permissions, no domain-specific engine inside the pure substrate |
| **Domain/system realization** | instantiate, serve, operate, apply | Computer Systems and competent downstream domain engines plus Coesite/Group/Public actual instances | Package/source admission is not running process, external recipient success or market fit |

The order expresses **typed dependencies**, not a requirement to pass every operation through all five movements. An isolated SignCell is useful by itself. A graph can relate cells without a temporal chain. A chain can be valid without an emitted graph. Higher-order fabrication may read multiple levels. No layer may silently infer authority from the layers below it.

## A reference counterexample

A system description may say operation P produces schema S and operation Q consumes schema S. Cellularization creates source-qualified assertion cells. Semiotic graphing may present a *candidate* directed edge P→Q. To obtain executable chaining, the actual producer postcondition, consumer precondition, effects, versions, authority and failure/refusal semantics must admit composition. A SignCraft/Systems realization may then expose a governed operation. Installing and invoking that operation is yet another independently observable fact. 'One matching schema' cannot replace these judgments; otherwise a candidate graph edge would counterfeit an execution proof.

Two presentations of the same cell may disagree; a graph is required to retain that contradiction rather than fork an alleged semantic vertex. The upstream [SignGraph cell-graph source](https://github.com/red-cup-engineering/signgraph/blob/main/src/cell-graph.mjs) joins witnessed SignCells by triple identity for extensional graph projection. This is not a substitute for the separately recoverable original performances described in [Articulating](https://github.com/lenticule-science/obsmetrologia/blob/main/Obsmetrologia/Articulation/Articulating.lean). A strong mathematical identification between these software constructions and Obsmetrologia's original relational derivations remains a Science obligation, not something an English verb can supply.

**Use-and-improve decision:** Before making a new "semiotic composer" or engine-local codec, determine whether the missing mechanism is general Data, Code, Networking or Systems; if it is unique to witnessed cell identity, chaining, graphing or crafting, repair its original Sign package; if it is market/domain specific, put it downstream. Delete duplicated leaf mechanisms only once the original consumer uses the upstream replacement. Prefer standards such as RFC 6901 source JSON Pointers and JSON Schema 2020-12 where their actual jobs fit; standards describe transport/validation, not the foundation of Obsmetrologia.


## A working typed composition producer, October 19, 2026

[Computer Code's native `operationSchemaTopology` and `operationSchemaMesh`](https://github.com/red-cup-engineering/computer-code-engineering-services-section/blob/main/src/operation-schema-topology.mjs) take *actual discovered operation signatures*, resolve full JSON Schema identifiers using the original schema provider, and yield **candidate** directed producer→consumer edges and type frontiers. They do not assert interface subtyping, cross-actor standing, a Hoare proof or executable installation. [Computer Systems](https://github.com/red-cup-engineering/computer-system-engine/blob/feature/architecture-signcell-projection-20261019/src/rpc/manifold.mjs) consumes these operations and makes their declared input/output schema incidences and tentative joining edges available as source-bound SignCells on its existing MCP resource. This removes a bespoke Systems join calculator; no fourth use of the word "semiocodification" is introduced.

To **semiocodify**, stronger evidence is still required: the corresponding native SignChain/Computer Code term must be admitted and executable through its rightful SignCraft/Systems consumer. A schema-equal candidate and a graph edge remain *information about possible composition*, not code or proof of successful operation.
