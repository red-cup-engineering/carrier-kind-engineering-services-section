# Cellularization — interpreting admitted information as semiotic cells

**Cellularization** is the typed, partial transformation of *admitted source information* into SignCell predication cells and a relation from those cells back to the occurrences that supplied them. The Leader's new spelling **celluralization** is treated as a provisional lexical alias, not a second kind of operation, library, type or engine. In ordinary language, **to cellularize** is to represent information as semiotic cells *using SignCell*, while retaining the difference between the information, the act of stating it, the cell's extensional identity and what the statement proves.

## Type and invariant, without reversing Obsmetrologia

For an explicitly declared source schema \(\tau\) and interpretation \(I\), describe the *engineering* projection as a partial map

\[
  \operatorname{cellularize}_{\tau,I}:S_{\tau}
  \rightharpoonup
  \bigl(O_{\tau}\times\operatorname{SignCell}\bigr)^{*}
  \longrightarrow \operatorname{CellSet}.
\]

Here \(S_{\tau}\) denotes source values admitted by their own schema, \(O_{\tau}\) source-occurrence coordinates, and the final arrow is the *extensional join* of cells with the same canonical triple. This equation specifies a computational contract; it does **not** prove that a conventional JSON schema, software class or graph is a primitive mathematical object of Obsmetrologia. The source ordering is Relationality → Obsmetrologia Mark/Utterance/Articulating → derived predication → technological SignCell carrier, never imported JSON/RDF/category theory → invented foundational Mark axioms.

A cell's **identity** is the canonical content address of its ordered **(subject, predicate, object)** triple. Its **information** additionally carries independent told-for and told-against witness sets and a possible FOUR reading. Joining two witnessed presentations of the *same triple* preserves both evidence sides and may yield a contested reading. Distinct triples do not become identical merely because their human descriptions are synonyms. A repeated *source act* can refer to the same extensional cell without becoming the same occurrence. A source pointer is a coordinate into the admitted input; it is neither a cell NI address nor authority to trust the source.

The operation refuses unadmitted schemas, dangling references, reserved-predicate impersonation, unaddressable carriers and anything that would require fabricating absent evidence. It may preserve a claim as **unobserved**: emitted cell ≠ evidence for that claim ≠ verified role ≠ running host ≠ received customer outcome.

## Real source-owned realization

The existing [Systems interpretation function](https://github.com/red-cup-engineering/computer-system-engine/blob/feature/architecture-signcell-projection-20261019/src/architecture-cellularization.mjs) takes a typed software architecture source with component role assertions and oriented relations. The [source schema](https://github.com/red-cup-engineering/computer-system-engine/blob/feature/architecture-signcell-projection-20261019/specifications/architecture-cellularization.schema.json) delegates software role admission to its [single authoritative role schema](https://github.com/red-cup-engineering/computer-system-engine/blob/feature/architecture-signcell-projection-20261019/specifications/software-role.schema.json). The consumer uses the *existing* SignCell cell, evidence join, content addressing and type admission. It returns an extensional cell set **and** source-ordered occurrences with RFC 6901 JSON Pointers. It does not put architecture-specific role semantics in Metrologic or duplicate canonical hashing in Systems.

**Valid case A:** A system is *asserted* to have server and client roles from separately named sources. Both role cells exist as distinct triples, without asserting they are disjoint. A relation asserting that it consumes a provider remains oriented; its converse is not invented.

**Valid case B:** Two role assertions point to the same triple, one with for-evidence and one with against-evidence. The projected cell set contains **one** canonical identity with *both* witnesses. The occurrence list retains two separately addressed original assertions.

**Refusal:** A relation points to an undeclared component, a role is not in the admitted role schema, or a general relation tries to counterfeit the reserved role predicate. These do not become silent partial results or truth-claims.

The [live MCP operations manifold consumer](https://github.com/red-cup-engineering/computer-system-engine/blob/feature/architecture-signcell-projection-20261019/src/rpc/manifold.mjs) can cellularize discovered operation declarations and schema-composition *candidates* without pretending that matched schema IDs are authorized executions. SignGraph may graph the resulting cells, SignChain may handle justified ordered evidence, and SignCraft may realize qualified compositions: a cell does not cause the other stages automatically.

## Not synonyms

Cellularization is **not** semantic interpretation proved correct; **not** syntactic serialization alone; **not** inventing a cellular-automaton lattice; **not** biological cell division; **not** a server installation. A graph of already cellularized assertions is a SignGraph *projection*. A chain is a SignChain composition with its own order and proof obligations. Semiocodification, where warranted, means a specified semiotic operation becoming executable code; it is not a fancy word for a graph or data conversion. Preserve these distinctions when converting engines, applications, offices, Worlds or services.
