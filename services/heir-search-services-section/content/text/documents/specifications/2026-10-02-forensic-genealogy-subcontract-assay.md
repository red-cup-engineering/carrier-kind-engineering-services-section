# Forensic Genealogy Research-Only Subcontract — bounded assay

Status: assay only, not a standing new offer
Date: 2026-10-02

## Current buyer shape

A current marketplace buyer seeks research on a deceased property owner: spouse/marital history, children/descendants, parents/siblings when relevant, descendants of deceased siblings, a family tree as of date of death, source support for each relationship, confirmed-vs-probable distinctions, likely living family members, and relevant probate/vital records. The buyer explicitly does not ask the researcher for a legal opinion on ownership or inheritance percentages.

This shape is close to existing Heir Search report production and is therefore a useful stress test. A listing is not a 561 sale.

## Assay deliverable

Given buyer-supplied decedent/property identifiers, produce a research-only packet with:

- case identifier and buyer-supplied starting facts;
- relationship graph/tree;
- one evidence row per asserted relationship;
- documented / probable / disputed / unknown / excluded state;
- source/citation and observation date;
- negative searches where material;
- probate/vital/obituary/deed/title-related records located;
- unresolved identity collisions or missing generations;
- explicit no-legal-conclusion statement;
- contact-data enrichment excluded unless separately authorized and scoped.

## Acceptance

A buyer/assayer can trace every material relationship to source evidence, distinguish documented from probable/unknown links, identify unresolved branches, and use the packet as research input without mistaking it for a title opinion, inheritance determination, or legal advice.

## Conversion gate

Before proposal/contact, separately establish seller/platform authority, price, permitted data handling, exact buyer scope, and whether the buyer accepts research-only delivery. Do not add cold calling, legal conclusions, or title-curative representation merely to match adjacent demand.
