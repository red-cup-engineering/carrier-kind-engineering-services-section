# Heir Search Joint-Venture Caller Agreement

**Between:** {{contractingParty}} ("the Group") and ______________________ ("the Caller").

1. **The venture.** The Group and the Caller jointly pursue selling heir search reports and
   heirs'-property title-clearing support to probate and estate attorneys, title companies,
   heirs'-property nonprofits and USDA Section 2501 grantees, county probate and public
   administrators, land trusts, and tax-forfeit/partition buyers. Each order the Caller
   originates is a joint venture between them for that order.
2. **Origination.** An order is the Caller's when the Caller first reaches the buyer who signs
   the engagement letter, and logs the contact in the shared lead list before the Group's intake
   call.
3. **Roles.** The Caller finds and qualifies buyers and sets up intake calls. The Group prices
   the order, signs the engagement letter, runs or assigns the research, and delivers the report.
4. **Split.** For each order the Caller originates, the Caller receives ______% of the order's
   price, paid on delivery of the report or within five business days after the Group receives
   payment.
5. **Referrals.** When a buyer the Caller brought onto the lead list places an order the Caller
   did not originate, the Caller receives ______% of that order's price.
6. **Expenses.** Each party pays its own phone, time and tools. The Group pays for paid document
   retrieval in paper-only counties and any subscription research access it uses.
7. **Independence.** The Caller works their own hours with their own means, is not an employee,
   and is responsible for their own taxes. The Group issues a 1099 where required.
8. **Conduct.** The Caller identifies themselves truthfully, does not promise a specific finding
   or timeline the research has not yet supported, honors every request not to be contacted
   again, and does not sign an engagement letter for the Group.
9. **Leads.** The buyer lead lists (funders, nonprofits, and any attorney/title-company/probate
   office contacts the Caller develops) are shared venture property. Either party may end this
   agreement by written notice. Orders under signed engagement letter at that time are still
   split under section 4.
