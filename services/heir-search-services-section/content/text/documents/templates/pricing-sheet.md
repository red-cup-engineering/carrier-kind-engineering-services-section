# Heir Search Pricing

## Standard pricing

| Item | Price |
|---|---|
| Retainer (opens county deed-index and probate-docket access) | $150 |
| Per year of research | $60 |
| Per heir located | $175 |
| Rush (same-week turnaround) | +$200 |

**Example:** a two-generation, single-county search locating 3 heirs runs $150 + (2 x $60) +
(3 x $175) = **$795**.

## Contingency pricing

For estates where an upfront fee is the wrong fit, we take **20-30% of the estate value**
recovered instead, matching the incumbents who offer this model. We agree the percentage before
work starts, in writing.

## What is included

- The heir tree, with each entry's evidentiary reading (documented, disputed, excluded, or
  presumed) and the sources checked.
- The chain-of-title summary supporting the reconstruction.
- A written report, delivered as a paralegal-tier input to your own attorney's review and sign-off
  — never a title opinion.

## What can add cost, quoted before it is billed

- **Paper-only county records.** Where a county's deed index, probate docket or vital records are
  paper-only and unindexed, in-person or ordered document retrieval is a real per-county cost. We
  name it and get your approval before it is billed.
- **Additional counties or generations** discovered mid-research beyond the ordered scope.

## How this compares

Named incumbents in this market (HeirSearch, Heir Pros, Morse Genealogical) charge roughly the
same $60/year plus $175/heir; Discovering Connections and DavisDNA work on 20-30% contingency. We
price at parity, not a discount race, because the multi-county cross-referencing this work
requires is the cost, not the margin.
