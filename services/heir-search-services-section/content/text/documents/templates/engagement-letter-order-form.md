## Scope

We will reconstruct, from county deed indexes, probate dockets, obituary indexes, vital-records
indexes and public genealogy sources, an heir tree and a chain-of-title summary sufficient to
support the Client's own title opinion or partition filing. Scope ordered: {{scope}}.

**This report is a paralegal-tier input.** It is not a title opinion and does not substitute for
the Client's own attorney's review and sign-off. Every heir tree entry we cannot confirm from a
second independent source is marked presumed or disputed rather than asserted as fact.

## Timeline

We turn most single-county, single-generation orders in 5-10 business days; multi-county or
multi-generation orders in 2-4 weeks, and we tell the Client as soon as a county's records prove
to be paper-only and require ordered document retrieval, since that adds days and a per-county
retrieval cost the Client approves before it is billed.
