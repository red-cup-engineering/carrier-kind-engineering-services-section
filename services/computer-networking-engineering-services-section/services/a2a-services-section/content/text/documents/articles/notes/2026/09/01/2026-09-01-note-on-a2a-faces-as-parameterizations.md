## The parameterization law

Owner law (org-layering, ): exchange protocols are one package over node functions, in metroplat/protocol custody, never inside science or function packages. A service's A2A face is a parameterization — an agent card plus skill-to-function bindings as data — served through a2a-contracted-agent-service.

## Measured violation

Dozens of services hand-roll their A2A serving boilerplate (the serve-clearing / clearing-executor shape appears in up to 60 packages by jsnode's duplication graph) while @red-cup-engineering/a2a-contracted-agent-service (published, stable) owns the capability.

## Ruling

Per-service serve-*.mjs A2A boilerplate is deletion inventory, migrated as bounded lots (the climate market trio first; the remainder from the confirmed inventory that lot records). A capability the serving owner lacks merges upstream into it additively; it is never re-rolled locally in a consumer.
