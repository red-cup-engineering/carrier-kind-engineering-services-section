`services/business-research-system-services-section/` assays market gaps: it
reads a `MarketGapStudy` (a coded supply of comparable works and demands
carried by graded evidence for and against) with metrologic's formal context
and FOUR, and writes the `MarketGapReadings` a work's evidence packet cites
(`assay-market-gaps`). The method is the business-science note on finding a
market gap; the studies live in the science section of their topic.
