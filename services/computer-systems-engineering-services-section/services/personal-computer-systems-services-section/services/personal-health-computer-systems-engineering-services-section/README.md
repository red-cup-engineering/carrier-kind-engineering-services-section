# Personal health computer systems engineering services section

Owns composed personal computer systems whose primary purpose is helping one
person conduct, record, and review their own health practices. It narrows
personal computer systems by health purpose while leaving scientific claims
with the relevant health-science sections and leaving generic interfaces,
storage, networking, and sensing capabilities with their respective
engineering sections.

## Services

- `tai-chi-computer-system-services-section` — composes shared Tai Chi science,
  practitioner-owned state, movement observations, and human-facing practice
  applications.
- `nutrition-practice-system-services-section` — composes shared nutrition
  science, practitioner-owned nutrition state, and nutrition-practice
  applications.
- `pacing-intelligence-services-section` — pure biometric math for
  infection-associated chronic illness pacing (rolling baselines, morning
  deviation, the Workwell heart-rate heuristic, evidence-labeled PEM risk
  flags); the compute layer a future practitioner-facing app and a paid
  agent API both call.
