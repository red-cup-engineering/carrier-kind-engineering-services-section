# Computer Proofing System Services Section

This section owns infrastructure for proof assistants. It currently provides
the Lean integration and the tools that inspect, census, and audit Lean proof
artifacts. Mathematical applications and mathematical research live with their
mathematical subjects and computer-math applications.

## Services

- `services/lean-system-services-section/` provides Lean workspace, source,
  Blueprint, Verso, and metaprogramming tooling.
- `apps/obsmetrologia-theorem-audit/` reads and audits the
  scientific Obsmetrologia Lean library, producing theorem and axiom census
  results without owning the mathematical corpus.

The scientific formalizations are held by their subjects: Obsmetrologia-related
formalizations under Math's Obsmetrological Services Section, and the
SemioticUniverse Lean and spatiotemporal projects under Relationality.
