VaultMaze is the composable knowledge-navigation and learning application that
must meet and beat Microsoft MindMaze. It forms admitted CAIRN resources into
rooms, passages, review experiences, and user-configured routes without
becoming their content repository. The engineering realization is the
[VaultMaze application](../../../../README.md).
