## Who this book is for {.unnumbered}

You have agreed to find sellers or buyers for the 561 Group, probably on
commission, probably working your own hours with your own phone. You may never
have worked in real estate. This book assumes nothing about what you know
except that you can hold a conversation and keep notes.

By the end of it you will know what the business buys and sells, who pays for
what, what to say on a call and why, which rules you work under and where they
come from, how to record your work so that it pays you, and how to tell from
your own numbers whether you are getting better.

## How this book is organized {.unnumbered}

The book follows a deal from the first search to the money at closing.

- **Chapter 1** says what the business does and why each party says yes.
- **Chapter 2** describes the kinds of property we contract and who buys
  each.
- **Chapter 3** covers buyers first: the buy-box call, which decides every
  other call you make.
- **Chapter 4** teaches you to read a lead before you dial.
- **Chapters 5 and 6** are the seller call and its objections.
- **Chapter 7** covers letters, for owners you cannot reach by phone and for
  land worked by mail.
- **Chapters 8 and 9** follow a yes to a signed contract, then the contract to
  an assignment and a closing.
- **Chapter 10** is the rules: licensing, disclosure, calling hours and how
  we treat people.
- **Chapter 11** shows you how to do all of this in the operations world, the 561
  Group's operations world, where your office gives you your commands.
- **Chapter 12** is your day and your week, as a plan of actions.
- **Chapter 13** is the numbers: what a deal pays, what calling yields, and
  how to read your own funnel.
- **Chapter 14** covers the second lines of work: county auctions,
  unclaimed surplus, and estates.
- A glossary and an appendix of scripts, outcome codes and stage reasons
  close the book.

The examples are real parcels from the county records, pulled on
2026-09-26, with the owners' names left out. The scripts are the ones we use.

## How to use it {.unnumbered}

Read chapters 1 through 6 before your first call. Keep the appendix open
while you dial. Read chapter 10 twice.

When something in this book does not match what happens on your calls, the
book is wrong. Tell the acquisitions lead, and the next edition will say what
actually works. A lost deal is how the procedure gets better.
