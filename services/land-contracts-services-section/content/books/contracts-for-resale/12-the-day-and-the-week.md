# Your day and your week

## Work to actions, not to goals

Sales goals make people cut corners. The Group plans your work as **actions**:
the specific things you do each day, in order, with the tools in chapter 11. If
you do the actions, the numbers follow, and when they do not, the actions are
what we change, together. A lost deal is information about the procedure, not a
mark against you.

## The day

1. **Buyers first (30 minutes).** Five to ten buyer calls a day until your
   market has twenty recorded buy boxes, then one or two a day to keep them
   current.
2. **Callbacks due (as long as they take).** `land-call-queue` puts them first.
   A promised callback is the most valuable call on the list.
3. **Seller calls in two blocks.** 9 to 11:30 a.m. and 4 to 7 p.m. in the
   owner's time zone, when people answer. Aim for 60 to 100 dials a day if you
   are part-time, 100 to 150 if full-time.
4. **Log every dial as you make it.** Not at the end of the day.
5. **Hand off the same day.** Every `interested` and `offer call set` is on
   the pipeline for the acquisitions lead the moment you log it; add a note
   with anything they need.
6. **Letters (once a week).** Draft and send the week's neutral letters and
   blind offers, and log them as `letter sent`.

## The week

Once a week, read your numbers (`land-activity since=<last week>`) with the
acquisitions lead:

- dials, letters, conversations, and interested owners, by day;
- the funnel: how many leads reached each stage, and the rate from each stage
  to the next;
- the reasons deals died, by stage.

Read the enterprise too (`enterprise enterprise=land-contracts`): its statement shows how many
owners the business has reached and served, what it captured, and each measure against its goal.

Then decide one thing to change. If owners say `not interested` far more than
usual, change the list (a different market, kind or score). If owners are
interested but deals die at `asking above ceiling`, call the buyers about their
prices. If conversations are low, check the numbers you are dialing. Write the
change down in the notes of the week's review, so the next edition of this book
can include what worked.

## Your first two weeks

- **Day 1:** read chapters 1 to 6 and 10. Get your account and seat. Type
  `powers` and `land-markets`.
- **Days 2 and 3:** buyer calls only, until you have recorded five buy boxes.
- **Days 4 to 10:** seller calls on matched leads, with a daily review of your
  log against the scripts.
- **Days 11 to 14:** your first mailing, and your first weekly review.
