# Appendix: the call sheet {.unnumbered}

## Seller call, on one page {.unnumbered}

1. "Hi, is this [first name]? This is [your name] with the 561 Group. I'm
   calling about your property in [place], the [lot / land / house] at [where].
   Do you still own that?"
2. "We buy property there directly from owners. Would you consider an offer?
   We close at a local title company, no agent, no commission."
3. How did you come to own it? Have you seen it lately? Anything I should know?
   Anyone else on title? "If we could close in 30 to 60 days, what number would
   make this worth doing?"
4. Repeat their number back. "I'll pass this to our offer lead. [Day] or
   [day]?"
5. Log it.

## Outcome codes {.unnumbered}

| outcome | tells | when |
|---|---|---|
| `no answer` | nothing | nobody picked up |
| `voicemail` | nothing | you left the voicemail script |
| `wrong number` | nothing | not this owner's number |
| `not owner` | nothing | the person no longer owns it |
| `letter sent` | nothing | you mailed a letter |
| `callback` | for | they asked you to call back; set `callbackAt` |
| `interested` | for | they named a price or asked for an offer |
| `offer call set` | for | you set a time for the offer call |
| `not interested` | against | they declined |
| `remove` | against | they asked not to be called; never call again |

## Why a deal died, by stage {.unnumbered}

| stage | reasons |
|---|---|
| lead | wrong number · not owner · owner deceased, no heir reached · never reached |
| contacted | not interested · remove · keeping it · listed with an agent |
| interested | asking above ceiling · no matched buyer · title problem named by owner · owner unresponsive |
| offered | offer declined · owner unresponsive · another buyer |
| contracted | title failed · due diligence found a defect · seller backed out |
| marketed | no buyer at the assignment price · contract expired |
| assigned | assignee failed to close · title failed |

## Where the rules come from {.unnumbered}

- Minnesota Statutes 82.55 (broker definition) and 82.56 (exemptions).
- Minnesota Statutes 282.005 (forfeited land: auction, surplus claims,
  repurchase), as rewritten in 2024.
- Minnesota Statutes 325E.27 (automatic dialing-announcing devices).
- Minnesota Statutes 513.52 to 513.60 (residential seller disclosure).
- 2023 Wisconsin Act 208 (real property wholesalers).
- The Group's notes on Minnesota law for assigning property contracts, on the
  Minnesota property-resale market, and on brokerage as a market mechanism.
