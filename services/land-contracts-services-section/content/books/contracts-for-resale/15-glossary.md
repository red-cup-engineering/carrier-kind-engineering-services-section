# Glossary {.unnumbered}

**Absentee owner.** An owner who does not live on or near the property, seen in
the county's records as a mailing address elsewhere.

**Assignment.** Transferring our rights under a purchase agreement to another
buyer before closing. The document is the *assignment of purchase agreement*.

**Assignment fee.** What the new buyer pays the Group for the assignment: the
assignment price less the contract price. Paid at closing.

**Blind offer.** A letter offering a stated price to an owner we have not
spoken with.

**Buy box.** A buyer's standing description of what they buy: markets, kinds,
areas, size, top price, speed, and how to send them a deal.

**Closing.** The day the title company takes the money, pays everyone, and
records the deed.

**Contract assignment.** The business: putting property under an assignable
purchase agreement and assigning it for a fee. The trade calls it wholesaling.

**Deal sheet.** The page that offers our contract, not the land, to matched
buyers.

**Due diligence.** The window after signing in which the buyer may inspect the
property and walk away.

**Earnest money.** The deposit the buyer places with the title company when the
agreement is signed, credited at closing.

**Equitable interest.** The buyer's right, under a signed purchase agreement, to
buy the property on its terms. It is what we assign.

**Forfeiture.** The state taking title to property whose taxes went unpaid
through the redemption period. Minnesota then auctions it.

**Funnel.** How many deals reached each stage, and the share that went on.

**Heir search.** Reconstructing a family's chain of title and heirs so an
estate's property can be sold or cleared; the Group's sister business.

**Infill lot.** Vacant residential land in a city, big enough to build on.

**Kind.** The kind of property a parcel is: infill lot, underbuilt site,
seasonal, rural acreage, house, or forfeited.

**Market.** A place the business works, read from one county's parcel records:
Duluth, Hermantown, Proctor, the Iron Range cities, St. Louis County's towns,
Lake, Itasca, Crow Wing, Cass and Aitkin counties, Minneapolis, Saint Paul.

**Operations world.** The Group's operations world at ops.561.group, where your office
gives you your commands.

**Neutral letter.** A letter asking whether an owner would sell, with no price.

**Offer ceiling.** The most the Group will offer: the matched buyer's price
less the fee, never above the county-estimate ceiling for the kind.

**Personal representative.** The person a court appoints to handle a dead
person's estate, who signs for it.

**Purchase agreement.** The contract in which the owner agrees to sell and the
Group, "or assigns", agrees to buy.

**Real estate broker.** In Minnesota, anyone who for another and for a fee buys,
sells or negotiates real estate, and anyone presumed in the business after five
transactions a year without a licensee representing them.

**Real property wholesaler.** Wisconsin's name for a buyer who intends to
assign; they must give written notice.

**Stage.** Where a deal is: lead, contacted, interested, offered, contracted,
marketed, assigned, closed, or dead.

**Surplus.** What a forfeited property's auction brings above the taxes and
costs, which the former owner may claim.

**Title company.** The neutral company that searches title, holds deposits and
closes the sale.

**Underbuilt site.** Commercial or industrial land whose buildings are worth
little against the land.
