# Before you sign

**Date:** {{date}}

**To:** {{seller}}

**About:** parcel {{parcel}}, {{location}}, {{place}}

Thank you for talking with us about your property. Before you sign the
purchase agreement, here is what it means, in plain words.

1. **Who is buying.** The buyer named in the agreement is {{buyerSigner}},
   "or assigns". That means we may transfer our right to buy your property to
   another buyer, usually a builder or investor, before closing.
2. **We may be paid for that transfer.** If we assign the agreement, the new
   buyer pays us an assignment fee. That fee comes from the new buyer, not
   from you. Your price does not change: you receive ${{price}} at closing,
   less any back taxes and your share of costs the agreement names.
3. **Nothing else changes for you.** Whoever closes, you close with the same
   terms, on or before the closing date, at {{closingAgent}}, a title company
   that holds the earnest money and handles the paperwork.
4. **We are not your agent.** We are a buyer. We do not represent you, and you
   may have a lawyer or a real estate agent read the agreement before you sign.
   You may also list your property on the open market instead; selling to us
   trades some price for a fast, certain sale with no commission and no
   showings.
5. **You can ask us anything.** Call {{callerName}} at {{callerPhone}} or write
   to us at the address below.

If anything in the agreement is not what we told you on the phone, do not
sign it; call us and we will fix it.

**561 Group** — {{groupAddress}}

I have read this letter.
