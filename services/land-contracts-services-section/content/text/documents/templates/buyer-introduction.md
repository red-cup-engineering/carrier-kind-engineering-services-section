# Parcels that fit what you build, before anyone else sees them

**For:** {{buyer}}

Thank you for telling us what you buy. Here is what we do with it.

- **We search every parcel in the county for you.** We read the county's
  parcel records for the areas you named ({{areas}}), in the sizes and kinds
  you build on, and call only the owners of parcels that fit.
- **We put the parcel under contract at a price that works for you.** Your top
  price ({{maxPrice}}) sets our ceiling, so every contract we send you already
  leaves you room.
- **You get the contract, not a listing.** We assign you our signed purchase
  agreement for a fee. You close at a title company with the seller; the
  deposit and the fee go through the title company.
- **We don't send you what doesn't fit.** If your areas, sizes or prices
  change, tell us and we update your buy box the same day.

For Duluth builders: the city's Housing Trust Fund lends gap and short-term
construction financing for infill, rehabilitation and conversion housing,
affordable and market-rate, with pre-applications taken on a rolling basis.

Deals will come to you by {{sendVia}}.
