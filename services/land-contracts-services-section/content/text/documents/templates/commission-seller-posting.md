# Commission callers wanted: property in {{place}}

The 561 Group buys property directly from owners who would rather have a
quick, certain sale, and passes its contracts to builders and investors for a
fee. We are looking for people in or around {{place}} to find the owners and
the buyers.

## The work

- Call builders and investors to learn what they buy.
- Call owners of vacant lots, land and rental houses that fit what those buyers
  want, and find out whether they would sell and at what price.
- Send letters to owners who are best reached by mail.
- Log every call and letter, and hand interested owners to our offer lead the
  same day.

You do not make offers, sign anything, or handle money. We do.

## The pay

You are a partner in every deal you bring in, under a written joint-venture
agreement, and you receive {{callerShare}}% of the Group's fee on each one,
paid at closing. Fees run from about $2,000 on a single city lot to $10,000
and more on larger lots, land and houses. Nothing is paid until a deal closes,
and there is no cap.

What that comes to depends on your hours and your market. On the trade's
published averages, a caller dialing about 100 numbers a day signs one or two
contracts a month. Your own numbers are in front of you every day, and we
review them with you each week.

## What we provide

- The lists: every parcel in the county's records that fits a buyer, scored,
  with the reasons it scored and what we would offer.
- The training: *Contracts for Resale*, our field manual, and a first two weeks
  planned with you.
- The tools: an account in our operations world, where your office gives you
  your call queue, lead details, logging and letters.
- The paperwork, the offers, the title company and the closings.

## What you need

- A phone, a computer, and a few hours a day you can set aside.
- Comfort talking with strangers, and the habit of writing down what they say.
- Honesty. We say only what is true, we take people off our list when they
  ask, and we never pressure anyone who cannot tell what they are agreeing to.

No real estate license is needed. If you hold one, tell us: licensees work
under different rules and can do more of the deal.
