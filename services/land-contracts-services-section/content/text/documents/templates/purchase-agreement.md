# Purchase Agreement

**Date:** {{date}}

**Seller:** {{seller}} (all persons on title)

**Buyer:** {{buyerSigner}}, or assigns

**Property:** the real property in {{place}}, {{county}} County, Minnesota,
known as parcel number {{parcel}} ({{location}}), legally described as
{{legal}} (the county's legal description controls), with any buildings on it.

1. **Price.** ${{price}}, paid in cash or certified funds at closing.
   Seller's delinquent property taxes, penalties and interest are paid at
   closing from Seller's proceeds.
2. **Earnest money.** Buyer deposits ${{earnestMoney}} with the closing agent named
   below within three business days after both parties sign. It is credited
   to the price at closing, and returned to Buyer if this agreement ends under
   section 5 or 6.
3. **Closing.** On or before {{closingDate}} (or within ten days after title is
   cleared, if later), at {{closingAgent}} (the closing agent), a
   Minnesota title company. Seller conveys by warranty deed (or by personal
   representative's or trustee's deed if selling as such), free of liens
   except easements and restrictions of record that do not prevent building
   a single-family home or the property's present use.
4. **Costs.** Buyer pays the title search, title insurance, and closing fee.
   Seller pays state deed tax and any costs of clearing Seller's title
   defects. Current-year taxes are prorated to the closing date.
5. **Due diligence.** For {{dueDiligenceDays}} days after signing, Buyer may inspect the
   property and review title, survey, zoning, utilities, and access. Buyer may
   end this agreement by written notice within that period, for any reason,
   and receive the earnest money back.
6. **Title.** If title cannot be made marketable before closing, either party
   may end this agreement and the earnest money is returned to Buyer.
7. **Assignment.** Buyer may assign this agreement, in whole or in part,
   without Seller's consent. Seller will close with the assignee on the same
   terms. Seller understands Buyer may assign for a fee paid by the assignee,
   and has received Buyer's written disclosure letter saying so.
8. **Access.** Buyer and its assignees, builders, surveyors and inspectors
   may enter the property before closing to inspect it.
9. **As-is.** Seller sells the property as-is. Seller states that it knows of
   no pending assessments, violations, or disputes with neighbors except:
   ______________________________.
10. **Residential disclosure.** If the property includes a dwelling, Seller
    has delivered, before signing, the written disclosure Minn. Stat. 513.55
    requires of a seller of residential real property (or the parties have
    waived it as 513.60 allows).
11. **Well and septic disclosure (Minn. Stat. 103I.235).** Seller certifies
    that Seller [ ] does not know of any wells on the property / [ ] knows of
    wells (list): ______________. Seller [ ] does / [ ] does not know of a
    subsurface sewage treatment system on the property.
12. **Default.** If Buyer defaults, Seller's only remedy is to keep the
    earnest money under Minn. Stat. 559.21. If Seller defaults, Buyer may
    seek specific performance or the return of the earnest money.
13. **Entire agreement.** This is the whole agreement. Electronic signatures
    and copies count as originals.

**Seller:** ______________________ Date ________

**Seller:** ______________________ Date ________
