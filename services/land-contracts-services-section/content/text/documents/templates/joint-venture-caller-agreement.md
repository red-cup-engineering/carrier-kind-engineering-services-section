# Joint-Venture Caller Agreement

**Between:** {{buyerSigner}} ("the Group") and {{caller}}
("the Caller").

1. **The venture.** The Group and the Caller jointly pursue buying land and
   houses in the markets the Group works, putting them under purchase
   agreement, and assigning or reselling them. Each deal the Caller originates is a joint
   venture between them for that deal.
2. **Origination.** A deal is the Caller's when the Caller first reaches the
   owner who later signs, and logs the contact in the shared lead sheet
   before the Group's offer call. The shared lead sheet is the Group's
   record of logged contacts (the `land-contact-log` verb in the operations world).
3. **Roles.** The Caller finds and qualifies owners and builders, and sets
   offer calls. The Group makes offers, signs agreements, puts up earnest
   money, handles title and closing, and markets contracts to builders.
4. **Split.** For each deal the Caller originates, the Caller receives
   {{callerShare}}% of the net fee. The net fee is the assignment fee, or on a
   resale, the resale price minus the purchase price, closing costs and
   holding costs. It is paid at closing or within five business days after
   the Group receives the funds.
5. **Builder introductions.** When a builder the Caller brought onto the
   buyer list takes an assignment on a deal the Caller did not originate,
   the Caller receives ______% of that net fee.
6. **Expenses.** Each party pays its own phone, time and tools. The Group
   pays earnest money, skip tracing it orders, title and closing costs.
7. **Independence.** The Caller works their own hours with their own means,
   is not an employee, and is responsible for their own taxes. The Group
   issues a 1099 where required.
8. **Conduct.** The Caller identifies themselves truthfully, dials by hand,
   calls between 8am and 9pm in the owner's time zone, honors every request
   not to be called, and does not quote a firm price or sign for the Group.
9. **Leads.** The lead lists and the builder list are shared venture
   property. Either party may end this agreement by written notice. Deals
   under signed purchase agreement at that time are still split under
   section 4.
