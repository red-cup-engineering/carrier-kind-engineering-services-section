VICTOR means **Voxelized Information Catalog Topos Orienting Relations**. It is
the website framework that forms domain applications and authored resources
into complete, conformant, buildable, and served websites. TUFTE is its
user-form topology engine. The engineering realization is the
[VICTOR application](../../../../README.md).
